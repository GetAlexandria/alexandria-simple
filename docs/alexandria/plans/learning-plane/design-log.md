# Learning-plane build — design log (in progress)

Working scratchpad for inventing the **Learning plane of Alexandria's product library**,
mirroring the strategy-plane rebuild (`docs/alexandria/plans/strategy-plane-rebuild/design-log.md`).
Not a deliverable; a running SoT so the thread survives context summarization. This build is
also **process-captured**: every phase is logged so the whole arc can be reverse-derived into
a play other directors can run (see "Play capture" at the bottom).

## The task (director, 2026-07-07)

Design and implement the Learning plane: organize the plane, define what goes into cards,
plan an elicitation session, and capture the process into a play. Director's framing:

- **Past learning** = the research that set the table for product decisions: system-builder
  video games (SimCity/Civilization, resource management) as the OS paradigm; human cognition;
  how factories / airplane cockpits / air-traffic-control centers visualize high-volume,
  high-tempo work; how NASA and the navy orchestrate complex action across many stakeholders.
  This informed strategy cards — they must connect.
- **Future learning** = "strategies are questions in disguise — they're really hypotheses."
  The job is to name what we must learn/prove to *earn the next release or phase*: at minimum
  the buckets of information needed at the product level; at maximum actual build plans with
  metric tracking that confirm or deny the hypothesis behind the strategy.
- **Present learning** = information being generated by in-flight initiatives answering those
  questions. Every in-flight learning needs a **called stopping point** — time, reps, money
  spent; limits must exist.
- Most experiments land **in the product** → dense links to the product plane.
- The WHEN part of the card schema is critical. An actual product roadmap / release plan is
  missing from both the company and Alexandria; the Learning plane's future register is where
  it becomes real.

## Proposed shape (for director ruling)

### Two first-class card types — the evidence register and the instrument register

- **Research** — evidence *held*. Settled learning, past by nature: a distilled lesson from a
  research corpus, a called experiment's result, or a market signal. Vitals: `confidence`
  (strength of the evidence itself) · `origin` kind (desk-research | run-result | signal).
  A Research card *informs* Bets (↑) and product cards (across). No horizon — settled
  evidence simply exists now.
- **Experiment** — evidence *being earned*. The bet's question made operational: instrument,
  metric, where it lands in the product, and the **stopping rule**. Stateful — the only
  lifecycle-bearing thing in the plane. Vitals:
  - `state: planned | running | called` (experiment lifecycle, ⟂ card `status`)
  - `horizon: future` (planned, not yet started → `## WHEN` section required by existing
    machinery) or absent/`now` (running or called)
  - `stop:` list of `{tag, note}` — tags `time` / `reps` / `spend` / `signal`; the called
    stopping point, mirror-shape of Bet `risks`
  - `milestone:` free string — the release/phase this experiment helps earn (the roadmap key)
  - `verdict:` on called experiments — `confirms | denies | mixed` + prose in HOW
  - A **maturity gradient**, not two types: minimum viable Experiment = the named question +
    the Bet it tests (the director's "bucket of information we need"); maximum = full protocol
    with metric tracking. Same card, filled deeper.
- Lifecycle: **Experiment (planned → running → called) `produces` Research**; Research updates
  the tested Bet's `confidence` and/or retires named `risks`. That update loop is the plane's
  whole job — the living business plan becomes self-correcting.
- Taxonomy impact: Research already exists (bucket 3). **Experiment is one new first-class
  bucket (11 → 12)** — additive, no retirement (unlike Rationale→Bet/Principle).

### Contexts (shelves) — REVISED 2026-07-07 (director write-aloud; supersedes programs-of-inquiry)

The index view is the front door, and its job is to tell the story of how the plane works.
The story of learning is the loop — learning drives strategy, strategy drives product,
product generates learning — and the three activities that power it. So the shelves are the
activities themselves:

- `research` — evidence held (the Research type lives here)
- `experiments` — bounded tests (the Experiment type)
- `measurement` — standing measures (the Measure type)
- `_index/Concept - Learning.md` — the keystone that narrates the loop

Each shelf is deliberately type-pure, which is already the shipped pattern in the strategy
plane (the principles shelf is all Principle cards). Activity-noun shelves hold thing-noun
types: research/Research, experiments/Experiment, measurement/Measure.

Inside a shelf, there is no sub-context level in the hierarchy, so interior order is carried
by the three tools that already exist: altitude, containment links, and the lead card's
story. The primary organizing head inside every shelf is **strategy**: members group under
the bet cluster they serve, because the ruled loop direction makes learning the upward
channel and the Bet is what gets updated. Product ties are cross-links (the constellation
renders them). Ownership still slices by bet — it is a band across the three shelves plus a
view, not a folder.

The research corpora become pillar-altitude lead cards *inside* the research shelf, each
containing and relegating its member lessons — exactly how the knowledge-organization context
organizes its own interior. Corpora never appear at index level (the director's test: "NASA
research at the pillar level is way off base"). (Named here as four working titles —
system-builders, cognition, high-tempo operations, NASA/navy coordination — before the
elicitation walk merged them to three and renamed one; see "Past — three corpora, not four"
below for the final list. Corpus lead altitude is also later revised from pillar to aggregate,
per D4.)

Time stays in fields, never shelves — a card must not change folders because a date passed.

**Rejected alternative (the original proposal, kept for the walk play's lesson):** shelves
as content programs — four corpora shelves plus three proving-ground shelves mirroring the
strategy bet clusters. Rejected because it imports the strategy plane's content-cluster
pattern into a plane whose index story is machinery-first: it puts instances (NASA) where
the story wants activities, and it spends the shelf axis on ownership that interior
structure and views can carry.

**Director observation logged for a later pass (not today's job):** the existing exemplars
have altitude inconsistencies of their own. The strategy index descends to the three
specific bets but keeps principles a level up (the exemplar is "kind of broken" there); the
product index mixes true pillars (playbook, library, ledger, triggers, viewer) with contexts
that feel one level down (canvas, knowledge-organization). Altitude organization and tagging
need tuning across all planes.

**Altitude-tuning pass — concrete findings (2026-07-07, after the director raised the
pillar confusion):** live distribution across 114 carded altitudes: aggregate 27 ·
component 24 · capability 18 · value 15 · pillar 14 · context 14 · keystone 2. The core
problem: **"pillar" does double duty** — the old absolute sense (THE primitives: and indeed
Ledger, Playbook, Library, Viewer all carry it) and the shipped relative sense (headline
card *of its own area*: Knowledge Organization, The Approach, Running Plays, Updating the
Library, Role - Director). Outliers found: deprecated `Concept - AI Colleague` still holds
pillar; `Surface - AX CLI` at pillar is arguably over-promoted; the three corporate Bets
carry pillar though the taxonomy ruling said keystone (in practice keystone = the two
`_index` overview cards only — ruling vs shipped drift). Data-model looseness: `altitude`
is a free string (nothing validates vocabulary); the viewer's lead-selection rank table
knows only 5 of 7 values (keystone and context unranked). The pass's load-bearing decision:
**rule pillar's grammar — relative (any area's headline; what the machinery assumes) vs
absolute (reserved for the primitives).** Learning-plane D4 follows the relative sense
pending that ruling; fallback if the director prefers not to deepen relative usage: only
the `_index` keystone elevated, shelf leadership via containment links alone.

### Altitudes

Keystone = the `_index` plane card. Experiments = `aggregate` (lifecycle-bearing — the

### WHEN — REVISED 2026-07-07 (director vote: WHEN is the card's biography)

Director's ruling-in-progress on time, superseding the "one temporal footing per card"
model below. Research is archaeological: a topic has layers already laid down (some wrong,
even harmful), a live dig, and digs we'd like to fund. Experiments the same: planned, then
mid-flight, then called — and sometimes later invalidated because the world changed ("it
was the future, then the past, then fake news"). One value can't say all that; a biography
can.

- **WHEN = the card's biography**: one narration in three registers — what happened (the
  layers), what stands now, what's intended next. Every learning-plane card carries WHEN,
  not just `horizon: future` cards. Types still set where a card *primarily* lives
  (Research leans past, Experiments bounded, Measures flow), but all three tenses belong to
  every card.
- `horizon` survives as the coarse machine-readable flag driving tooling; the section
  carries the truth. Fields-not-shelves restated stronger: **cards don't move through time;
  time accumulates in cards.**
- **Layering vs succession**: same question → layer in place (WHEN grows a stratum, the
  prose counterpart of dated `rulings:`); evolved question → new card with `derived_from`
  pointing at its ancestor. Improve-don't-churn applied to evidence.
- **Invalidation by drift** (the "fake news" state): a confirmed result whose premise the
  world later broke is neither denied (it was true when run) nor merely old — its current
  applicability died. Lever: `status: deprecated` + WHEN narrating the stratigraphy ("held
  from X until Y changed"). New loop rule joining D6: **retraction propagates** — deprecating
  evidence forces a re-read of the Bets whose confidence it fed; the loop runs backwards too.
- **Scope guardrail**: WHEN = biography-when, subsuming #633's planning-when; mechanism-when
  (trigger conditions) stays in HOW, per the existing template rules.
- **Follow-up logged (out of today's scope): the library-wide WHEN reactivation pass** —
  product cards want biographies too (shipped history, current standing, roadmap intent);
  that is the living roadmap fully realized, a pass on the scale of the de-narrate pass.

### WHEN / the roadmap (original proposal — horizon mapping still valid as the field layer)

- Past = Research (no horizon). Present = Experiment `state: running`. Future = Experiment
  `horizon: future` + `## WHEN` narrating the milestone it earns and its sequencing.
- **The roadmap is a view, not a card** (strategy-build precedent: "roadmap = living-plan
  view"): group `horizon: future` experiments by `milestone` → the release plan; group
  `state: running` by stopping rule → the in-flight board. Milestone names elicited from the
  director ("first proof" is already in the air for Bet #2).

### Link model

Prose wikilinks now; structured sockets later (factory issue, same as `embodied_by`):

- Experiment `## WHERE`: **tests [[Bet - …]]** (↑) · **runs in [[product cards]]** (↓).
- Research `## WHERE`: **informed [[Bet - …]] / [[product cards]]** (↑/across) · came from
  `[[Experiment - …]]` when run-born.
- Bet cards get their deferred tested-by wiring: each Bet's `## HOW` (or WHERE) gains
  wikilinks to the Experiments that test it and the Research that has moved it — closes
  embodiment-map loose end #3 via an **evidence-map.md** (same format/discipline as
  embodiment-map.md: single source for which links get written).
- Frontmatter reuse: `produces` (Experiment → Research), `derived_from` (Experiment ←
  the Bet whose question it operationalizes), `related_to`. New keys `tests` / `informs`
  deferred to a factory issue.

### Body contract (draft — to freeze in card-contract.md after rulings)

- **Research**: `## WHAT` the lesson, plainly · `## WHERE` provenance (corpus / experiment /
  signal) and what it informs (wikilinks) · `## HOW` the evidence and its limits — what was
  observed, how strong, what it does NOT establish.
- **Experiment**: `## WHAT` the question — the Bet restated as the hypothesis it secretly is ·
  `## WHERE` the Bet it tests + where it runs in the product · `## HOW` instrument and metric,
  the stopping rule in prose, what confirm/deny would look like; verdict narrated here once
  called · `## WHEN` (horizon: future only) the milestone it earns, sequencing, plan origin.
- All bodies prose-only, de-machined; structured data in frontmatter; strategy-plane voice
  rules apply.

## External research — five-literature gap-check (2026-07-07)

Five parallel researchers, each gap-checking the proposed model against one literature:
Lean Startup / hypothesis-driven entrepreneurship (Ries, Blank, Maurya, Bland/Osterwalder,
Gothelf) · product discovery (Torres, Cagan/SVPG, Gilad, Hall) · experimentation at scale
(Kohavi/Microsoft ExP, registries, pre-registration) · R&D maturity + gates (NASA TRL,
Cooper Stage-Gate, clinical phases, Cynefin, XP spikes, Heilmeier) · organizational learning
capture (Army AAR, NASA LLIS + its GAO-documented failure, ASRS, SRE postmortems, decision
journals, Commoncog, SECI). Director's constraint honored: logic only — no label imports.
Full reports in the session transcript; caveat: many claims snippet-derived (svpg.com 403s;
Gilad's exact rung labels unverified), key primaries page-verified (DARPA Heilmeier, NASA TRL,
Stage-Gate, Microsoft ExP series, GAO-02-195, sre.google, Strategyzer cards).

### Confirms (external validation, keep as designed)

1. **The two-type split is the literature's own shape.** Strategyzer ships it as a physical
   pair — Test Card (design) / Learning Card (result); Build-Measure-Learn is a loop because
   design and result are different moments with different failure modes. Experiment/Research
   recreates this independently. D1 validated.
2. **Roadmap-as-view validated twice over**: Torres/Cagan/Patton all render the roadmap as a
   view over a living structure, never a persisted entity; and Lean Startup has *no* unit for
   multi-bet release planning at all — our `milestone` grouping is genuinely additive, not an
   import we missed. D5 validated (with one amendment, gate logic, below).
3. **The pre-committed stop rule is stronger than the literature's default.** Ries stops on
   "diminishing returns" — a post-hoc, motivatedly-delayed judgment. The director's
   limits-must-exist rule is closer to clinical pre-registration. Keep; do not weaken.
4. **Research bodies should stay case-shaped** (Commoncog: experts transfer via cases;
   principles alone are "useless on their own") — the drafted WHAT/WHERE/HOW contract already
   is. Time-as-fields and shelves-as-programs drew no contradiction anywhere.

### Candidate gaps (consolidated across the five reports; several converge)

- **G1 — Experiment weight-class.** Torres's assumption test, Cynefin's safe-to-fail probe,
  and the XP spike are the *same missing thing*: a cheap, disposable, often-parallel learning
  unit below the instrumented experiment. Without a legible light tier, every question gets
  the full card shape → cheap checks never get carded (the majority of real discovery work,
  per Torres/Cagan) or fake rigor they don't have. Absorption: **`kind: probe | experiment`**
  (the `kind` field already parses as a free string — zero schema change); probe relaxes
  fill requirements (no metric/guardrails required; timebox via the existing `stop` time tag);
  spikes = probe whose only stop is a timebox and whose Research may be a null result.
- **G2 — Evidence grade.** Strategyzer's say-vs-do ladder ("what people say" caps at weak no
  matter the N), Gilad's log-scaled confidence meter, TRL's fidelity ladder
  (artifact-fidelity × environment-fidelity), and clinical phases converge: *how proven* is a
  dimension our cards lack. Without it, a 3-person demo confirm and a market-scale confirm
  read identically and Bet confidence rises on weak grounds. Absorption: one ordered
  free-string **`grade`** vital on Experiment (instrument rigor, e.g. say < demo < pilot <
  market — values are a director call) echoed on the Research it produces; plus a HOW-contract
  clause requiring verdict narration to name evidence *type* (say/do, lab/field).
- **G3 — Verdict vocabulary.** Kohavi-world is emphatic: negative ≠ inconclusive ≠ invalid.
  Inconclusive is the *majority* outcome in real programs (50–80%); a genuine denial should
  move the Bet, an inconclusive must NOT — conflating them corrupts the confidence loop
  (the plane's whole job). Invalid (bad instrument, e.g. SRM) is not a verdict at all — the
  card cycles back to planned/running and re-runs. Absorption: verdict enum gains
  **`inconclusive`**; contract rule: instrument failure never reaches `called`. Denied
  results carry an "already tried that" clause — the falsified condition + what would have to
  change to justify a re-test (negative-results literature: prevents silent re-litigation).
  Replications use `derived_from` (new Experiment ← the inconclusive one), never `related_to`.
- **G4 — Guardrails.** The OEC/guardrail split is categorical: the success metric proves the
  hypothesis; guardrails are what must not break regardless. A guardrail breach is the ONE
  sanctioned early stop; stopping because the success metric "looks good" is the peeking
  fallacy. Our `stop.signal` tag currently conflates the two. Absorption: **`guardrails:`**
  list ({tag, note}, mirror-shape of `stop`) on full-grade experiments (optional at probe
  weight); `stop.signal` reserved for guardrail-class triggers by contract language.
- **G5 — Pre-registration + expected outcome.** Every calibration system (decision journals,
  AAR's "what was supposed to happen", registered reports, ExP's Seedfinder) binds validity to
  *when the commitment was made*. Two absorptions: (i) `stop`, metric, and guardrails
  **freeze at the planned→running transition** — and since state transitions will be Ledger
  events, we get a provable pre-registration timestamp for free; (ii) new **`expected:`**
  field filled before running, so the verdict is read against a prediction and *surprise* —
  the highest-value learning signal — becomes visible. Retrofitted expectations are worthless;
  capture from day one even though viewer surfacing waits.
- **G6 — Research register (direction + settledness).** The generative/evaluative research
  distinction says our Research type is doing three jobs: corpus lessons that *founded* bets,
  results that *measure* bets, and raw observations/opportunities not yet resolved into
  either (Torres's opportunity layer — pre-bet, allowed to be open). Collapsing them erases
  causal direction in the Strategy↔Learning loop and pressures half-formed observations to
  masquerade as settled lessons. Absorption: a **register value on Research's `kind`**
  (e.g. founding-lesson | result | observation — labels TBD by director); an `observation`
  may legitimately point at nothing yet and later distill into a lesson or seed a new Bet.
- **G7 — Milestone earn-logic.** Grouping-as-view is fine; the *kill/earn* half is missing:
  nothing says which experiments a release actually hangs on. Cooper's must-meet (binary,
  knock-out) vs should-meet (scored) is the proven cut; Heilmeier Q8 (mid-term vs final exam)
  seconds it. Without it, "have we earned this release?" is re-derived by eye and drifts.
  Absorption: **`gate: must-meet | should-meet`** on milestone-bearing experiments +
  convention: a milestone is earned when all its must-meets are called-confirmed. Pure
  content + view logic later; no new card type (D5 stands).
- **G8 — Consumption mechanism (the LLIS warning).** The sharpest finding: NASA's lessons
  system failed *exactly* the way a write-only Research shelf would — passive pull, no push,
  lessons unread at decision moments (GAO-02-195; managers took ~2 weeks to search; 27%
  didn't know it existed). Everything that works binds the record to a live event (AAR),
  a scheduled revisit (decision journals), or an active push layer (SRE newsletters/rituals).
  Absorption: a **mechanism, not a field** — at Bet/Experiment authoring time, existing
  Research touching the same risks/question must be surfaced (Raven's job in the plays; later
  a lint on the evidence-map + the structured-socket factory issue). Named in the plane
  keystone: the plane exists to be read at decision time, not archived.
- **G9 — Principle-grain Research.** Commoncog: distilled cross-cutting principles are an
  index into cases, not a replacement. Absorption: existing altitude ladder — a
  `pillar`-altitude Research card (distilled principle, e.g. "de-risking ≠
  confidence-building") must wikilink to ≥1 `value`-altitude case card that instantiates it.
  Authoring convention only; also refines D4 (Research is not uniformly `value`).

Minor, folded into contracts rather than fields: Heilmeier Q2 ("how is it done today and
what are the limits") as an Experiment WHAT/HOW clause; cost-as-forecast vs stop-as-ceiling
noted (Heilmeier asks for a budget, our stop is a kill-switch — the stop list's spend tag can
carry the forecast in its note).

## Amended ruling set (post-research, 2026-07-07 — supersedes bare D1–D5)

- **D1 Types — CONSOLIDATED (2026-07-07, after the Measure/Release challenge; subsumes D7).**
  The plane's type roster is **Research · Experiment · Measure** (11→13 buckets), generated
  by the **type test**: a first-class type = a distinct *lifecycle signature* — a different
  relationship to time, different vitals, a different way of touching Bets. Research =
  evidence **held** (settled, no lifecycle; informs). Experiment = evidence **sought,
  bounded** (ends by design: stop/states/verdict; tests). Measure = evidence **flowing**
  (never ends: definition/target/trend, no verdict; watches). Held/bounded/flowing — and a
  card's type never changes as time passes (the field-not-shelf principle applied to types).
  What the test excludes: **Observation** is Research earlier in its life — it matures in
  place via the register `kind` (observation → lesson), like stub → confirmed; a type would
  force type-churn on distillation. **Measurement/readings** are high-frequency events with
  no narrative — Ledger/telemetry, not cards (Play vs Play Run precedent); a *significant*
  reading becomes Research (kind: result, origin: measurement) — that is how Measures feed
  the plane. **Release** classifies nothing — it coordinates knowledge in time across all
  three planes, so it is an organizer (see D8), not a type; its content is relational
  (membership/gates/sequence = view material), and a shipped release's slice doesn't decay,
  it freezes into "what happened."
  **Challenge (director): why not one umbrella bucket "Evidence" with the three downstream?**
  Resolution (recommended): the umbrella already exists — it is the **plane**. "Learning"
  occupies the collection-noun slot exactly as "Strategy" does (no `Strategy` type exists;
  the plane name + `_index` keystone narrate how Bet/Principle relate; likewise no `Product`
  type over the product plane's eight). An `Evidence` type would (1) copy the plane axis
  into the type axis, breaking the ruled orthogonality (type ⊥ altitude ⊥ organizers);
  (2) repeat the retired-Rationale mistake in reverse — the 2026-07-06 ruling refined the
  umbrella into first-class types precisely because different registers carry different
  vitals/contracts, and type is what folders, icons, contracts, and lints key off — a union
  type discriminated by `kind` would need type→kind→sub-kind (three levels in a two-level
  schema, since `kind` is already spoken for on Experiment and Research); (3) mislabel its
  own contents — only Research IS evidence; Experiment and Measure are *instruments*
  (bounded and continuous) that produce it, which is why "Learning," covering activity and
  yield, is the right umbrella word and "Evidence" is only Research's register. If the
  umbrella deserves a card, that is the plane keystone `Concept - Learning` (+ optionally a
  knowledge-organization vocabulary card), not a type. Acknowledged cost of first-class:

## Release, Measure, incidental learning (director challenge, 2026-07-07)

Director: the library is a better, living roadmap — what happened / what's happening / what
will happen / why. A roadmap needs the release schedule, the phase, sequence and priority —
which live at the **intersection of all three planes**. And plenty of releases aren't about
an experiment: we build to fulfill a strategic bet, with measurement defined; the build
generates insights from what we set out to measure; that drives strategy and may *demand* an
experiment. Inventions are often accidental/incidental — important learning along the way.
Don't over-tack to experiment-driven development.

Concession first: the elicitation plan's Phase 3 was already over-tacked — the bets'
elicited tested-by instruments (hours-transferred golden metric, switching/consolidation
hours, adoption/substitution) are **standing measures, not experiments**: no stop rule, no
verdict, no end. An experiment *ends*; a measure *persists and reads*.

### The structural resolution (proposed)

1. **Measure = third first-class Learning-plane type (12→13 buckets).** The backwards case
   differs from an experiment in one structural way: which came first, the question or the
   build. Experiment: the question constructs the build (instrument). Build-and-measure: the
   bet constructs the build; measurement rides along; questions emerge from readings. Forcing
   builds into Experiment cards manufactures fake hypotheses and fake stop rules. Second,
   independent argument (Kohavi/ExP): mature orgs treat metrics as **shared, owned, defined
   objects that experiments reference** — OEC/guardrail are *roles a measure plays in an
   experiment*, not kinds of metric; so G4's embedded `guardrails:` strings were the wrong
   factoring. A Measure card defines a named quantity and its meaning: what it measures,
   where it reads from (instrumented product surfaces), what it informs (bets), target/trend
   as vitals; readings are telemetry/Ledger business, not card content. The golden metric =
   keystone-grade Measure. Plane assignment: learning, by the Bet precedent (counting code =
   product Mechanism; the measure's meaning/target/strategic significance = learning).
   Register cut rhymes with Bet/Principle: Experiment ends-with-verdict · Measure
   persists-and-reads.
2. **Release = an organizer (time axis), not a card type.** Sequence/priority spanning all
   three planes is the tell: organizers (Plane/Context/Domain — the ruled "third thing",
   neither type nor altitude) organize *space*; Release is the same move applied to *time* —
   a key cards from all three planes carry. A release IS its cross-plane slice:
   `horizon: future` product cards being built (the #663 WHEN machinery was built for
   exactly this), the Measures watching them, the Experiments (if any) gating them, the Bets
   advanced. The roadmap = the library re-sliced by the time organizer (G7's
   experiments-only milestone framing was too narrow). Earn-logic generalizes: build-type
   release *done* when its product cards flip horizon, *judged* when its measures read;
   experiment-gated release *earned* when must-meets confirm. **Open sub-question:** does a
   live release instance get a thin lead card (like a context lead) narrating intent —
   recommended home: learning `_index`, since learning owns "what this release must prove" —
   or stay a pure view?
3. **Incidental learning = no new type.** G6 `observation` register + an origin value
   (emerged-from-build, beside desk-research/run-result/signal) + a wikilink to the product
   card/release where it surfaced. What it needs is a **trigger discipline** (AAR: capture
   binds to an event): the release boundary is the natural harvest moment — "what did we
   learn building this that we didn't set out to learn?" — which also supplies the ritual
   layer the LLIS warning (G8) demands.

**Net reframe:** experiment-driven development is the special case. General loop:
**Bet → Release (plan) → Build → Measure → Insight (Research) → strategy update → (when a
bounded question surfaces) → Experiment.** Type roster: Research (evidence held) · Experiment
(evidence sought by design) · Measure (evidence flowing continuously); Release = the
time-organizer binding the planes into the living roadmap.

**Elicitation impact:** the release ladder moves from Phase 4 to the front (it's the
skeleton); the per-bet future phase splits into (a) define standing Measures, (b) design
Experiments only where a bounded question exists.

## Releases — intent and canon, never execution (the Marvel-wiki reframe, 2026-07-07)

Director's challenge on D5+G7: Alexandria is not Trello. Work management — done/not-done,
issues, who's doing what — lives in GitHub (imperfect, but how we work), and releases are
*operationally managed* there. Product roadmaps at most orgs are moldy paper: product keeps
them, tech ignores them, they get back-filled from what tech did. The wanted middle is
**intent**. Organizing metaphor: Marvel's wikis (the director's context-library-pt2 essay,
2026-02-09; copy at .context/attachments/prISlF/pasted_text_2026-07-07_15-11-08.txt).

What the metaphor rules:

1. **Library = wiki; GitHub = bullpen.** The comic (release) is written and shipped in the
   bullpen; the wiki never manages that work. Its relationship to an issue is exactly
   two-directional: it **informs** the work going in (intent) and **reports on** the work
   coming out (canon). The library refuses to model how anyone plans or executes —
   spam-code startups and heavy planners get the same canon.
2. **Gate becomes intent, not enforcement.** "This is the most important thing this release
   — make it happen" is a declaration the library holds; GitHub decides when things ship.
   Marvel's own structure supplies the membership model: appearances carry roles
   (featured / cameo / flashback) → a release *features* some cards (must-meet: the arc
   exists for this) and others *cameo* (should-meet: riding along). Importance is per-arc
   (a property of the appearance), never global to the card.
3. **Done-ness is reported, never managed — the mechanism already exists.** The sweep is
   the wiki's correspondent: it scans shipped reality and flips the product card's horizon.
   The comic publishes; the wiki updates.
4. **Proof-standing stays fully in-canon** — experiments, measures, verdicts are library
   citizens; "what must this arc prove and where does the proof stand" is answered natively.
   Shipping with a promise unkept is recorded honestly (the rushed issue) — institutional
   memory most companies never keep.
5. **The arc page resolves D8**: Marvel wikis have event pages (Secret Wars) narrating an
   arc and linking everything it touched. Thin release lead card: WHAT = the arc + its
   featured-intent declaration (+ D10 proof clause where a metric exists); WHY = bets
   advanced; WHEN = biography (planned / in-flight / shipped). Home: learning `_index`
   (learning owns what an arc must prove); fallback if arcs crowd the index: a small
   dedicated shelf.
6. **Three tenses, sharpened**: future arcs = planned intent; the current arc = the present
   being reported; past arcs = frozen canon. Public Marvel wikis tell only the past; the
   internal writers' wiki holds all three tenses to unify the story — that internal wiki is
   what we are building.

Essay-sourced contract additions: the **explicit-N/A rule** — every card addresses its
dimensions or explicitly marks N/A ("N/A — planned, no past yet" is complete; silence is
incomplete) — lintable, joins the body contract for all learning cards (and pairs with
biography-WHEN, whose definition the essay independently confirms: WHEN = "what's the
story — publication history, key arcs"). And the coherence argument: "the structure itself
— not coordination meetings, not central authority — keeps it coherent" is the case for arc
pages existing at all. Kent's aligned-perception passage ("enshrining what is mutually
agreed upon and crafting tests to clear up disagreement") is the experiments shelf in
embryo — cite it in the plane keystone's source trail.

### Evidence Strength — supersedes G2's say/demo/pilot/market (RATIFIED)

- **Concept noun = "Evidence Strength."** Frontmatter field stays `grade` — `strength` as a
  key name already means something else in the taxonomy (Principle's hard/soft field).
- **Rung words, renamed and re-topped**: say/demo/pilot/market → **reported → demonstrated →
  piloted → at-scale** (top rung renamed from "established" to kill an "established = it's
  true" misreading). Method modeled: Strategyzer say-vs-do × NASA TRL's artifact/environment
  fidelity axes × clinical-trial phases — the ordinal logic is earned from the literature,
  the label words are a director call (page-verified in `research-reports.md`).
- **THE disambiguation**: `grade` was conflating two orthogonal things — the **stage** the
  evidence was produced at (the four rungs) and the **quality of the case for a claim**
  (confidence). Resolution:
  - **`grade` = stage only, verdict-neutral.** Rates the instrument (direct/real/
    representative), never the result — an at-scale test can still fail ("we rigorously
    proved it's meh").
  - **`verdict`** (Experiment) stays the separate field for what was found.
  - **A Bet-risk's per-claim confidence is a rollup** across its attached evidence, each
    piece weighted by stage — it is Bet `confidence`, not a new evidence-card field (the
    Strategyzer Innovation Scorecard rollup, applied).
- **Rule A — grade is per-claim**: attach evidence to the specific Bet-risk it addresses (the
  same study can be near-at-scale for one claim, only demonstrated for another).
- **Rule B — N is not a rung**: more reps sharpen within-rung confidence or cross a Measure's
  statistical-power threshold (which emits a Research card) — never a promotion. (Resolves:
  dogfooding = demonstrated not piloted, since piloted needs an independent/representative
  subject; unprompted signal can outrank prompted opinion at the same rung; a hundred
  readings of us is still not "piloted.")
- **A Measure never carries `grade`**: it reads, it doesn't stage. "In flight" = instrument
  maturity (instrumented? reading at power?); crossing power with a notable reading emits a
  Research card, which is where a stage + verdict attach. **OPEN** (parked to Phase 3 Measure
  design): does in-flight maturity want its own vital (`instrumented`/`powered`) or live in
  `trend` prose?
- **Must also live as a library card, not just this log** (the director's meta-point — this
  IS the dogfood): `Concept - Evidence Strength` (knowledge-organization) carrying the four
  rungs, the stage⊥verdict⊥confidence split, Rules A/B, and the Measure-emits-Research
  mechanic. Scaffolding work, not this PR (A5, on hold for #666).

### Arc vocabulary — supersedes D5/D8's milestone/gate (RATIFIED)

- **Not a ladder** — a **timeline in three tenses** (shipped=canon / current=reported /

  ladder; this isn't a second one).
- **Unit = `Arc`** (dropped Chapter/Release as synonyms — "release" is what software teams
  call it, not the library noun). Definition: *a named stretch of the product's story —
  intent going in, canon coming out.* **Field rename: `milestone` → `arc`.**
- **Roles, renamed**: **`role: headline | supporting`** replaces both featured/cameo and
  must-meet/should-meet (the old field name was `gate`; "must" re-smuggled the enforcement
  reading the "intent not enforcement" ruling, D5+G7+D8, killed). **Field rename: `gate` →
  `role`.**
- **The heart is plural and cross-plane**: an arc's heart is a SET of `role: headline` cards,
  one from each plane the arc touches (a Bet, a product instantiation, a piece of evidence) —

- **`role: headline` is the per-arc override on altitude, both directions**: it promotes a
  low-altitude card into the heart when the arc is specifically about it ("the company's

  merely present. Altitude is the default; role is the exception lever, not a duplicate of
  it.

Company Design]] → the onboarding flow that grows Raven from a director's documented company
