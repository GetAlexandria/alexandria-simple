## Validation Criteria

- Scanner proposals are presented grouped by domain, not dumped as a flat list
- Summary layer shown first ("12 entities in 4 domains, here are the top 5")
- User can confirm, rename, merge, split, or reject each proposal
- Confirmed entities are written to wizard-config.json as "present" status
- Confirmed entities feed into gap analysis (Step 5) with appropriate scoring
- A solo builder with a typical codebase completes the flow in under 10 minutes

## Motivation

The user IS the product expert. The scanner proposes what it sees; the human provides the intent layer ("Notifications is our core engagement system, not a utility"). The grouped conversational flow respects the QA-by-Dumping anti-pattern while being efficient enough to stay under 10 minutes.
