## The three bets (corporate how-to-win)
*Independent — one can be false while others hold; each has a rollback; at least one is probably wrong.*

### Bet #2 — Colleagues as the primary interaction layer  · **KEYSTONE**
- **Claim:** business adopts named, persistent AI colleagues as the primary way it interacts with AI — over hidden/embedded/unnamed AI.
- **State:** contested (market grain runs toward hidden/embedded AI; high conviction; close).
- **Rollback:** colleagues go invisible — automation/features, not employees.
- **North-star metric:** human-colleague-hours transferred to full AI-colleague ownership (+ needed-but-undone work). Tens of thousands → millions. Measured on SocioTechnica first, then customers.

### Bet #3 — Centralization: colleagues grown from AI-native company design  · **MOAT**
- **Claim:** great colleagues are grown from a centralized, deliberately-designed company substrate (one living library/playbook/ledger/triggers) — not assembled from disparate agents.
- **Mechanism (why it's the moat):** the library = the company's single **living source of truth** → colleagues get real, current info → coherence. Disparate agents run on stale, scattered context → incoherence. Centralization is the *only way* colleagues are actually good.
- **State:** contested (most — the whole agent ecosystem bets on disparate/composable agents).
- **Rollback:** substrate loosens → Alexandria becomes an orchestration/marketplace layer over independent agents.
- **Test:** head-to-head quality (grown-from-substrate vs bolted-together) + consolidation (hours: retail-AI / CLI → Alexandria).

### Bet #1 — Work environments must be visual, immersive, traversible
- **Claim:** the work environment — the work, the agents, the context — must be visual/immersive/traversible, not lived through text/CLI/chat. Two facets: **traversible/spatial** (the map) + **immersive/present** (embodied presence).
- **State:** untested.
- **Rollback:** viewer thins to a CLI; map/coin/presence come out; work returns to text.
- **Refraction:** the paradigm spans bets — lifebuild = life-map, Alexandria = work-map ("map-first living").
