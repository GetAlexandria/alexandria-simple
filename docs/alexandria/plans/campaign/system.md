# SocioTechnica New Media System

> The operating system that turns SocioTechnica's real work into content
> **produced once and re-packaged across channels** — run by an AI team so two
> people out-produce a media org. The "AI team" is also the **decomposition of
> the Damien EP agent**. Companion to [`strategy.md`](./strategy.md).
>
> v1 reflects Damien's EP review: slimmed to a Season-1 footprint, with a
> **north-star** and an **outreach handoff** so the System serves the campaign
> goal (get resourced) rather than vanity output.

## Purpose & definition of "working"

A vignette/episode series only works if there's a System producing and
distributing it — but the campaign isn't "make content," it's **get resourced**.
So the System is measured against a **north-star, not reactions**:

> **North-star: qualified acquirer/client conversations started + owned-email
> growth** (e.g. double owned email — currently ~500 — over the campaign).

Reactions and views are leading indicators, not the goal. The System produces
*warm signal*; the **business-development playbook converts it** (see the
outreach handoff below).

It exists to: out-produce two people (AI does the repetitive work; humans keep
judgment, capture, approval); produce once and re-package everywhere; compound an
owned audience; and tell the "AI at companies" story to acquirers + clients.

## The production loop (one engine, ~2×/week)

```
signal → angle / episode-thesis
  → [proof check: which REAL, demoable capability does this show?]
  → primary asset (vignette, or a written piece)
  → [HUMAN approval gate]
  → multi-format packaging → distribution per channel → community / response
  → [direct outreach (1:1): each founder shares the asset with 5–15 contacts —
       feedback ask or conversation proposal — logged to the biz-dev CRM]
  → measurement (against the north-star)
  → reusable prompts / assets / playbooks → (loop)
```

Two additions Damien flagged as load-bearing:

- **Proof check** — every episode maps to a real, demoable capability (receipts,
  not sizzle); source from the proof inventory below. Strongest receipts are the
  **dogfooded** products (WeDo, Hearthfire).
- **Direct outreach (1:1)** — the loop does **not** dead-end at broadcast. For
  every asset, **each founder direct-shares it with 5–15 well-connected personal
  contacts** (DM / text), asking for feedback or proposing a mutual conversation.
  This is the **highest-conversion tier** and the explicit seam to the
  business-development workstream — relationships and tracking live in its CRM
  (the shelved sales-marketing Alexandria + outreach agent). This is what
  connects media to the campaign goal (qualified conversations).

The unit of work is a **content object**, not "a video" — versioned and
re-packaged across surfaces.

## Channels & package

### Front door (owned home): **sociotechnica.org**

The owned home; its job is to **tie the constellation together** (products,
books, episodes, social, lists). Hosts the dated "nine-square" episode package +
the lab notes blog (13 notes).

### Operating rule (Season 1)

> Publish from the **founders' personal reach** → **drive to sociotechnica.org**
> → **capture email.**

Two distinct kinds of reach — use both deliberately, don't conflate raw count
with audience fit:

- **Peer / amplification reach (Jess-led):** X `@jessmartin` (3.8k), Bluesky
  `@jessmart.in` (1.7k), YouTube `@jessamartin` (346), personal list (380).
  Large, but skews the **future-of-computing / tools-for-thought crowd — not the
  target buyers.** Best for credibility, sharing, and brand-building.
- **Target-audience reach (Danvers-led): LinkedIn.** Danvers
  (`/in/danversfleury`, ~1,000) + Jess (`/in/jessmartin`, 850). Smaller count,
  but the **highest audience fit** — acquirers, clients, and execs live here.
  LinkedIn is **Danvers' territory**; for *this* campaign it is likely the most
  important distribution channel, despite X's bigger raw numbers.

The SocioTechnica brand channels (Buttondown 138, ST Bluesky, lab notes) are
nascent and grow over the season.

### Direct outreach (1:1) — the conversion tier

Broadcast builds brand and credibility; **direct outreach starts the qualified
conversations the campaign is actually after.** It's the founders' biggest
underused asset: rich personal relationships, well-connected, who like knowing
what they're up to.

The motion, per asset: **each founder picks 5–15 relevant contacts and shares it
1:1 (DM / text)** with a specific ask — *feedback* or a *mutual conversation*.
High-touch, human-sent, never automated.

- **AI assists, humans send:** AI drafts the personalized note, suggests which
  contacts fit a given asset (from the CRM), and tracks who got what / who
  replied; founders personalize, send, and carry the conversation.
- This is **business-development / CRM** territory — the relationships and
  tracking live in that workstream; the System feeds it warm, asset-anchored
  reasons to reach out.

### Default package per episode (slim — protect the approval gate)

**YouTube episode + one lab note + two social posts + an email mention.** The
packager *proposes* extra derivatives (clips, written cut, page block); humans
approve extras rather than always producing the full set. (Six derivatives ×
2/week through a human gate is too much approval surface for two people.)

### Owned-audience target

Email is the durable owned channel: ST Buttondown (138) + personal list (380) ≈
**~500**. Growing this is the primary north-star proxy — far better than reaction
counts.

## Cadence & season architecture

- **2 episodes/week** — intense but appropriate for the push.
- **Pin one weekly slot** to the recurring spine: the **"AI at companies"
  pain→build vignette**. **Float the second** slot (BTS, books,
  Damien-behind-the-scenes, a product). Season coherence without locking both.
- **Episode 1 = the Friday Freeq session with Bryan & Chad** — treat it as the
  **pilot** that validates the whole loop.

## Operating model: run manually first, crystallize a skill later

Run the System **manually now** (Damien + founders). Don't freeze it into a skill
yet — run the loop for the first episode(s), learn what's stable vs. what flexes,
then extract the validated process into a `new-media-system` skill + standing
agents. Damien is already orchestrating it; the skill earns its existence once
the loop is proven.

## Open / deferred

- Standalone signal-scout + measurement agents, ST-brand channel growth,
