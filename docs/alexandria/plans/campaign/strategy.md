# SocioTechnica Campaign Strategy

> The go-to-market push to get SocioTechnica **resourced**. This is the company
> strategy that all New Media / demo work serves. Held loosely — "the plan as
> stated"; changing it could be the smartest thing we do. Source material in
> [`inputs/`](./inputs/) (conversation transcript + the "have / could ship"
> whiteboard).

## Why

Live a beautiful life and share it with others. Build useful things. Earn a seat
at the table inventing the future.

## The goal: get resourced

Change SocioTechnica's economic posture so the founders can keep building rather
than walk away with nothing. Not unlimited wealth: "enough is enough," Alan Kay
over a venture moonshot. The bar is deliberately modest and life-compatible:

- **Jess:** ~$100k this year = didn't dip too far into savings.
- **Danvers:** step up to a better run rate; on track for next year.
- A **~$300k/yr** business in North Carolina, sustained ~5 years = "made it."

## Audience for the media plan: acquirers + clients

The **media plan** targets **acquirers and clients** — they have far more in
common with each other than with anyone else:

- An acquirer might simply want us to **AI-ify their company** — effectively a
  client, a "size-small acquisition" ("salaries and equity to come revolutionize
  us"). Acquirers would also want to **sell us to clients**.
- Acquirers care about **integration edges, market opportunities, and
  distribution** — "how much scale do they have?" Strike while the iron is hot;
  get enough, now, before the world changes.
- Clients want **AI integration in their company** (~$400k contract + smaller
  ones), with a **client-acquihire** edge (the "cli-acquified" hybrid — e.g.
  Fabro, trading partnerships).

**Investors are not a media-plan target.** We're not telling a growth story in
the media. We remain **open to investors generally** — but they're pursued
deliberately in the **business-development playbook (a separate workstream)**,
not via the episodes. (The standing pyramid still has investors as a tier; the
media simply doesn't aim at them.)

**Not the Future-of-Computing crowd.** They'd love a Mother-of-All-Demos homage,
but none have companies and none could hire us — the wrong artifact for these
targets (see Media strategy).

## Story / positioning: "AI at companies"

The thing *everyone* wants to talk about is **how badly AI is going inside their
org** — dysfunctional teams, dumb implementations, overwhelmed by complexity,
weak processes, scattered and disorganized everything, a black screen and a
blinking cursor ("Claude DOS"). Most inbound conversation requests are exactly
this: "can I just talk to you about everything that sucks about using AI in my
organization? You said *factory* — does it actually work? Can we trust it?"

SocioTechnica tells the **opposite** story: operationally, organizationally, and
human-focused in light of AI. **Nobody has solved AI at companies** — and we have
real, hard-won confidence in how to do it, even as just two people.

- **Living in the unscaled future.** Like the Mother of All Demos showed a narrow
  slice of "home computing," we show a narrow slice of AI-at-companies — we hold
  the primitives and the architecture and point at the path. Even the "size
  small" version (knowing where everything is + AI making software *without us*)
  is revolutionary; "size large" (talk to AI in a meeting and they build it) is
  the dream.
- **Steak, not sizzle.** Be ourselves; record real work with mistakes left in;
  not the smoothest or most "wow" — "a future way of dealing with today."
- **The pain→build voice.** "I did this and it drove me crazy, so we built this.
  We were working with these people and just couldn't, so we did this." Sharing
  the beautiful life and how we actually work.
- **"You could do some of this too."** Use our stuff, or just the ideas.
- A **"Claude DOS"** blog post (the black screen / blinking cursor) is one
  on-brand anchor for this story on the SocioTechnica site.

## Media strategy: a vignette / episode series — not one big demo

The biggest shift: **stop trying to ship one big "Mother of All Demos" cut.**
Instead ship a **series of short vignettes / episodes** that chain together and
build over weeks.

- **Why:** everything in our universe gets materially better every week; video
  freezes a point in time. A series lets us capture the latest, ship **1–2 per
  week**, and avoid the "big-bang-then-slice" editing trap (also solves the
  TikTok problem — do the framing now, not later).
- **Silver the production.** Build the agents + a repeatable **format** so we can
  reliably crank episodes out: a comprehensible, consistent frame (consistent
  intro/outro/length/visual treatment — "you know it's ours"), variable middle.
  Think **episodes and seasons**.
- **Part of a package.** Episodes are point-in-time, embedded in a living page
  (getalexandria.ai / sociotechnica.org) — a dated "nine-square" of process +
  inventions with click-through updates. Rolling thunder.
- **Forcing function:** Friday/Saturday recording pushes; aim for **~3 episodes**
  this weekend (one is the Freeq session with Bryan & Chad; the other two TBD).
- **The MoAD homage is deferred, not dead.** ~end of July, once we've "earned
  it": either a simple re-record or a full theatrical Engelbart homage (rent an
  auditorium, costumes, slide projector, typewritten menus, agents as the "team
  back in Palo Alto"). It will help acquisition talks — but it's the wrong
  artifact for acquirers/clients *now*.

What carries through from the MoAD: steak-not-sizzle, being ourselves, recording
with mistakes in, and "showing a future way of dealing with today."

**Detailed episode planning is the next step** (a separate demo/episode plan).

## Constraints & risk discipline

- **North Carolina.** Both founders stay; relocation price "has gone up." Plan
  acquisition/work arrangements around it.
- **Next LX failure mode.** Don't over-invest weeks chasing cold acquirer leads
  "to no avail" while the safe client floor goes unbuilt. Read the tea leaves;
  clients are the floor.
- **Human approval gate.** AI drafts and does the grunt work; humans decide and
  send.
- **Enough is enough.** Resources are a means to the beautiful life, not the end.
