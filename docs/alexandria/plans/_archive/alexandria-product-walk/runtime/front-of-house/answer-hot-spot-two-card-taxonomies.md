Not a conflict — two complementary layers of the same system.

Atomic card categories (10 buckets: Rationale, Research, Roles, Domains, Surfaces, Entities, Capabilities, Mechanisms, Patterns, Economy) are the superstructure — the universal buckets that most product companies' nouns land in, regardless of the specific product. This is Alexandria's meta-schema for product libraries.

Product card v1 is the format each card follows once filed into one of those buckets — carrying a type (which category), a name, plane (strategy/product/learning), status (stub through confirmed), confidence, and links to other cards.

Legacy mode in the catalog schema is backward compatibility with the old 208-card quarantined library, kept for QA and comparison but not part of this process.

No card changes needed. The hot spot is resolved: categories are the superstructure, product card is the format. Two layers, one system.
