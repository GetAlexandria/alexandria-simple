Class rule for Role cards:

- Director: headline-level (pillar). The human running the show that all AI colleagues report to.
- Agent: the generic term for AI colleague. Headline-level as a concept — AI colleagues are the whole point of the system. (May need renaming to "AI Colleague" or similar if "agent" is too generic/technical.)
- Raven, Damien: instances/examples of the Agent class, not individually headline-level. They are example AI colleagues — Raven being the one somewhat built out, Damien having a coin and a few off-playbook skills.

The distinction: Director and the AI Colleague concept are pillars. Individual named agents are instances of the class, not separate pillars.
