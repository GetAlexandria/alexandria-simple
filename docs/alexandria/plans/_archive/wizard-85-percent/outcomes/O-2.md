## Validation Criteria

- The mode-specific risk narrative is shown after Q1 and before Q2/Q3, so users
  have a concrete failure to anchor on while answering calibration questions
- During gap analysis self-assessment, each area's "when missing" failure symptoms
  are visible alongside the status prompt, turning abstract completeness questions
  into symptom checks

## Motivation

Non-systems-thinkers can recognize concrete failures ("every feature feels equally
important") even when they can't reason about abstract properties ("how interconnected
is your product?"). By priming them with the risk narrative before questions and showing
failure symptoms during gap analysis, we convert abstract self-assessment into
experience-matching — something everyone can do.
