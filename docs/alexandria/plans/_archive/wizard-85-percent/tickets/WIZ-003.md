## Motivation

The risk narrative is the best systems-thinking primer in the wizard. "Silent wrong
defaults — hundreds of autonomous micro-decisions that cumulatively define the
experience" tells a non-systems-thinker exactly what failure looks like. Currently it
appears in the output, after all questions. Showing it before Q2/Q3 primes more
thoughtful answers.

## Description

Reorder the Step 1 flow so the mode-specific risk narrative is shown immediately
after Q1 (AI Mode), before Q2 and Q3.

**Current flow:** Q1 → Q2 → Q3 → [risk narrative in output]
**New flow:** Q1 → risk narrative → Q2 → Q3

The risk narrative is already in `wizard-engine.yaml` under `mode_narratives`. No new
content needed — just surface it earlier.

**Why after Q1:** The narrative is mode-specific. Need the mode answer first to select
the right narrative. Q1 (AI Mode) is the question least affected by systems thinking —
it's about team structure, which people observe directly.

**Files to modify:**
- `skills/wizard/SKILL.md` — Step 1 flow reorder
