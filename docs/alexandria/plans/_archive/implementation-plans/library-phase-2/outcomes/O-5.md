## Validation Criteria

- Expert calibration reference skill exists at `skills/raven/expert-calibration.md` (or equivalent path)
- Raven's wizard-mode job procedure loads the expert calibration skill on entry
- Raven surfaces the Frankenstein diagnostic early in first sessions: "If I were going to prototype something like yours, what would I grab?"
- Raven applies the guidance posture spectrum (prescriptive / advisory / transparent) based on configuration and area stakes — not a uniform level of advocacy
- Raven makes unlock logic explicit at stopping points: deterministic gates vs heuristic unlocks vs speculative unlocks
- Raven recognizes the ~25% "raw dump" trap and flags it explicitly rather than reading it as meaningful progress
- Calibration material is written as internalized working knowledge (not a script Raven reads linearly)

## Motivation

Expert calibration is what separates a confident Raven from a trustworthy one. Without calibration, Raven will open sessions, ask questions, and produce configurations — but her stopping-point calls will be theoretical, her mismatch detection will be missing, and her guidance posture will be uniform when it should be configuration-specific.

The expert interview material has already been synthesized (`docs/alexandria/sources/expert-calibration-library-construction.md`). This outcome encodes it as a reference skill Raven actually loads — closing the gap between material that exists and knowledge Raven acts on.
