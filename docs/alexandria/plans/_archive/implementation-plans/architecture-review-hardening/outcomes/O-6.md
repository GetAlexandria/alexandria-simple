## Validation Criteria

- Playbook documents a single "Health Check + Quality Cycle" play with two phases: Assess and Repair
- Grade (Job 2) is positioned as a step within the assess phase, not a standalone play
- The play definition reflects the simplified model: grade → diagnose → recommend → human approves → surgery → fix → re-grade

## Motivation

Health Check and Quality Cycle are currently documented as separate plays but are really two phases of one workflow. Assessment without repair is a to-do list that rots. Unifying them makes the play structure match how the library actually gets maintained.
