## Motivation

The `Principle - Agentic-Deterministic-Agentic Pattern` says deterministic middle layers keep mechanical work on rails while agentic outer/inner layers retain judgment. Bridget's step 6 is the canonical case: hop expansion, direction, budget, U-shape ordering have no judgment in them — they are profile execution. Agentic Glob/Grep on wikilinks is lossy under token pressure. Migrating step 6 to the CLI sharpens Bridget's verb (assemble) and removes a quiet quality risk.
