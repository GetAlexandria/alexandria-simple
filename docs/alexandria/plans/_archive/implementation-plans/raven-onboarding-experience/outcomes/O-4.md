## Validation Criteria

- Banking Vision writes Raven's Source of Truth Markdown document.
- Raven state records the Source of Truth path and content hash.
- Vision onboarding becomes `banked`.
- Raven's Knowledge Bank shows Vision as banked.
- Future Knowledge Bank subjects are visible but grayed out.
- No library cards are generated by this slice.

## Motivation

Banking is the moment Raven gains durable capability. The Source of Truth is Raven's internal product context document, and the Knowledge Bank is the user-visible checklist that shows what Raven can now rely on. This outcome keeps those concepts distinct from the Library's atomized cards.
