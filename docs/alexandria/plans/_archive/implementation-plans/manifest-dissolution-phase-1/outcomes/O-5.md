## Validation Criteria

- Every card on disk under `docs/alexandria/library/` declares an `area:` field in its YAML frontmatter
- `area:` values reference valid area IDs from `alexandria-config.json` (e.g., `1.1`, `2.2`, `5.2+5.4`)
- `packages/ax/src/tools/scoreboard-derive.ts` reads card→area attribution from frontmatter `area:` field instead of `getAreaMatchers` path-based heuristics
- The matcher table in `scoreboard-derive-matchers.ts` is retired or demoted to a fallback that emits warnings (decided during FEAT-104)
- A drift-lint check flags any card whose frontmatter `area:` is inconsistent with its folder location
- Manual sample of 24 cards (the same set plan.md analyzed) shows zero misfiles after migration — the ~5/24 misfile rate from the matcher era is resolved
- `scoreboard-derivation.md` spec is updated to describe the frontmatter-driven mechanism

## Motivation

This outcome closes the arc that PR #87 (matcher orphan cleanup) and the
type-claim taxonomy reification (this PR) opened. The original observation —
that cards should self-declare their knowledge area in YAML frontmatter rather
than be matched by filename heuristics — has been a known design issue for
months. Phase 1 finally pays it off.

Plan.md identified ~5 of 24 sampled cards as systematically misfiled by the
matcher table (Principles and Standards forced into area 1.2). The scoreboard
inherits that misfile rate. Until cards self-declare their area, every reader
that consumes "cards by area" carries the inaccuracy.

This outcome is the user-visible value the plan delivers: cards are
consistently categorized and scored. Without it, Phase 1 is plumbing without a
demo.
