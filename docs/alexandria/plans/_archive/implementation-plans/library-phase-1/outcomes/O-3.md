## Validation Criteria

- Each of the three configuration questions (AI mode, domain novelty, product
  complexity) is accompanied by a substantive explanation of why it matters
- The WHY explanation is calibrated to the specific configuration context, not a
  generic blurb (e.g., Noun Vocabulary at Factory mode gets a different explanation
  than at Short-Order Cook)
- The guidance posture spectrum is encoded: prescriptive for high-stakes areas,
  advisory for areas where the user's hypothesis may differ, transparent for
  Amplifier-tier areas
- Raven surfaces her inference before asking when scanner output or shared docs
  already suggest an answer (inference-before-asking)
- A user who pushes back on Raven's read ("I said Low complexity but it actually
  feels more medium") is met with engagement, not silent acceptance
- The guidance posture is applied at question presentation time, not just at
  output time

## Motivation

The current wizard presents configuration questions as a form: each question
arrives with a brief label and option set, no explanation of why the question
matters or what the answer changes downstream. Users pick options without
understanding the stakes.

The expert calibration material (sections 3-5) describes the guidance posture
spectrum: prescriptive when being wrong guarantees hardship; advisory when the
team's hypothesis could legitimately differ; transparent when user preference
should drive. The same knowledge area warrants different guidance intensity
depending on configuration. A single generic blurb per question doesn't cover
this. Raven should calibrate her explanation to the specific configuration in
front of her.

This outcome is "should" rather than "must" because the opener (O-1) and
greenfield path (O-2) are the structural changes. Configuration question
presentation is an experience improvement that layers on top.
