## Validation Criteria

- On Jess's repo, `ax scoreboard render .` shows Product Entities with coverage (not 0%).
- On Bryan's repo, `ax scoreboard render .` shows Foundation as FULL given his 24-of-24 closed library.
- `ax lint` accepts every canonical card type (including `Anti-Pattern`) without unknown-type errors.
- `packages/ax/src/lib/graph.ts` `KNOWN_TYPES` is the sole source imported by scoreboard matchers and the linter — no string duplication.
- Knowledge-area → card-type mapping lives in typed code read by the initialize engine, scoreboard, and Bridget's profiles.

## Motivation

Bryan's scoreboard showed 25% Foundation on a library he had just closed at 24/24 cards; our own scoreboard shows Product Entities at 0% against a library that documents every product entity under other type names. Both failures trace to the same class of bug: scoreboard matchers, the linter, and the initialize engine each carry their own copy of the type taxonomy, and those copies have drifted from the live `KNOWN_TYPES`. This outcome collapses the drift to a single typed source. Fixes the symptom, removes the class.
