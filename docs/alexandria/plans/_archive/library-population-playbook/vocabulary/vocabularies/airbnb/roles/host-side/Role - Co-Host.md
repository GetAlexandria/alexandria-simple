# Co-Host

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a delegated host role: a person the primary [[Role - Host]] invites to help manage one or more [[Entity - Listing|Listings]]. Co-Hosts can perform operational tasks — managing [[Entity - Reservation|Reservations]], communicating with [[Role - Guest|Guests]], updating the [[Surface - Calendar]], adjusting the [[Entity - Listing]] — within the permission scope the Host grants. Co-Host is a sold feature: it became a formal platform capability (not just an informal workaround) with its own permission tiers and payout routing._

_A Co-Host earns a share of Host payouts negotiated directly with the primary Host. The Co-Host role is distinct from a professional property management company, though property managers often operate as Co-Hosts. Airbnb chose "Co-Host" over "Secondary Manager" or "Property Manager" to preserve the hospitality-register "Host" identity across both the primary and delegated case._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Role - Host]] (the primary Host who grants Co-Host access), [[Entity - Listing]] (the Listing Co-Host helps manage), [[Surface - Host Dashboard]] (Co-Hosts access this surface within their permission scope), [[Economy-instance - Payout]] (Co-Hosts receive a share of Host payouts)._
