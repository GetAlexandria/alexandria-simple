# Co-Traveler

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a secondary guest-side participant who travels with the primary [[Role - Guest]] but does not hold the [[Entity - Reservation]]. Co-Travelers are named on the booking for occupancy counting and Host notification purposes but lack the account-level identity of the primary Guest. Airbnb uses "Co-Traveler" primarily in the Trips context rather than the Reservation context._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Role - Guest]] (the primary booking holder; Co-Traveler travels alongside), [[Entity - Trip]] (the experience-layer concept where Co-Travelers are associated), [[Entity - Reservation]] (the transaction record owned by the primary Guest)._
