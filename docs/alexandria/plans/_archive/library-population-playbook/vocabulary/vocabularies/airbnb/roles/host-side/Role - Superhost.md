# Superhost

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — both a Role tier and an Economy-status: a [[Role - Host]] who has earned the Superhost designation by meeting Airbnb's published performance thresholds (minimum stay count, rating average, response rate, and cancellation rate) over a trailing assessment window. Superhost is displayed as a badge on the Host's profile and on every [[Entity - Listing]] they own, conferring search-ranking uplift and Guest trust signals. The designation is re-evaluated quarterly; Hosts lose it if thresholds slip._

_As a faceted concept, Superhost operates simultaneously as a Role tier (a variant of the Host role with different platform-side treatment) and as an Economy-status ([[Economy-instance - Superhost Status]]) — the meta-resource that translates past performance into ongoing distribution advantage. Airbnb chose "Superhost" over "Premier Host" or "Certified Host" to keep the hospitality-register "Host" identity central: the prefix "Super" signals magnitude, not category-change. This is the one modifier in the Airbnb signature that could have been a full new noun but deliberately wasn't._
