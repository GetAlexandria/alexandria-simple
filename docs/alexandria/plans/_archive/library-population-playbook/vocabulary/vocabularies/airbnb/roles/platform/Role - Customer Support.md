# Customer Support

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the platform-side intermediary: Airbnb staff or contractors who intervene when a [[Role - Guest]] or [[Role - Host]] encounters a problem that can't be resolved between the two parties. Customer Support has elevated platform permissions — they can issue refunds ([[Entity - Refund]]), mediate disputes ([[Entity - Resolution]]), and override cancellation policy outcomes. Customer Support is the human layer on top of the [[System - Trust and Safety]] engine._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Resolution]] (the dispute artifact Customer Support manages), [[Entity - Refund]] (Customer Support can authorize refunds outside normal policy), [[System - Trust and Safety]] (the system-layer counterpart to this human role), [[System - AirCover]] (the insurance product Customer Support administers for claims)._
