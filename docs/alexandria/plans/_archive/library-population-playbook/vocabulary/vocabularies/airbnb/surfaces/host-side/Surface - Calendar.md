# Calendar

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the [[Role - Host]]'s availability and pricing surface: a date-grid view where Hosts block or open dates, set minimum stays, and apply custom [[Economy-instance - Nightly Rate|Nightly Rates]] per date or date range. The Calendar is both a supply-control surface (which dates are available to [[Capability - Booking|book]]) and a pricing surface (per-night prices that override the base rate). [[System - Smart Pricing]] recommendations are also displayed and applied here. The Calendar is the central operational tool for active Host management._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Listing]] (Calendar is per-Listing), [[Economy-instance - Nightly Rate]] (base and custom rates managed here), [[System - Smart Pricing]] (pricing suggestions displayed in the Calendar), [[Entity - Reservation]] (confirmed Reservations block dates on the Calendar), [[Capability - Pricing]] (the Capability this surface supports)._

## WHY: Rationale

_Stub — owner-supplied._

## WHEN: Timeline

_Stub._

## HOW: Specification

_Stub — to be enriched with: date-level availability toggle (blocked / open); custom pricing per date, date range, or day of week; minimum and maximum stay length settings; preparation time (blocked nights between reservations); how iCal sync with external calendars (VRBO, direct booking) works; how Smart Pricing overlays on the Calendar._
