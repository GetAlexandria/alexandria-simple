# Verification

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the identity-verification subsystem that validates [[Role - Guest]] and [[Role - Host]] identities before allowing full platform access. Verification collects and checks government ID documents, selfie-to-ID matching, and in some cases phone and email confirmation. The Verification system outputs a verified/unverified status that appears as a trust signal on user profiles and that gates certain Listing and booking permissions. Hosts can require verified Guests for their Listings._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[System - Trust and Safety]] (Verification feeds identity signals into Trust and Safety), [[Role - Guest]] (Guests must verify to book certain Listings), [[Role - Host]] (Hosts must verify to publish Listings; verification badge appears on profile), [[Entity - Listing]] (Hosts can require Guest verification as a Listing setting)._
