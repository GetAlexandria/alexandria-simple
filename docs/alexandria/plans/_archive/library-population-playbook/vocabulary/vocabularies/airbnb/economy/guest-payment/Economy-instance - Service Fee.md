# Service Fee

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — Airbnb's platform charge to the [[Role - Guest]]: a percentage of the booking subtotal ([[Economy-instance - Nightly Rate]] × nights + [[Economy-instance - Cleaning Fee]]) added at checkout. The Service Fee is Airbnb's primary revenue mechanism on the guest side — it is displayed in the price breakdown in [[Surface - Booking Flow]] but is not shown in the per-night rates on [[Surface - Search]] (a source of guest friction and the driver of Airbnb's total-price display change). The Guest Service Fee is distinct from the [[Economy-instance - Host Service Fee]]._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Economy-instance - Nightly Rate]] (the rate the Service Fee is calculated on), [[Economy-instance - Cleaning Fee]] (included in the subtotal the Service Fee applies to), [[Economy-instance - Host Service Fee]] (the parallel fee charged to the Host), [[Entity - Payment]] (the total Payment includes this fee), [[Surface - Booking Flow]] (where the Service Fee is disclosed)._
