# Experiences

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — Airbnb's second product domain: a marketplace for host-led activities and tours that Guests book separately from or in conjunction with a [[Domain - Stays]] [[Entity - Listing]]. An Experience is not a place to sleep — it is a time-bounded activity (a cooking class, a walking tour, a photography session) hosted by a person rather than a property. Experiences launched in 2016 as Airbnb's expansion beyond accommodations. The Experience booking flow, pricing model (per-person rather than per-night), and supply model (individual hosts, not properties) differ from the Stays domain._

_Stub — links to: [[Domain - Stays]] (the parallel domain), [[Role - Host]] (Experience Hosts are a distinct Host sub-type within this domain), [[Role - Guest]] (Guests who book Experiences)._
