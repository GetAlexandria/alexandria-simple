# Photo

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the visual documentation of a [[Entity - Listing]]: images uploaded by the [[Role - Host]] to represent the [[Entity - Property]], its rooms, and its [[Entity - Amenity|Amenities]]. Photos are the primary conversion driver on the [[Surface - Listing Detail Page]] — Airbnb's research consistently identifies photo quality as the largest determinant of click-through and booking conversion. The cover photo is the first image shown in [[Surface - Search]] results._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Listing]] (Photos are attached to and ordered on the Listing), [[Surface - Listing Detail Page]] (the surface where Photos are presented to Guests), [[Surface - Search]] (cover Photo appears in search result tiles), [[Capability - Listing Creation]] (the capability during which Photos are uploaded and ordered)._
