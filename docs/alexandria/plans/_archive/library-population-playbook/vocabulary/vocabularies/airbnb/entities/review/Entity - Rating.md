# Rating

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the numeric component of a [[Entity - Review]]: the star score (1–5) a [[Role - Guest]] assigns to a [[Entity - Listing]] and [[Role - Host]] after a [[Entity - Stay]]. Ratings are multi-dimensional: overall, and sub-ratings for cleanliness, accuracy, check-in experience, Host communication, location, and value. The average Overall Rating is the primary trust signal on the Listing and in [[Surface - Search]] results. Ratings drive the [[Economy-instance - Star Rating]] economy-layer resource and feed the [[Pattern - Superhost Qualification]] threshold._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Review]] (the Review of which this Rating is the numeric component), [[Economy-instance - Star Rating]] (the Economy-layer resource that aggregates individual Ratings), [[Pattern - Superhost Qualification]] (overall rating threshold for Superhost status), [[Surface - Listing Detail Page]] (where aggregate Ratings are displayed), [[Surface - Search]] (Rating displayed in search result cards)._
