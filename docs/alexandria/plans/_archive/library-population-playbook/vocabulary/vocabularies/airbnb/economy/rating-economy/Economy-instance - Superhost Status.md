# Superhost Status

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the meta-resource earned by [[Role - Host|Hosts]] who meet performance thresholds under the [[Pattern - Superhost Qualification]] pattern: a durable, publicly-visible designation that translates past hosting performance into ongoing distribution advantage. As an Economy-instance, Superhost Status is a storable, transferable-in-effect resource — it is earned, displayed, lost when thresholds slip, and re-earned when performance recovers. Its economic value is measured in increased [[System - Search Ranking]] placement and higher Guest trust, both of which compound into more bookings and higher nightly rates._

_Superhost Status is a faceted concept: as an Economy-instance it is the meta-resource (earned, quantified, displayed); as a [[Role - Superhost]] it is the role-tier extension of the Host identity. The same underlying designation appears in both Category folders. The primary home is Economy (the resource and its value mechanics); the Role card is the persona and permissions face. Cross-link: [[Role - Superhost]]._
