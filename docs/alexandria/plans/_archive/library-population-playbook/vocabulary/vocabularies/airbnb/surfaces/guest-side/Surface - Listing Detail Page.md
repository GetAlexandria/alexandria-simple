# Listing Detail Page

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — Airbnb's equivalent of the Product Detail Page (PDP): the Guest-facing surface that presents the full content of a single [[Entity - Listing]] and drives booking conversion. The Listing Detail Page is the most conversion-tracked surface in the product — Airbnb's growth and product teams measure click-to-book rate here as the primary conversion metric. It shows the [[Entity - Photo]] gallery, [[Entity - Amenity]] list, [[Role - Host]] profile, [[Entity - Review|Reviews]] and [[Entity - Rating|Ratings]], pricing breakdown, and availability calendar. The Book button here initiates the [[Surface - Booking Flow]]._

_The Listing Detail Page is structured as a scrolling narrative: photos first (the conversion trigger), then the pitch (title, description, amenities, highlights), then trust signals (reviews, host profile, Superhost badge), then the booking call-to-action (date picker, price breakdown, reserve button). This sequence is A/B-tested continuously. The term "Listing Detail Page" is used internally; Guests just call it "the listing."_

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Listing]] (the Listing whose details are shown), [[Entity - Photo]] (Photo gallery at top of page), [[Entity - Amenity]] (Amenities section), [[Entity - Review]] (Reviews section), [[Entity - Rating]] (aggregate star rating displayed prominently), [[Surface - Booking Flow]] (the flow initiated from the reserve button), [[Role - Host]] (profile displayed on this page)._
