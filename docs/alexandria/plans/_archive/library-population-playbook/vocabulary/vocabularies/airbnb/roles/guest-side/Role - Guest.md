# Guest

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the demand-side principal: the person who searches, books, and stays at an [[Entity - Listing]]. Guest is the primary user of all guest-side surfaces ([[Surface - Search]], [[Surface - Listing Detail Page]], [[Surface - Booking Flow]], [[Surface - Trips Dashboard]]). Airbnb reserves the word "Guest" for the transacting individual — the platform never uses "Traveler" or "Renter" in UI copy, even though those are common altLabels._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Reservation]] (the booking record a Guest holds), [[Entity - Trip]] (the felt-experience framing of a Guest's booking), [[Surface - Trips Dashboard]] (the Guest's primary post-booking surface), [[Role - Host]] (the counterpart role), [[Capability - Booking]] (the core Guest action)._
