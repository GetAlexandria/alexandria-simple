# Wishlist

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the [[Role - Guest]]'s saved-listings surface: a named collection where Guests bookmark [[Entity - Listing|Listings]] for later consideration. Wishlists are a felt-experience naming choice — Airbnb deliberately chose "Wishlist" over "Saved Listings" or "Favorites" to evoke the anticipatory desire of travel planning rather than the utilitarian action of archiving. A Guest can have multiple named Wishlists (e.g., "Summer trip ideas," "Anniversary options"), and Wishlists can be shared with others._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Listing]] (the Listings saved to a Wishlist), [[Role - Guest]] (the Guest who curates this Wishlist), [[Surface - Search]] (the surface from which Listings are saved to Wishlists), [[Role - Co-Traveler]] (Wishlists can be shared with Co-Travelers for collaborative trip planning)._
