# Amenity

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a declared feature of a [[Entity - Listing]] that a [[Role - Guest]] can filter for and expect to find on arrival. Amenities are Airbnb's standardized vocabulary for what a property offers: WiFi, kitchen, pool, parking, washer, air conditioning, pet-friendliness, and so on. Hosts declare Amenities during [[Capability - Listing Creation]]; Guests filter on them during [[Capability - Filtering]]. Amenity is the principal discoverable attribute that drives filter-based search._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Listing]] (Amenities are declared on the Listing), [[Entity - Property]] (Amenities describe what the Property physically has), [[Capability - Filtering]] (Amenities are the primary filter dimension in Guest search), [[Surface - Search]] (Guests filter by Amenity here), [[Surface - Listing Detail Page]] (where declared Amenities are displayed to Guests)._
