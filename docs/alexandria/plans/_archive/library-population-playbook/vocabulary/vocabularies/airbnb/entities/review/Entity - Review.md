# Review

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the post-stay written evaluation that constitutes Airbnb's trust economy currency. Reviews are mutual: a [[Role - Guest]] writes a Review of the [[Entity - Listing]] and [[Role - Host]], and the Host writes a Review of the Guest. Both parties write within a 14-day post-checkout window; neither sees the other's review until both have submitted or the window closes (double-blind system). Reviews are publicly visible on the Listing and on the Host and Guest profiles. The [[Pattern - Review Cycle]] describes the full mutual-review mechanics._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Rating]] (the numeric star rating component of a Review), [[Entity - Response]] (the Host's optional public reply to a Guest Review), [[Pattern - Review Cycle]] (the mutual-review pattern), [[Pattern - Two-Sided Lifecycle]] (Review is the final state in the lifecycle), [[Entity - Stay]] (the completed Stay that a Review is written about), [[Economy-instance - Star Rating]] (the Economy-layer representation of review scores)._
