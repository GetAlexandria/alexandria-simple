# Review Cycle

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the mutual, double-blind post-stay review mechanism: both [[Role - Guest]] and [[Role - Host]] submit [[Entity - Review|Reviews]] and [[Entity - Rating|Ratings]] within a 14-day window after checkout, and neither sees the other's submission until both have submitted or the window closes. The double-blind design reduces retaliation-review bias — if Hosts could see Guest reviews before writing their own, negative Host reviews of Guests would drop (and vice versa). The Review Cycle is the trust-building mechanism that makes Airbnb's marketplace function: Listings with more reviews rank higher ([[System - Search Ranking]]) and Guests with good Host reviews find it easier to book._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Review]] (the artifact the Review Cycle produces), [[Entity - Rating]] (the numeric component of Reviews), [[Entity - Response]] (the Host's post-publication reply to a Guest Review), [[Pattern - Two-Sided Lifecycle]] (Review Cycle is the final step in the lifecycle), [[Pattern - Superhost Qualification]] (review volume and rating are Superhost thresholds)._
