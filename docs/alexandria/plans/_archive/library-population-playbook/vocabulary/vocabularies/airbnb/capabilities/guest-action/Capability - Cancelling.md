# Cancelling

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the [[Role - Guest]]'s action to withdraw from a confirmed [[Entity - Reservation]], triggering the [[Pattern - Cancellation]] policy to determine [[Entity - Refund]] amount. Cancelling is accessed from the [[Surface - Trips Dashboard]] Trip card. The Refund the Guest receives depends on the cancellation policy tier (Flexible, Moderate, Strict) attached to the Listing at booking time and how many days before check-in the cancellation occurs. [[Role - Host|Hosts]] can also cancel — with more severe platform consequences (Superhost status impact, automatic Refund)._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Surface - Trips Dashboard]] (the surface from which Cancelling is initiated), [[Entity - Reservation]] (the Reservation being cancelled), [[Pattern - Cancellation]] (the policy governing this Capability's outcomes), [[Entity - Refund]] (the financial outcome of Cancelling)._
