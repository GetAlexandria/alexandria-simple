# Reviewing

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the mutual post-stay evaluation action available to both [[Role - Guest|Guests]] and [[Role - Host|Hosts]] after a completed [[Entity - Stay]]. A Guest submits a [[Entity - Review]] of the Listing and Host; the Host submits a Review of the Guest. Both parties have a 14-day window after checkout. Reviews are held until both are submitted or the window closes (double-blind), then published simultaneously. Reviewing is the closing action in the [[Pattern - Two-Sided Lifecycle]] and the primary mechanism of Airbnb's trust economy._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Review]] (the artifact this Capability produces), [[Entity - Rating]] (the numeric component submitted alongside the written Review), [[Pattern - Review Cycle]] (the mutual-review pattern this Capability implements), [[Pattern - Two-Sided Lifecycle]] (Reviewing is the final lifecycle step), [[Surface - Trips Dashboard]] (where Guests access Reviewing for past Trips)._
