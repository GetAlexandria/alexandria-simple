# Resolution

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the dispute-handling artifact: the formal record created when a [[Role - Guest]] or [[Role - Host]] submits a claim through Airbnb's Resolution Center. A Resolution is opened when the two parties cannot reach agreement on a financial or property dispute — typically a damage claim from the Host or a stay-quality claim from the Guest. [[Role - Customer Support]] reviews the Resolution, adjudicates, and can issue out-of-policy [[Entity - Refund|Refunds]] or charge additional [[Entity - Payment|Payments]]. Resolution is the terminal dispute artifact; it closes by mutual agreement or Customer Support decision._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Refund]] (the financial outcome Customer Support may issue), [[Entity - Payment]] (additional charges Customer Support may authorize against the Guest's payment method), [[Role - Customer Support]] (the adjudicating role), [[System - Trust and Safety]] (the system that routes and tracks Resolutions), [[System - AirCover]] (Host damage claims are processed through AirCover and Resolution together)._
