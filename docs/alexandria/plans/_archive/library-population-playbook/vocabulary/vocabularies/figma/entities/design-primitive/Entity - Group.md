# Group

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a lightweight container that collects multiple [[Entity - Layer]]s for joint movement and selection, without the structural constraints of a [[Entity - Frame]]. Groups do not clip content and do not support [[Capability - Auto Layout]]; they are the informal sibling of Frame — useful for temporary groupings and local organization within a Frame._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Frame]] (Frames are the structural alternative; Groups are informal), [[Entity - Layer]] (Groups contain Layers), [[Surface - Layers Panel]] (Groups appear as collapsible nodes in the layer hierarchy), [[Surface - Canvas]] (selection and movement of Groups happens on the Canvas)._
