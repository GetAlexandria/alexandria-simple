# Team

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the primary organizational container in Figma. A Team holds [[Entity - Project]]s, which hold [[Entity - File]]s. Membership is managed at the Team level; seat costs are billed at the Team level. Teams are the unit of [[Economy-instance - Plan]] subscription._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Project]] (Projects are contained within Teams), [[Role - Owner]] (the Owner created or was transferred the Team), [[Role - Admin]] (Admins manage Team membership), [[Economy-instance - Plan]] (the Plan tier is subscribed to by the Team), [[Entity - Library]] (Libraries are scoped to Teams)._
