# Surgery

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — Surgery is Conan's Job 7: producing six-phase fix plans for Sam after health checks or grading reveal quality problems. Surgery plans follow the Filtered Handoff Pattern — they include domain context, specific tasks, and acceptance criteria, but exclude grades, cascade analyses, and diagnostic framing. Surgery does not directly edit cards; it tells Sam exactly what to fix, in what order, with what success criteria. The full capability card is at [[Capability - Surgery]] in the existing library._
