# Component

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a Component is a named, composed piece of a larger structure — a sub-region within a Template that carries its own identity and quality criteria. Alexandria's Components include: WHAT Section, WHERE Section, WHY Section (regions within the Card Template), and Gap Manifest, Task Frame, Success Outcome (regions within the Context Briefing and Plan templates). A Component is distinct from a Primitive (atomic, no sub-parts) and from a System (mechanism, not structure). Note: the use of "Component" violates Alexandria's own signature preference for Anglo-Saxon monosyllables — flagged in the Nomenclature Signature as a known exception, tolerated because the gaming/engine sense of Component is unlikely to bleed into Alexandria's vocabulary._
