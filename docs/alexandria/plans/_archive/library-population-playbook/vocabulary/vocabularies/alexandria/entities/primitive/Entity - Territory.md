# Territory

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a Territory is one of Alexandria's four top-level library folders: `experience/`, `product/`, `rationale/`, and `temporal/`. Each Territory groups cards by the kind of claim they make, not by topic. `rationale/` holds why-claims (principles, standards, theses). `product/` holds what-it-is claims (agents, systems, capabilities, primitives). `experience/` holds how-it-feels claims (goals, loops, journeys, forces). `temporal/` is a staging area for time-indexed material awaiting absorption. The four Territories are the library's top-level ontology._
