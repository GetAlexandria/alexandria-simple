# Playbook

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the Playbook is a named tab surface in Alexandria — the strategy-stone tab in Alexandria's UI. It surfaces the play-execution layer: the Director's defined plays, hotkeys, and team coordination mechanisms. The Playbook is distinct from the Library (which is the knowledge graph) — the Playbook is the operational layer, the Library is the knowledge layer. Together they are two lenses on the same underlying product context._
