# Director

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the human product owner who directs Alexandria's agent team. The Director holds the intent, the product vision, and the judgment. Agents (Raven, Conan, Sam, Bridget, Solomon) execute on behalf of the Director, but the Director decides what gets built, what gets banked, and what gets changed. Alexandria's operator model collapses what other products split: the Director is both the day-to-day user and the strategic owner — there is no separate Admin / Member split because the library is personal to the product owner who runs it._
