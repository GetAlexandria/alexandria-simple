# Banking

## WHAT: Definition

Banking is the Director's commit gesture — the action of permanently adding a card, claim, or source to the library. To "bank" something is to declare it settled enough to live in the knowledge graph. Banking is the Director's primary production action: after Raven surfaces an insight, after Sam builds a card, after Solomon classifies a claim as settled, the Director banks. The term is borrowed from the finance metaphor running through Alexandria's vocabulary (Bank, Ledger, Coin): value is stored by banking it.

Banking is also the gerund-form capability in the Vocabulary framework. The directness of "Banking" over "Committing" or "Saving" is intentional — banking implies permanence and value-storage, not just file-persistence.
