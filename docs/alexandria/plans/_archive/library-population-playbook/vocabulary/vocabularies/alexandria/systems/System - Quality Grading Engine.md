# Quality Grading Engine

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the Quality Grading Engine is the scoring mechanism that converts five-dimension rubric assessments into letter grades, zone scores, and a system-wide score. Dimension scores (A-F per dimension) aggregate into card grades (weighted average), card grades aggregate into zone scores (with a completeness cap), and zone scores aggregate into the system score. Built bottom-up from observed quality failures, not top-down from documentation theory. The full system card is at [[System - Quality Grading Engine]] in the existing library._

## HOW: Specification

_Stub — the completeness cap: fewer than 25% of expected cards present means a maximum zone grade of D, regardless of individual card quality. This prevents a library with two excellent cards from scoring well on a zone that needs forty._
