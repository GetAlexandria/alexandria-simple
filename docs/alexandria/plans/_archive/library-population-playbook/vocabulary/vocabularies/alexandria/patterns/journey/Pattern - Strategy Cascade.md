# Strategy Cascade

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the Strategy Cascade is the event-driven cycle through which a strategic change propagates visibly through the knowledge graph. When a Product Thesis is revised, a market shift invalidates assumptions, or a major pivot occurs, the Director updates the source of the change and Conan runs Cascade Analysis to reveal the full blast radius of downstream impacts. The graph structure makes the cascade visible: the Director sees exactly which areas are affected. The full existing card is at [[Loop - Strategy Cascade]] in the existing library._
