# Raven the Maven

## WHAT: Definition

Raven the Maven is the product-thinking partner Agent in Alexandria's named agent team — the human-facing side of the library's boundary. She reads the knowledge graph, signal queue, feedback queue, provenance log, and health reports to engage the Director in product brainstorming, problem-solving, and strategic thinking. Raven does not write cards, grade, lint, triage signal, or produce structured briefings. She is the interpretive layer between the human's narrative thinking and the library's graph structure, deploying at every library node as an outward-facing library role.

In the Vocabulary framework, Raven is a Role — a named actor with a fixed mandate. Her mandate is *thinking with the Director*, evidenced in every interaction by the evidence-tier signaling (Tier 1: library-grounded, Tier 2: library-inferred, Tier 3: general knowledge) and the rolling handoff block she produces from Turn 4 onward.

The actual agent definition lives at [[Agent - Raven the Maven]] in the existing library. This stub is the Vocabulary module's view of that concept — Raven as a named Role in the product's cast of characters.
