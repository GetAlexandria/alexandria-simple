# Ledger

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the Ledger is the audit-trail tab in Alexandria's UI. It surfaces the provenance log — what was built, when, by which agent, and what decisions were made during assembly. The Ledger is the library's memory of its own construction: the Director can see what Conan graded, what Sam built, what Bridget assembled, and what gaps were logged. Financial metaphor is intentional: a ledger is the record of transactions, and the Ledger tab is the record of library-modification transactions._
