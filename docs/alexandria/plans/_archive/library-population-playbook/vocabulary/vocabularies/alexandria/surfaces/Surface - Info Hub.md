_Stub — the Info Hub is the microscope-stone tab in Alexandria's UI — the surface where modules unroll as kanban cards, and where the Director tracks what has been built, what is in progress, and what is planned. Previously called "Information Station" and "Kanban" in early designs; Info Hub is the settled name._
