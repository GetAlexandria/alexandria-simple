# Teamspace

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a second-level container within a [[Entity - Workspace]], grouping Pages and Databases for a team or department. A Teamspace has its own membership (a subset of Workspace Members), its own default permissions, and its own Sidebar section. Teamspaces are Notion's distinctive mid-level organizational unit — the container between the Workspace boundary and the individual Page._

_The noun is a single compressed word ("Teamspace," not "Team Space") — the one exception to Notion's two-plain-words compound rule, suggesting it was coined as a product-specific term to distinguish it from Linear's "Team" (a project-management scoping object) or Slack's "Workspace." The compression is intentional: Notion did not want "Space" as a standalone noun (too generic), and "Team" was already occupied by competitors in adjacent categories. "Teamspace" as one word signals a named, product-owned concept._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Workspace]] (Teamspaces live within a Workspace), [[Entity - Page]] (Teamspaces contain Pages), [[Role - Member]] (Members are added to Teamspaces), [[System - Permission Model]] (Teamspace membership determines default access to Pages within it), [[Surface - Sidebar]] (each Teamspace appears as a labeled section in the Sidebar)._
