# Comments

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the inline and page-level discussion surface. Comments can be left at two scopes: (1) Inline comments — attached to a specific [[Entity - Block]] or text selection within a Page, appearing as a highlighted annotation that opens a thread in the right margin; (2) Page comments — attached to the [[Entity - Page]] as a whole, appearing in a comment section at the bottom of the [[Surface - Page View]]. Comments support @[[Capability - Mentioning]] to notify specific [[Role - Member]]s and can be resolved to mark a discussion thread as closed._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Page]] (comments are attached to Pages or Blocks within Pages), [[Entity - Block]] (inline comments are scoped to Blocks), [[Capability - Mentioning]] (@ mentions within comments notify Members), [[Surface - Page View]] (the surface where comments are displayed and created)._
