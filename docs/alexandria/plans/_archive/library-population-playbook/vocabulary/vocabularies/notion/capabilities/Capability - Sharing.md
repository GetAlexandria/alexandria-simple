# Sharing

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — granting access to a [[Entity - Page]] subtree to a specific [[Role - Member]], [[Role - Guest]], or the public web. Sharing is the mechanism for: inviting a Guest to a Page, adding a Member to a Teamspace, changing the permission level of an existing collaborator on a Page, and publishing a Page as a public web page (accessible without a Notion account). Sharing permissions cascade to [[Entity - Sub-page]]s within the shared Page unless explicitly restricted — see [[System - Permission Model]]._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[System - Permission Model]] (Sharing is the action; the Permission Model governs the rules of what cascades), [[Role - Guest]] (Sharing with an external email creates a Guest), [[Role - Member]] (Sharing within the Workspace adds a Member to a Page or Teamspace), [[Entity - Page]] (Sharing is configured per Page), [[Entity - Teamspace]] (Teamspace-level sharing adds Members to the Teamspace)._
