# Plan

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a bundle of feature access, [[Economy-instance - Seat]] pricing, and usage limits purchased at the [[Entity - Workspace]] level. Notion's current Plan tiers are: **Free** (unlimited Blocks for personal use, limited Guests, limited history), **Plus** (unlimited Blocks for small teams, 30-day history, unlimited Guests), **Business** (SAML SSO, private Teamspaces, 90-day history, bulk PDF export), **Enterprise** (advanced security, unlimited history, audit log, SCIM, customer success). Plans are billed per [[Economy-instance - Seat]] on monthly or annual cadence._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Economy-instance - Seat]] (Plans are priced per Seat), [[Economy-instance - Block Limit]] (the Free Plan historically had Block limits; see Block Limit for lifecycle), [[Entity - Workspace]] (a Plan is subscribed at the Workspace level), [[Role - Workspace Owner]] (the Owner manages the Plan)._
