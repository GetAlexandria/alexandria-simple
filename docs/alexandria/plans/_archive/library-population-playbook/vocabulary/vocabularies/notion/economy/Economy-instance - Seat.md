# Seat

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a billable slot corresponding to one [[Role - Member]] in the [[Entity - Workspace]]. Each Member consumes one Seat; [[Role - Guest]]s do not consume Seats. The Seat count determines the monthly or annual charge for paid plans. Seats can be added on demand; the [[Economy-instance - Plan]] determines the per-Seat price and any included Seat minimums._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Role - Member]] (each Member consumes one Seat), [[Role - Guest]] (Guests do not consume Seats), [[Economy-instance - Plan]] (the Plan determines per-Seat pricing), [[Entity - Workspace]] (Seats are counted at the Workspace level)._
