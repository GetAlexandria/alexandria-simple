# Workspace Owner

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the account holder with billing rights and the ability to delete the Workspace. A Workspace may have multiple Owners, but one Owner is typically the original creator and billing contact. Owners can perform any action available to [[Role - Workspace Admin]], plus: manage billing and subscription, transfer or delete the Workspace._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Workspace]] (the tenant boundary this Role governs), [[Role - Workspace Admin]] (the delegatable subset of Owner rights), [[Economy-instance - Plan]] (the Owner manages tier and billing), [[System - Permission Model]] (Owner is the apex of the permission hierarchy)._
