# Guest

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a user with project-scoped access only. Guests cannot see Issues outside the Projects they have been explicitly granted access to, and do not consume a full Seat (or consume a reduced one, depending on the Plan). The Guest noun is the standard end-of-access-gradient Role in the B2B SaaS productivity family — useful for external collaborators such as contractors, customers, or partner-team reviewers who need a narrow window into the Workspace._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Role - Member]] (the full-access counterpart), [[Entity - Workspace]] (the boundary Guests cannot traverse fully), [[Economy-instance - Seat]] (Guests don't consume Seats, or consume partial Seats), [[Economy-instance - Plan]] (Guest availability varies by Plan tier)._
