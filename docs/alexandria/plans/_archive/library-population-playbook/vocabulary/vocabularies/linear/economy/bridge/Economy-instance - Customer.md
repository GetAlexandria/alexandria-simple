# Customer (as Economy unit)

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — cross-tenant collaboration packaged as a Plan feature. The Customer economy noun names the commercial capability: the Workspace's ability to receive and track feedback from people outside the tenant boundary via Customer Requests. This is the Bridge case from the families.md cross-tenant finding: cross-tenant collaboration is a sold feature, and "Customer" appears as a product noun precisely because a specific Plan feature enables it._

_This card has a sibling facet: [[Role - Customer]] describes who the Customer person is; this Economy card describes the commercial packaging — the feature bundle, Plan gate, and business model decision that makes the cross-tenant intake surface available. The Role card answers "who is a Customer?"; this card answers "what does selling the Customer capability mean commercially?" Cross-reference [[Role - Customer]] for the identity and access facets._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Role - Customer]] (the Role facet of the same concept), [[Economy-instance - Plan]] (the Plan tier that gates the Customer Requests feature), [[Entity - Workspace]] (the Workspace is what the Customer reaches into from outside), [[Role - Member]] (Members are the internal recipients of Customer submissions)._
