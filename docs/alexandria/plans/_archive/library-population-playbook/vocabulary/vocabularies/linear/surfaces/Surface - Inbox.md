# Inbox

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the per-Member pending-attention surface that lists notifications routed to that Member. The Inbox is named for the felt-experience encounter — the same word used in email and Slack — rather than for the underlying mechanism ([[System - Notification Routing]]). A Member visits their Inbox to see what requires action or awareness; the Inbox does not show all Workspace activity, only events routed to that specific Member._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[System - Notification Routing]] (the rules that determine what appears in the Inbox), [[Role - Member]] (each Member has their own Inbox), [[Surface - Activity Feed]] (the Workspace-wide event stream; the Inbox is the Member-scoped version), [[Capability - Commenting]] (mentions via Commenting are a primary Inbox routing trigger)._
