# Customer

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a person outside the Workspace who submits feedback or feature requests via Linear Customer Requests. A Customer interacts with the product's public-facing intake surface without being a Member or Guest inside the tenant._

_This is the Bridge case from the families.md cross-tenant finding: cross-tenant collaboration packaged as a sold feature. The noun "Customer" surfaces in the Linear product vocabulary precisely because the feature that enables it — Customer Requests — is a named, billable capability. Most users never encounter this Role; it exists at the Workspace boundary only when the team has purchased and enabled that surface. Compare [[Economy-instance - Customer]], which describes the commercial packaging; this Role card describes who the person is._

_Stub — links to: [[Economy-instance - Customer]] (the commercial packaging of this Role), [[Role - Member]] (the internal counterpart who receives and triages Customer submissions), [[Entity - Workspace]] (the boundary Customers touch from outside), [[Economy-instance - Plan]] (Customer Requests availability gated by Plan tier)._
