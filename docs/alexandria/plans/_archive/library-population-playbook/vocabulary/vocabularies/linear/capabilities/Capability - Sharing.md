# Sharing

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — granting access to an Issue, View, or Project to other Roles inside the Workspace, or to external [[Role - Customer]] (Bridge) Roles outside it. Sharing spans the internal-to-external boundary: within the Workspace it promotes visibility; across the Workspace boundary it becomes the Bridge case from the families.md cross-tenant finding, where cross-tenant collaboration is a sold feature. The two cases differ in consequence (internal Sharing is free; external Sharing may require a specific Plan tier)._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Role - Guest]] (Sharing with an external person often creates a Guest), [[Role - Customer]] (the Bridge role Sharing crosses the tenant boundary to reach), [[System - Permission Model]] (Sharing is a Capability constrained by the Permission Model), [[Economy-instance - Plan]] (external Sharing availability depends on Plan tier)._
