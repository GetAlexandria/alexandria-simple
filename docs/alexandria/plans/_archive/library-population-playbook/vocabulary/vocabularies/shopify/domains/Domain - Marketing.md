# Marketing

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the domain that owns discounts, abandoned-cart recovery, and email communications: the growth and retention layer of Shopify's commerce platform. The Marketing domain owns [[Economy-instance - Discount|Discounts]], [[Pattern - Abandoned Cart Recovery]], marketing automation, and the analytics surfaces that measure their effect. Shopify Email and Shopify Audiences are first-party tools in this domain; Klaviyo, Omnisend, and similar email/SMS platforms are the dominant third-party integrations._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Economy-instance - Discount]] (primary Marketing tool), [[Pattern - Abandoned Cart Recovery]] (primary retention tactic), [[Entity - Customer]] (the audience Marketing addresses), [[Entity - Cart]] (Cart behavior triggers Marketing flows), [[Domain - Catalog]] (Marketing promotes Catalog entities)._

## WHY: Rationale

_Stub — owner-supplied._

## WHEN: Timeline

_Stub._

## HOW: Specification

_Stub — to be enriched with: Shopify Email (first-party email marketing, integrated with Customer segments and Order data); Shopify Audiences (lookalike audience syndication to ad platforms); marketing automation triggers (Order placed, Abandoned checkout, Customer first purchase, Winback); UTM tracking and marketing attribution; Discount strategy patterns (launch discounts, loyalty rewards, win-back codes); the relationship between Marketing domain and Shopify Analytics._
