# PLP

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the Product Listing Page: the multi-[[Entity - Product]] display surface, filterable and sortable, typically scoped to a [[Entity - Collection]]. The PLP is where shoppers browse and discover Products before navigating to a [[Surface - PDP]]. Faceted filtering (by size, color, price, product type) is the primary PLP capability. PLP-to-PDP click-through rate is the primary conversion metric at this surface._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Collection]] (most PLPs are Collection-scoped), [[Entity - Product]] (the Products displayed in the grid), [[Surface - PDP]] (the downstream surface each product tile links to), [[Surface - Online Store]] (the channel this surface lives within)._
