# Cart Drawer

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the persistent in-page [[Entity - Cart]] access surface; a slide-out or overlay panel that displays Cart contents without navigating away from the current page. The Cart Drawer appears after [[Capability - Add to Cart]] and lets shoppers review their Cart, adjust quantities, and proceed to [[Surface - Checkout]] without losing their browsing context. Cart Drawer vs dedicated Cart page is a conversion-tested choice; Cart Drawer reduces friction for browsing flows._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Cart]] (the Cart this surface displays), [[Capability - Add to Cart]] (the action that triggers this surface to open), [[Surface - Checkout]] (the surface the "Check out" button in the Drawer leads to), [[Surface - Online Store]] (the channel this surface is part of)._
