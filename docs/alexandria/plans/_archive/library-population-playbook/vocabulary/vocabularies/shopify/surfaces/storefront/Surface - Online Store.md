# Online Store

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the customer-facing storefront; the primary sales channel for shopper-side interactions. The Online Store is the Shopify Channel that renders the [[Entity - Product]] catalog, [[Entity - Collection|Collections]], [[Surface - PDP|PDPs]], [[Surface - PLP|PLPs]], [[Surface - Cart Drawer]], and [[Surface - Checkout]] to [[Role - Customer|shoppers]]. It is powered by a Theme (Liquid-based template layer) and optionally extended with a headless frontend via the Storefront API._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Surface - PDP]] (the product page within this Channel), [[Surface - PLP]] (the collection/listing page), [[Surface - Cart Drawer]] (the Cart surface within the storefront), [[Surface - Checkout]] (the checkout funnel this Channel connects to), [[Pattern - Catalog Fanout]] (the Online Store is one Channel in the fanout)._
