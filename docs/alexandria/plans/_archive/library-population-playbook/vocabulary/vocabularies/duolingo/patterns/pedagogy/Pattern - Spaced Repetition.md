# Spaced Repetition

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the pedagogical Pattern of re-surfacing previously-learned material at expanding intervals calibrated to the Learner's demonstrated retention. Items the Learner has gotten wrong recently are re-surfaced sooner; items with a strong retention history are re-surfaced later. Spaced Repetition underlies the Practice Hub and the per-Skill "strength" indicators shown on some Course views. This is a borrowed pedagogical term from the learning science literature (Ebbinghaus forgetting curve, Leitner system), not an invented Duolingo noun._
