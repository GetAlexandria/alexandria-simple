# Streak Freeze

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the meta-resource whose only purpose is to protect a Streak. Purchased in the Shop with Gems before a missed day; automatically consumed by the Streak Engine when a day passes without qualifying activity. The Streak Freeze has no independent utility — it does not award XP, does not advance the Path, does not grant access to any feature. It exists only to intercept Streak loss._

_The Streak Freeze is the canonical example of the "Freeze meta-resource" pattern in families.md for gamified-life-OS products. Families.md defines it explicitly: "a meta-resource whose only purpose is to protect another resource (the Streak). Cited 48%+ retention lift at Duolingo." The mechanism is portable: any gamified-life-OS product (Hearthfire explicitly named in families.md) that has a Streak-equivalent should implement a Freeze-equivalent. The retention evidence is among the strongest in the category. The key design insight is that the Freeze does not reduce loss-aversion — it gives the loss-aversion response a productive outlet. Learners who fear losing their Streak now have a concrete action (buy a Freeze) that addresses the fear, which increases both Gem spend and Streak longevity._
