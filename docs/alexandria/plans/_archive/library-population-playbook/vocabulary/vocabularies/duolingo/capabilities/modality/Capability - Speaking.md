# Speaking

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — microphone-driven exercise type in which the Learner speaks target-language phrases aloud; Duolingo's speech recognition evaluates the production. Speaking exercises appear within Lessons and are the primary modality of Video Call with Lily (Duolingo Max). The Speaking Capability is particularly load-bearing in the Max tier: Video Call with Lily is sold on the promise of extended, open-ended spoken conversation practice that Lesson-embedded Speaking exercises cannot provide._
