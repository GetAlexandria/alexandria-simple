# Shop

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the surface where Gems are spent on consumables and power-ups. Primary items available in the Shop include Streak Freezes (purchased preventively before a missed day), Streak Repairs (purchased after a Streak breaks), and cosmetic items. The Shop is the Economy's primary sink — the point where accumulated Gems convert to durable or consumable benefits._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Economy-instance - Gems]] (the currency spent in the Shop), [[Economy-instance - Streak Freeze]] (the canonical Shop purchase), [[Economy-instance - Hearts]] (Hearts can be refilled via Gem spend in the Shop), [[Pattern - Streak Recovery]] (the Streak Freeze and Streak Repair items that power this Pattern are purchased here)._
