# Super

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the primary subscription tier. Super removes ads, grants unlimited Hearts, provides extra Streak Freezes, and unlocks the Practice Hub. Naming-history note: per the [[Standard - Duolingo Nomenclature Signature]]'s "subscriptions get power-word suffixes" rule — "Super" is the suffix, the product name is formed by attaching it to the brand ("Super Duolingo," shortened to "Super" in most product surfaces). The pattern is deliberate: brief, declarative, immediately legible. Formerly marketed as "Duolingo Plus" — the rename to "Super" was a signature application of the power-word convention._

_Super sits alongside Duolingo Max (the top tier, which adds GPT-4-powered features including Explain My Answer, Roleplay, and Video Call with Lily). The two-tier subscription structure follows the same power-word suffix rule: Super and Max are the two named tiers; Max signals the capability ceiling. No other suffix names are in use; any future tier would require a new power-word that fits this closed set._
