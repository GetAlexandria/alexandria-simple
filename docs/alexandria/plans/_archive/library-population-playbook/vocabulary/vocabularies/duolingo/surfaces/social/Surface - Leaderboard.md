# Leaderboard

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the weekly competitive ranking surface. Displays the Learner's XP rank among the ~30 Learners assigned to their League cohort for the current week. Resets every Sunday; Learners who finish in the top positions are promoted to the next League tier, Learners who finish in the bottom positions are relegated. This card covers the Surface (the screen); [[Pattern - Weekly League Cycle]] covers the competitive cycle Pattern; [[Economy-instance - League]] covers the tier as an Economy noun._

_Facet note: the Leaderboard sits at the intersection of Surface (a screen the Learner visits) and Pattern (the weekly competitive cycle the screen renders). Both facets are load-bearing — the surface is named, A/B-tested, and conversion-tracked independently of the pattern it hosts._
