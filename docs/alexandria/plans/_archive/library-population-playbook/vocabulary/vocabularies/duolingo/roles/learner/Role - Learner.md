# Learner

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the primary Role in the Duolingo product. The person completing Lessons, maintaining a Streak, accumulating XP, and progressing along the Path._

_Naming-history note: Duolingo uses second-person interface copy almost everywhere — "Your Streak," "Your Goal," "You've completed the Lesson." The product rarely names the Learner as a third-person noun; the underlying Role is inferred. "Learner" is the canonical name when a noun is required (e.g., in documentation, API design, or library cards). Duolingo reserves named third-person Roles for entertainment and social surfaces — Duo (the mascot), Lily (the Max character), Friend (the social graph noun). The Learner is the "You" that the product is calibrated to._
