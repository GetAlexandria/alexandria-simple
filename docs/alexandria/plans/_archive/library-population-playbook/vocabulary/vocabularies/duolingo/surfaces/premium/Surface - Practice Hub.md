# Practice Hub

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a Super-only review surface that surfaces previously-completed material for spaced-repetition practice. Learners who have not completed recent Lessons and whose prior vocabulary is weakening are directed here. The Practice Hub is a tier-gated capability surface: it exists in the product for all Learners but is accessible only to Super (and Duolingo Max) subscribers._
