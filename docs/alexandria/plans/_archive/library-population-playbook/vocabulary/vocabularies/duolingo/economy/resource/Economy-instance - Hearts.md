# Hearts

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — limited per-day attempts. A Learner starts each day with a full Heart count (5 on free tier); each wrong answer during a Lesson costs one Heart. When all Hearts are depleted, the Learner cannot continue new Lessons until Hearts regenerate over time or are refilled via Gem purchase. Super subscribers receive unlimited Hearts; the Heart cap is a key Conversion driver — Learners who exhaust their Hearts mid-learning-session encounter the subscription upsell directly._
