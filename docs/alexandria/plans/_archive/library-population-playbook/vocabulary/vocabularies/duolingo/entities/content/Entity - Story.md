# Story

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a short interactive dialogue designed for reading and listening practice. Stories are parallel to but distinct from Lessons: they present a narrative exchange between characters, ask comprehension questions, and award XP on completion. Unlike Lessons, Stories are not positioned on the main Path; they occupy a separate entry point (Stories tab or dedicated nodes)._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Entity - Course]] (Stories are Course-scoped, with different Stories per language), [[Capability - Listening]] (Stories are a primary Listening practice surface), [[Economy-instance - XP]] (Story completion awards XP), [[Entity - Path]] (Stories may appear as optional path nodes in some Course configurations)._
