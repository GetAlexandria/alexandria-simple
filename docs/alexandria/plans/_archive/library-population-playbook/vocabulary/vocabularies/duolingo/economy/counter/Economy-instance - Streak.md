# Streak

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the consecutive-day participation count. A Learner's Streak increments by 1 for each calendar day in which qualifying activity is completed; it resets to 0 if no qualifying activity occurs and no Streak Freeze is consumed._

_Triple facet — the Streak is the canonical noun in families.md that appears in all three categories simultaneously: Entity (a counter you can address, display, and act on), Economy (a resource users accumulate and actively protect via Gem spend and Streak Freezes), Pattern (the daily-return habit the entire product is organized around). Families.md names the Streak as "aesthetic-named loss-aversion mechanic" — the noun describes the felt experience of continuity (a streak of days), not the accounting mechanism underneath. Streak beats `habit_completion_count` by exactly the MDA-inversion test: a Learner who loses a 300-day Streak does not feel they have lost a `habit_completion_count` record; they feel they have broken a streak. The noun IS the loss-aversion hook, not a label for it._
