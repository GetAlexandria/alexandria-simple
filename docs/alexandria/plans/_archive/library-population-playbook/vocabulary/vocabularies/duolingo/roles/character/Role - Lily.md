# Lily

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a named in-product character who powers the Duolingo Max Video Call feature. Learners have open-ended spoken conversations with Lily to practice target-language speaking. She is distinct from Duo (the owl mascot) in that she is a named human-presenting character with a consistent visual design and a defined personality._

_Naming-history note: characters are first-class Roles in Duolingo's vocabulary — Duo, Lily, Zari, Vikram, and others are named, not "personas" or "avatars." The naming choice is consistent with the signature's entertainment-surface exception: branded character names are reserved for surfaces where the character IS the experience (Video Call with Lily, DuoRadio). On non-entertainment surfaces, the Learner is addressed in second person and no named character intermediates. This two-mode approach — You everywhere except where a named character owns the surface — is a deliberate product-vocabulary decision._
