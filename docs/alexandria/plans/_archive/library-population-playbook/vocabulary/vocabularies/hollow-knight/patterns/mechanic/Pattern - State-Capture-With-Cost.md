# State-Capture-With-Cost

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the pattern where a save action bundles checkpoint (progress is captured), rest (health and resources are restored), and risk-reset (enemies respawn). The three effects are inseparable — accepting the save means accepting the reset. This creates meaningful resource decisions: the player who has cleared a dangerous room must weigh "save here and lose my progress on clearing" against "keep going without saving and risk losing the forward progress." The pattern is named structurally; the aesthetic name in Hollow Knight is "Bench" (the player sits to rest; enemies return; the world resets around that rest)._

_Families.md names this pattern explicitly as the "State-Capture-with-Cost (Soulslike Bonfire)" pattern, citing Dark Souls' Bonfire as the canonical instance. Hollow Knight is the Metroidvania's version: the Bench is softer in aesthetic register than the Bonfire (rest vs survival), but identical in structural terms (save + restore + reset). The aesthetic difference is deliberate — Hollow Knight's tone is elegiac rather than hostile; sitting on a Bench feels like respite in a sad place, not survival at a desperate outpost. Same mechanic, different aesthetic clothing. This is exactly the MDA-from-aesthetics-inward approach: Team Cherry chose the felt experience of rest and designed a bench; FromSoftware chose the felt experience of desperate shelter and designed a bonfire. Both are State-Capture-With-Cost instances; neither is named for the state machine underneath._
