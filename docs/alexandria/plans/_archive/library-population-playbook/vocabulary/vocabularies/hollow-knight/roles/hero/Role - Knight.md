# Knight

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the player's avatar; the nameless Vessel who descends into Hallownest. "Knight" is a title, not a name — the character has no personal name because identity is a central arc of the story. The player controls the Knight through every surface in the World, and the Knight's capabilities (Dashing, Wall-Jumping, Healing, Casting Spells) expand as the game progresses via item acquisition._

_The deliberate namelessness is signature design, not an oversight. Hollow Knight's narrative turns on the question of whether the Vessel has a self — an identity, a will, a dream — and the title-as-name keeps that question open for the player's entire playthrough. Naming the character would resolve the thematic tension prematurely. This is the "inhabited metaphor" rule from the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]] applied to role design: the player does not play "Character001"; they play a Knight, a title anyone could hold, which makes the question of whether *this* Knight is more than a vessel into a genuine narrative question rather than a given._
