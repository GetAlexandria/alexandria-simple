# Geo

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the primary currency of Hallownest; the medium of exchange for purchasing items, Maps, Charms, and Charm Notches from merchants. Geo is earned by defeating enemies, breaking geo deposits in the environment, and opening Geo-containing chests. It is carried on the Knight's person and lost on death, dropped at the death location as a Shade — recoverable if the player reaches and kills the Shade._

_"Geo" is the canonical short-metaphor-name per the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]]. The word carries geological weight — mineral, extracted, ancient — suggesting that Geo is not minted money but recovered matter from a ruined civilization. Geo beats "currency," beats "coins," and absolutely beats "currency_balance" by the MDA-inversion test: when a player drops 800 Geo on death and sprints back across two rooms to recover their Shade, they are not watching a `currency_balance` field fluctuate — they feel the weight of what they have lost and the urgency of recovery. The name is part of that felt weight._
