# Mask

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the health economy; Masks are lost by taking enemy hits (sink), restored by Focus/Healing (source within combat, Soul-cost), and fully restored by resting at a Bench. The Mask economy has a permanent-expansion path via Mask Shard collection (four Shards = one permanent Mask added to maximum). Mask is the loss-aversion economy noun: the player who is at one Mask is in a qualitatively different strategic position than one who is at four — the number is small enough to be felt, not just counted. [[Entity - Mask]] is the object-level card._
