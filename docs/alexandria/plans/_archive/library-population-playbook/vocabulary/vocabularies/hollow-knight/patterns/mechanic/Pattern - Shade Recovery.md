# Shade Recovery

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the pattern governing Geo and Soul loss on death and their conditional recovery. When the Knight dies, all carried Geo and some accumulated Soul are dropped at the death location as a Shade — a dark, hostile echo of the Knight. The player respawns at the last Bench. To recover the dropped Geo and Soul, the player must navigate back to the death location and defeat the Shade in combat. If the Knight dies a second time before reaching the Shade, the Shade is destroyed and the Geo and Soul are permanently lost._

_This is Hollow Knight's adaptation of the Bloodstain pattern from Dark Souls (in Dark Souls: die, drop Souls; run back to the bloodstain; touch it to recover; die again before recovery and the Souls are lost). Hollow Knight's Shade has an additional design element that raises the stakes beyond passive retrieval: the Shade is an *enemy*, an active threat that must be defeated. This is a deliberate aesthetic escalation — the fallen Knight becomes a hostile shadow that the living Knight must fight. It is death-made-tangible; the consequence is not merely a resource floor but a confrontation with failure. The name "Shade" is the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]] applied to death mechanics: a shade is a ghost, a shadow self, the thing left behind when a being is diminished. This beats "death penalty token" or "recovery orb" by exactly the MDA-inversion test: the player who faces their Shade does not feel they are collecting a `death_recovery_item`; they feel haunted._
