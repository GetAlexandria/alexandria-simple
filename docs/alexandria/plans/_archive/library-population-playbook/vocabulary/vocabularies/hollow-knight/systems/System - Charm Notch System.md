# Charm Notch System

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the equipment-slot economy governing Charm loadout. Each Charm has a Notch cost (1–4 Notches); the Knight has a total Notch budget that starts at 3 and expands by collecting Charm Notches from Salubra and world locations. The player assembles a loadout within their current Notch budget. Equipping Charms beyond the Notch budget puts the Knight in the Overcharmed state — all damage taken is doubled. The Charm Notch System is the system; [[Entity - Charm]] and [[Economy-instance - Charm Notch]] are the user-facing nouns it governs._
