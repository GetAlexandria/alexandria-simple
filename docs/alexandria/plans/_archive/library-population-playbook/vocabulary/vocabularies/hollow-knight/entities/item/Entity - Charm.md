# Charm

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — an equippable passive-ability item; each Charm modifies the Knight's behavior, stats, or combat options while equipped. Charms are equipped and unequipped at a Bench. Equipping a Charm consumes one or more Notches from the Knight's limited total Notch count; the player assembles a Charm loadout within whatever total Notch budget they currently have._

_The name is a deliberate aesthetic choice: "Charm" invokes the folk-fantasy register of the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]] — a charm is an object imbued with power, worn or carried, a thing from hedge-witch tradition and hand-me-down magic. The alternative mechanical name would be something like "passive equipment module" or "loadout modifier" — naming from the system rather than the felt encounter. The Charm framing means players relate to their loadout as a collection of talismans, each with a flavor and a story, rather than as a set of stat-delta toggles. Individual Charms are named in the same folk-fantasy register: Quick Slash, Steady Body, Shaman Stone, Mark of Pride, Grubsong. None leak engine vocabulary; all are self-explanatory by feel if not by exact mechanic._
