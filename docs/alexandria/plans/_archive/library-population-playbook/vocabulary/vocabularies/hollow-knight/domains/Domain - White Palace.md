# White Palace

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the endgame surreal Domain; a gleaming, impossible palace accessible only through the Dream Nail by entering the mind of a dead Kingsmould. The White Palace is a pure platforming gauntlet — no enemies to fight, only a brutal sequence of saw-blade obstacles, moving platforms, and precision jumps. It exists in a dream-memory of the Pale King's seat of power. The name is the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]] at its starkest: White (the Pale King's color, purity, the Pale Light, emptiness) + Palace (grandeur, power, the seat of rule). The surreal quality — reached through a Dream, impossible in geometry — is signaled only by context and experience, not by the name itself._
