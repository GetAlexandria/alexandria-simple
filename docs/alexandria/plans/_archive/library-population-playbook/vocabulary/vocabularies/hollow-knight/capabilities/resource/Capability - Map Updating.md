# Map Updating

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the capability of automatically filling in the area Map with visited rooms; triggered by sitting at a Bench when the Knight has a Quill in inventory and a purchased area Map. Without the Quill, exploring an area does not update the Map sketch. Map Updating is a deliberate friction design: the player must invest in both the cartographic record (buy Cornifer's Map) and the updating instrument (buy Iselda's Quill) to have a fully informative Map. It rewards players who engage with the merchant layer and creates a satisfying information-completeness loop: explore → rest → see explored rooms filled in._
