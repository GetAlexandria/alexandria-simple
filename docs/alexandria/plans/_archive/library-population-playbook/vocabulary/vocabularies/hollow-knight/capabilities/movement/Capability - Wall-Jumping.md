# Wall-Jumping

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a mid-game vertical traversal capability granted by acquiring the Mantis Claw in the Mantis Village (adjacent to the Fungal Wastes). Wall-Jumping allows the Knight to cling to vertical surfaces and leap upward, enabling traversal of tall shafts and vertical passages previously impassable. It is the canonical demonstration of the [[Pattern - Retroactive Unlock]] — acquiring the Mantis Claw retroactively makes every tall vertical surface encountered since the game's start into a newly traversable passage. Players who have been accumulating mental notes of "that shaft I couldn't climb" experience a cascade of retroactive access when the Claw is found._

_This is the defining Metroidvania mechanic: the world does not change, but the player's capability does, and that capability reaches backward through time to unlock what was there all along. The Mantis Claw is named from its aesthetic (a claw like a mantis, gripping stone) rather than from its function ("WallClimbAbility" or "VerticalTraversalModule"). The name is the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]] applied to ability acquisition: the player acquires a mantis's claw, and through that acquisition earns a mantis's mobility._
