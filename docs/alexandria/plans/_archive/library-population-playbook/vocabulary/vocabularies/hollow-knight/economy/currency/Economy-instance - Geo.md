# Geo

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the primary currency economy; Geo enters the economy as enemy drops and world-object yields (geo deposits, chests), and exits the economy via merchant purchases (Maps, Quills, Charms, Charm Notches, Pale Ore). Geo is also the loss-recovery resource of the [[Pattern - Shade Recovery]] — the player who dies risks their Geo, and recovering it requires the added challenge of defeating the Shade at the death location. Geo is the closed-loop economy noun; [[Entity - Geo]] is the object-level card._
