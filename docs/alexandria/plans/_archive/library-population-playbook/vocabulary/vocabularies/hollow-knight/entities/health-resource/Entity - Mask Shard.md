# Mask Shard

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a fractional health upgrade. Collecting four Mask Shards permanently increases the Knight's maximum Mask count by one. Mask Shards are found throughout the World — purchased from merchants, awarded by bosses, hidden in obscure locations, and given by rescued Grubs via Grubfather. The Shard framing signals incompleteness: a Shard is a piece waiting to join three others; the player tracks accumulation toward the next whole Mask._
