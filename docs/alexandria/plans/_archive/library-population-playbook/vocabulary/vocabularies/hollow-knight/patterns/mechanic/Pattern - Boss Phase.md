# Boss Phase

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — multi-stage Boss encounters with escalating mechanics; a Boss transitions from Phase 1 to Phase 2 (or more) at a health threshold, introducing new attack patterns, movement behaviors, or arena transformations. In Hollow Knight, Phase transitions are common across major Bosses: the Hollow Knight transitions to a second form at low health; Hornet adds additional attacks at her second encounter; the Radiance has multiple distinct visual and mechanical phases. The Phase is named from the felt encounter: the boss changes when wounded enough; the player recognizes a transition by the visual and behavioral shift, not by reading a state variable._
