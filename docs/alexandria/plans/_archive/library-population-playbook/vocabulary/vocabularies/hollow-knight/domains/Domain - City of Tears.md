# City of Tears

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the mid-game urban Domain; a grand vertical city drenched in perpetual falling water, the former capital of Hallownest. The City of Tears is the game's tonal and visual centerpiece — its scale, the mournful sound design of rain on stone, and the architecture of a civilization at its height make it one of the most atmospheric areas in the game. The name follows the compound-evocative pattern of the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]]: City (scale, civilization, built-form) + of Tears (the perpetual rain, the mourning). It contains the Soul Sanctum (housing a major Boss), the Tower of Love, and the direct path to the Black Egg Temple._
