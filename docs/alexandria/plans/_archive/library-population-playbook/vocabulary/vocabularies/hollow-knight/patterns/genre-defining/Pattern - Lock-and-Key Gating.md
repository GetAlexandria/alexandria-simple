# Lock-and-Key Gating

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a World-access pattern where a specific item (the Key) in the player's inventory unlocks a specific blocked passage (the Lock), allowing progression. Unlike the [[Pattern - Retroactive Unlock]] (which grants a Capability that works broadly across the World), Lock-and-Key Gating requires a specific named item for a specific named door. In Hollow Knight: the Tram Pass token enables tram travel between specific areas; the Simple Key opens specific locked doors; the City Crest activates a specific gate into the City of Tears. The pattern creates deliberate exploration detours: the player must find the key before they can proceed, often requiring substantial backtracking._
