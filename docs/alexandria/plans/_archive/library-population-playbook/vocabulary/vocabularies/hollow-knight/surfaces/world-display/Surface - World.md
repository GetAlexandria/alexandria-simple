# World

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the playable 2D side-scroll space; the totality of Hallownest through which the Knight moves. The World is the primary surface of the game — all combat, traversal, exploration, NPC encounters, and Boss fights occur here. The World is non-linear: Domains (named areas) are connected by corridors and gates, each gate controlled by a gating mechanic (Lock-and-Key, ability requirement, or story trigger). The World is the spatial surface; the Map surface is its cartographic representation._
