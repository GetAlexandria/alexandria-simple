# Map

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the cartographic display surface; the screen the player opens to view their position in Hallownest and the shape of explored areas. The Map surface renders the [[Entity - Map]] — the cartographic record Cornifer drew and the Knight updated. The Map surface is a navigational affordance, not a gameplay space: the player cannot be harmed while viewing it, and time does not advance. Opening the Map is a deliberate pause to orient._
