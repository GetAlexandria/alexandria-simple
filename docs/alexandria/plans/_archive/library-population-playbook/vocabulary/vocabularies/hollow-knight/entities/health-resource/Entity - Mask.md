# Mask

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the unit of health. The Knight begins with four Masks; the maximum is nine, expanded by collecting Mask Shards (four Shards = one additional Mask). Taking an enemy hit removes one Mask; when all Masks are depleted the Knight dies. The Healing capability (Focus) restores one Mask at a time by spending Soul._

_"Mask" is also literal: the visual representation of the Knight's health on the HUD are small white shell-shaped icons — masks, the same kind the Knight wears. The name does double duty: it's both the health-unit noun and the visual element that makes health legible in the game's art register. This is the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]] at its most precise — the name is aesthetics-named (mask = the thing you see on the HUD), structurally truthful (mask = the Knight's own face-shell), and mechanism-silent (no reference to "HP," "hitpoints," or "health capacity")._
