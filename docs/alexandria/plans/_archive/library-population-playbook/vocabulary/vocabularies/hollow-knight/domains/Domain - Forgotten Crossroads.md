# Forgotten Crossroads

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the entry area and central hub of Hallownest; the first Domain the Knight explores after descending from Dirtmouth. The Forgotten Crossroads is the geographic center of the game's World — tunnels radiate outward to Greenpath, the Fungal Wastes, the City of Tears, and more. The name carries its narrative history: these were once the main thoroughfares of the kingdom, now abandoned and overgrown. Later in the game, the Crossroads transforms into the Infected Crossroads when the Dream narrative progresses, changing its visual and enemy composition._
