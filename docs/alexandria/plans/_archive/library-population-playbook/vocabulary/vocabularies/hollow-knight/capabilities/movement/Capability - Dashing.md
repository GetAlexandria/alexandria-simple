# Dashing

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a basic horizontal mobility capability granted by acquiring the Mothwing Cloak in Greenpath. The Dash allows the Knight to move quickly in a chosen direction, pass through enemy projectiles, and cross short gaps. It is the first major capability expansion in the typical playthrough and the first instance of the [[Pattern - Retroactive Unlock]] — earlier World areas with tight gaps or projectile-heavy corridors become easier or newly traversable after the Dash is acquired._

## WHERE: Ecosystem

_Stub — links to: [[Domain - Greenpath]] (where the Mothwing Cloak is found), [[Pattern - Retroactive Unlock]] (Dashing is a primary example), [[Capability - Wall-Jumping]] (Wall-Jumping is the next major mobility expansion), [[Entity - Charm]] (several Charms modify Dash behavior — Sharp Shadow, Dashmaster)._
