# Dialogue

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the NPC conversation surface; the text-panel display that appears when the Knight interacts with a named character in the World. Dialogue in Hollow Knight is monologue-forward: most characters speak at length; the Knight does not have voiced or selected responses. The player advances text by pressing the interaction button. Dialogue is the primary lore-delivery and narrative surface — world history, character motivations, and story context are almost exclusively delivered through NPC Dialogue rather than cutscenes._
