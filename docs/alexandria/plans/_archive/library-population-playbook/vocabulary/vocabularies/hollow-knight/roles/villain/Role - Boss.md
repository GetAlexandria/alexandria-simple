# Boss

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — a major-encounter antagonist; a named character (or pair) that occupies a dedicated arena, follows a multi-phase attack pattern, and must be defeated to progress the main quest or unlock areas. Every Boss in Hollow Knight has a proper name — Hornet, the Mantis Lords, Soul Master, Nosk, The Hollow Knight — never a category label. The Boss role is distinct from the Knight role in that the Boss is not player-controlled and has its own narrative identity._
