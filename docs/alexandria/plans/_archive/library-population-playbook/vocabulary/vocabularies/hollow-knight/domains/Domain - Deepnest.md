# Deepnest

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the late-game horror Domain; a lightless, labyrinthine nest deep below the other areas of Hallownest, inhabited by spider-like Beasts. Deepnest is deliberately disorienting — the area has minimal lighting, confusing non-Euclidean-feeling layouts, and enemy types that are faster and more aggressive than earlier areas. It is the game's tone-shift moment: from melancholy ruin to active dread. The name is blunt compound: Deep (depth, darkness, the below) + nest (infestation, organic architecture, not built but grown). Per the [[Standard - Hollow Knight Nomenclature Signature]], the name describes what happened here aesthetically, not what the player does here mechanically._
