# Charm Notch

## WHAT: Definition

_Stub — the loadout-slot resource; a Notch is the unit of Charm equip capacity. The Knight begins with 3 Notches and can acquire more by purchasing from Salubra and collecting from World locations, reaching a maximum of 11. Each Charm costs 1–4 Notches to equip; the player assembles a Charm loadout within their current Notch budget. Charm Notch is the economy-layer noun for the slot resource; it is a non-fungible, non-depleting capacity unit — unlike Geo or Soul, Charm Notches are not spent on use, only consumed as occupation (a Charm equipped occupies its Notch cost; unequipping frees those Notches)._
