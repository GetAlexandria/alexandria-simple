## WHAT
_Stub —_ the tag attached to a claim, decision, or section that says *who* established it and *when*. Three main classes seen in briefs: **Grounded** (from research), **Orchestrator call** (agent judgment, surfaced at next gate), **DIRECTOR DECISION / RULED** (Director ruling, dated).

## WHERE
End of brief sections (per the elicitation-review experiment). On decision queue items in workshop pages. In the README's "Rules adopted from the field" / "Rules inherited from the autopsy" sections.

## WHY
"Date every state claim and every amendment. The next agent trusts dated, attributed records and re-verifies undated ones — make everything the cheap kind."

## WHEN
Applied at every claim, every ruling, every section.

## HOW
- Three-class system: research-grounded / agent-call / human-ruling.
- Decision classification (gstack adoption): mechanical / taste / Director-challenge.
- Rulings preserved with RULED stamps (decision queue history kept).
