## WHAT
_Stub —_ the human role at the top of the Studio: owns goals, designs, and gates. Cannot catch agentic errors by reading code — process is built so he never has to.

## WHERE
Acts at every gate, every ruling, every confirm. Every checkpoint in [[Aggregate - The Loop]] emits an artifact for the Director to judge — briefs, interview reports, verdicts, dry-run transcripts, diagrams.

## WHY
A separation of concerns: design and judgment belong to a human; mechanical authoring and verification belong to agents. Earned because the factory era confused the two.

## WHEN
At every gate decision, ruling, and source-canon audit.

## HOW
- "Put him at design decisions; bring him rulings, not options-surveys."
- "One question at a time, formatted as a decision brief — stakes named, one marked recommendation, honest pros/cons."
- Rulings scribed into artifacts with date and provenance.
