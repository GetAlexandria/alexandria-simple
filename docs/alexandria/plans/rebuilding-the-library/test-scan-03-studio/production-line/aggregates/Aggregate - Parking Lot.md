## WHAT
_Stub —_ the playbook-wide / product-wide idea ledger: big ideas raised mid-design that span the whole playbook (not just one play) and are out of scope for the current golden path but too valuable to lose.

## WHERE
`studio/plays/PARKING-LOT.md`. Distinct from per-play [[Aggregate - Improvement Plan]] (§8 / improvements.md) — rule of thumb: *this play, grown* → §8; *the playbook or product, changed* → here.

## WHY
The source-canon audit (2026-06-12) parked six plays from the golden path; their full reasoning + revival criteria sit here. Without a single ledger, that history would scatter.

## WHEN
Updated whenever a session surfaces a playbook-wide idea or a Director ruling parks/revives something.

## HOW
- Each entry: the idea, why it was parked, what would earn it back.
- "Earned back when:" criteria are explicit (not "someday").
