## WHAT

No longer a tentative wager: this is the operating charter of the fork.
Work and the key decisions inside it live on a single visualized map the
director moves through — a map-first work surface. The director's ruling
after the Skillmaker pivot inverted the fork's founding priorities: the
game of Alexandria is building your company on the map, and the
playbook — once the intended "game" — takes the third-class seat the map
used to hold.

## WHY

The original wager asked whether a spatial view is something directors
actually reach for, or simply admire. It now has its first demonstrated
answer: the day the map and board landed, the director ran real company
work from them — placed a live system, read its health, and opened an
improvement project from the map itself. What was "cheap to undo" is now
load-bearing: the [[Entity - Work Board]] persists as the system of
record underneath, but as the map's source of truth, not as a fallback
to retreat to.

## WHERE

A product-level refraction of the corporate bet that the work environment
must be visual and traversible — it charters up to
[[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment]]. It is embodied by the
[[Surface - Work Map]], built and in daily use, with the
[[Mechanism - Canvas|canvas]] its nearest ancestor.

## HOW

The map holds the whole [[Entity - Work System]]: divisions as regions,
Projects and Systems as placed tiles, health and escalation visible from
orbit, and rooms behind every tile so seeing the work and acting on it
are the same surface — the standard set by
[[Research - The Map Is the Command Center|the map-as-command-center lesson]],
which this fork is now living rather than testing. The open reads that
remain are about depth, not direction:
[[Experiment - Finding Your Way on the Map|the map-finding probe]] still
watches where navigation breaks as the world fills, and the
[[Experiment - Spatial Surface Versus Plain List|spatial-versus-list probe]]
becomes a question about which lens fits which moment, not which one
survives.
