## WHAT

The wager: building and leveling up a colleague is a visible, legible progression the director can watch and shape, rather than a change to a configuration file.

## WHY

What this wager buys, if it pays off, is a director who feels they are raising a colleague rather than editing settings — growth they can watch unfold and choose to shape. What it costs if it doesn't is nearly nothing: the same progression can fold back into ordinary configuration, and the company learns that directors were content with numbers and switches all along. The open question the wager tests is whether watching capability grow matters to a director, or whether only the resulting capability does.

## WHERE

A product-level refraction of the corporate bet that the work environment must be visual and traversible — it charters up to [[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment]]. It is embodied by the progression a director watches as a colleague grows and takes on more capability — today most concretely the [[Entity - Knowledge Bank Area|knowledge bank areas]] a colleague's knowledge is worked toward.

## HOW

If the wager holds, a visible growth progression is what lets a director feel like they are building a colleague, not just adjusting settings. If it is wrong — directors don't care to watch a colleague level up, and plain configuration suffices — the progression view can revert to plain configuration at little cost.

The current diagnostic is [[Experiment - Isolating the Visual Environment's Draw|the visual-attribution experiment]], which already names visualized colleague growth as the product-level refraction it needs to isolate. [[Research - Attribution State of the Art|The attribution synthesis]] explains why this subtle visual claim needs its credit separated from the louder colleague wager rather than assumed.
