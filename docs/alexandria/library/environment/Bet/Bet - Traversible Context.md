## WHAT

The wager: context is navigated as a graph the director and colleagues move through together, rather than read as a set of documents.

## WHY

Success here means a director and their colleagues can walk through company context the way they'd walk a map, rather than hunting across separate documents for the same understanding. Failure is informative rather than costly: it would show that graph navigation asks more effort than it returns, and the graph can simply step back into a secondary, optional view while documents keep doing the primary work. Either way the product learns something concrete about how people actually want to hold shared context in their heads.

## WHERE

A product-level refraction of the corporate bet that the work environment must be visual and traversible — it charters up to [[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment]]. It is embodied by the graph view a director traverses, inside the [[Surface - Viewer|viewer]], to move through company context.

## HOW

If the wager holds, a traversible graph is what lets a director move through context the way they'd walk a map, rather than searching documents for what they need. If it is wrong — navigating a graph is harder than reading and searching documents — the graph can become a secondary, optional view at little cost.

The direct friction read is [[Experiment - Finding Your Way on the Map|the map-finding probe]], because traversible context fails first where people lose their footing. [[Research - The Mental Model Is the Hidden Circuit|The mental-model lesson]] grounds why visible connections matter before any product-specific graph-versus-document experiment exists.
