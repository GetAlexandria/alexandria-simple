## WHAT

The experience feels like a well-run operation — consistent and reliable across every colleague and surface.

## WHY

Inconsistency, flakiness, or amateurish seams between parts of the product make the whole experience feel improvised rather than run, no matter how sound any individual bet turns out to be. A director judges the operation by its worst recent seam, not its best one, so one flaky surface taxes the trust every other part of the product has earned. That tax is paid in full regardless of which wager is currently being proven out.

## WHERE

Governs the whole experience — how every colleague and every surface of the product holds together as one operation.

## HOW

A violation is inconsistency, flakiness, or amateurish seams showing between parts of the product that should feel like one operation.
