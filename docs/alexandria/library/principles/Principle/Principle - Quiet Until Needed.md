## WHAT

The system stays out of the director's way, surfacing itself only when attention is genuinely required.

## WHY

Nagging, interrupting, or demanding attention without cause trains a director to tune a product out, costing every future signal that might actually matter. Attention is a budget the director controls, not one the product is entitled to draw from on its own schedule. Once a director has learned to ignore the system, no later wager gets a fair hearing — the cost of speaking up too soon is paid in every quieter moment that follows.

## WHERE

Governs notifications, the coin and Tray signals, and any moment a colleague would interject — every place the product could ask for the director's attention.

## HOW

A violation is the product nagging, interrupting, or demanding attention without cause: a signal that fires when nothing needs the director, or a colleague that speaks up when it should have waited.
