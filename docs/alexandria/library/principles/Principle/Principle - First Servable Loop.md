## WHAT

A new director reaches a first real, useful loop quickly — value before full setup.

## WHY

Making a new director build out most of the system before getting anything back spends the trust that onboarding is supposed to earn before it has a chance to pay off. Protecting a fast path to a first genuine payoff means setup effort is never owed in full before value shows up. Every other wager in the product needs a director still around to render a verdict on it, and none of them can win back the ones who left before a first loop ever paid off.

## WHERE

Governs onboarding and the initial experience — everything between a director's first arrival and their first genuine payoff.

## HOW

A violation is a director having to build out a lot of the system before getting anything useful back.
