## WHAT

The library's structure stays navigable and understandable to a human, not only to machines.

## WHY

A structure only a machine can traverse strands the director outside their own library, unable to follow how anything in it relates to anything else — the one place they should be able to think out loud. A library that grows faster than a person can follow it has started optimizing for the wrong reader. That failure shows up long before any wager about colleagues or centralization gets tested, because a director who can't walk their own library stops trusting what it tells them.

## WHERE

Governs the library's views and structure — how its contents are organized and presented for a director to move through.

## HOW

A violation is the graph becoming a hairball only an agent can traverse, leaving the director unable to follow its structure.
