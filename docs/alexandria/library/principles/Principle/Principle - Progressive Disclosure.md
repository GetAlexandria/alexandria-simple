## WHAT

Complexity is revealed gradually; a day-one director is never shown more than they can handle.

## WHY

Meeting a new director with the product's full complexity before they have any footing leaves them unable to make sense of what they're looking at. Revealing that complexity gradually, matched to how far a director's experience has actually grown, is what keeps the interface, onboarding, and feature set usable rather than overwhelming. Pacing is a property of the on-ramp itself, not of whatever the product is ultimately betting on — get the sequencing wrong and a director never gets past day one, regardless of which wager was waiting for them on day thirty.

## WHERE

Governs the interface, onboarding, and how features are exposed as a director's experience with the product grows.

## HOW

A violation is a new director met with the product's full complexity up front, before they have any footing to make sense of it.
