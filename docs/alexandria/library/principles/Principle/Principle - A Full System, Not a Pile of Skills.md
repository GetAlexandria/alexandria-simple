## WHAT

Alexandria wins as a complete, coherent system — not as better prompts, a skills marketplace, or a set of point features.

## WHY

Fragmenting into isolated point features or a marketplace of prompts would leave nothing coherent for the market to adopt, no matter which wager about colleagues or centralization actually pays off. Positioning and architecture have to stay pointed at one system so framing and build never drift apart into a pile of disconnected capabilities. Coherence isn't something any wager could restore after the fact — it either holds at the architecture level from the start, or a scattered pile of parts stays scattered no matter which bet inside it succeeds.

## WHERE

Governs positioning and architecture — how the product is framed to the market and how it is built underneath that framing.

## HOW

A violation is the product framed or built as a collection of skills or prompts rather than as one coherent system.
