## WHAT

Every interaction adds durable value to company memory — work compounds rather than being redone.

## WHY

Knowledge that evaporates after an interaction forces every future conversation to start from zero, no matter how much value the atomization, ledger, or library work actually produced. Keeping effort durable across company memory is what lets contributions compound instead of repeat, so the same ground never has to be covered twice. A wager only has something to show for itself once its results are still there to look at later, so retention has to hold before any single bet's outcome is even legible, not after.

## WHERE

Governs atomization, the ledger, and the library — every place company knowledge is captured and carried forward.

## HOW

A violation is knowledge being lost or re-created — effort that should have accrued instead evaporates and has to be redone.
