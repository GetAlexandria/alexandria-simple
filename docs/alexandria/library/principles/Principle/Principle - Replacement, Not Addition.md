## WHAT

Alexandria must become the place work moves to, not a tool used alongside the others; winning requires switching, not stacking.

## WHY

Adopted as one more tool running alongside what a team already uses, the product never becomes the place work actually moves to, leaving every other bet with nothing to stand on. Positioning and go-to-market have to be built around switching rather than stacking, so adoption is measured against displacement, not addition. Whichever wager underneath turns out right, none of them cash in unless adoption actually moves past stacking — switching is the door all of them are betting on walking through.

## WHERE

Governs positioning and go-to-market — every decision about how the product is pitched, adopted, and measured against what a team already uses.

## HOW

A violation is Alexandria adopted as an add-on beside retail AI, kept running in parallel without displacing what came before it.
