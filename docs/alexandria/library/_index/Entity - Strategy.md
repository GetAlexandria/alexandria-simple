## WHAT

The strategy plane is Alexandria's living business plan: three wagers on how the company wins, and the principles it holds while making them. A bet is a falsifiable wager — what it would mean to be right, and what rolls back if it is wrong; a principle is a rule the company keeps regardless of how any bet turns out. We will know the strategy is working when [[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered]] climbs on evidence rather than conviction, and the readings underneath it can say which wager earned the movement.

## WHERE

Three corporate bets anchor the plane, each on its own shelf and each held here for now, marked to transfer to a future Company Library once one exists. [[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer|Colleagues]] as the interaction layer wagers that business adopts named AI colleagues over AI kept hidden and unnamed. [[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design|Centralization]] — colleagues grown from company design, the moat — wagers that a centralized, deliberately-built substrate produces better colleagues than bolted-together agents. A visual, traversible work [[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment|environment]] wagers that work is best lived on a map rather than through text. A fourth shelf of [[Entity - Principle|principles]] holds the rules of conduct and quality that cut across all three.

## HOW

Each corporate bet is the what; its product bets, nested one level beneath it, are the how. The coin, the control-panel tray, colleagues in the channel and in the meeting, and the play as the unit of ownership refract the interaction-layer wager into this product. The living source of truth, atomic agent-readable knowledge, the ledger loop and the shared playbook refract the moat. The map-first surface, the traversible graph, and the visualized processes refract the environment. The principles do not refract — they constrain, holding across every bet and governing the product's shape and every colleague's conduct no matter which wagers pay off. Evidence from the product then returns through the Learning plane, sharpening the product's bets and, in time, the company's. What a strategy plane is, and the job each of its contexts does, is defined in [[Entity - Strategy Plane]].
