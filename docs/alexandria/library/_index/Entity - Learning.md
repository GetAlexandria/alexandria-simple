## WHAT

The learning plane is the product's memory of what it has already figured out
and its standing plan for what it must figure out next. A strategy is a set of
wagers, and a wager is a question in disguise, so this plane is where those
questions are named, answered, and kept, and where the answers flow back to
sharpen the wagers that raised them. We will know the plane is working when
the product's golden metric — [[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered]] —
climbs on evidence rather than on hope: the fair-market value of the work
Alexandria's AI colleagues deliver, counted by how heavily they are actually
used and set against what they cost to run and to manage, rising while the
wagers underneath it carry real proof of why.

## WHY

The wager: a strategy is only as honest as the channel that can prove it
wrong. The wagers set down in [[Entity - Strategy]] each carry a confidence,
and the product keeps a learning plane so that nothing but evidence is allowed
to move it. Without this plane the bets would be steered on conviction, and
lessons already paid for in full would be paid for again; with it, every
question a wager hides is given a place to be answered and the answer a way
back to the bet that raised it — retiring the risks the wager named, moving
the confidence it carries, and sometimes saying plainly that the bet is wrong.
The golden metric is that proof made standing: the value of the colleagues'
work climbing while the evidence underneath it holds is what it looks like
when the loop is actually turning.

## WHERE

The plane sits at the turn of the company's central loop. The [[Entity - Strategy]]
plane holds the wagers, the product embodies them, using the product throws off
evidence, and that evidence returns here and then upward to strategy. Learning
is the one channel that runs back up the loop. What the product these wagers
build actually looks like is the [[Entity - Alexandria]] plane; this plane is
what tells us whether building it that way was right.

## HOW

The plane holds four shelves. The [[Research - The Evidence We Hold|research]]
shelf holds the evidence in hand — our three corpora and the company's own
banked learnings. The [[Experiment - The Tests We Run|experiments]] shelf
holds our designed tests. The [[Measure - What We Watch|measurement]] shelf
holds the golden metric and the per-wager measures that feed it. The
[[Arc - The Stories We Tell|arcs]] shelf holds our release stories. What each
of those shelves is and why the plane is cut this way is defined in
[[Entity - Learning Plane]].

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — the plane is being founded now, its first shelves
and exemplars laid down as its opening layer. What stands in the present is
that skeleton grown to its first flesh: the four shelves and their leads, this
keystone, the corpora and banked results already held, the measure family
defined but not yet reading, the first designed tests still ahead of their
runs, and the first two arcs — one in the air, one deliberately standing
down. What is intended next is that the record starts moving: the standing
measures instrumented against real use so the golden metric reads, each
wager's designed tests frozen and run, new stretches of the story declared as
arcs when they open, and the evidence each one earns returned upward — until
the plane is the living record the loop turns on.
