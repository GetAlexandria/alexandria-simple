## WHAT
One saved unit of canvas work — an artifact in progress, saved then
submitted for review.

## WHY

A saved, addressable unit of work-in-progress is what makes the wager on
a visual, traversible work environment concrete — a director needs
something durable to see and move through, not just a finished result —
[[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment]]. Saving the step before
review is requested is what gives that environment something real to
show.

## WHERE

The record of a saved canvas step.

## HOW
Steps are the saved contents of the [[Mechanism - Canvas]]; a saved step is
what a [[Capability - Canvas Review]] is requested on.
