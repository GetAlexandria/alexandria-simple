## WHAT

The wager: the library is the company's single, always-current source of truth — a living plan — replacing scattered, stale knowledge tools.

## WHY

This wager's payoff, if it holds, is a company where every colleague draws on the same real, current picture instead of scattered and quietly stale knowledge tools. If it fails, the company learns two things at once: that a single source of truth doesn't clearly beat letting knowledge live per-agent or in outside tools, and that keeping one library genuinely current was harder than it looked. Because so much of the system already reads from the library, unwinding that finding would be a foundational change, not a quick substitution.

## WHERE

A product-level refraction of the corporate bet that great colleagues are grown from a centralized company substrate — it charters up to [[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design]]. It is embodied by the [[Entity - Library|library]] itself — this product's own [[Entity - Alexandria Product Library|Alexandria Product Library]] is the first working instance — the place company knowledge lives, kept current by [[Pattern - Updating the Library|continuously updating the library]].

## HOW

If the wager holds, one living source of truth is what lets every colleague draw on real, current company knowledge rather than a stale or fragmented picture. If it is wrong — a single central source of truth doesn't clearly beat per-agent or external knowledge — the advantage this refraction claims disappears, though the effort to keep it current in practice is what would surface that first, and because so much of the system reads from it, unwinding it is a foundational change, not a small one.

The behavioral read is [[Measure - Switching and Consolidation Hours|switching and consolidation hours]]: old tools and per-agent knowledge should stop carrying work if the library is earning its place. [[Experiment - Ten-Director Library Pilot|The ten-director pilot]] is the first place outsiders have to build with that library, while [[Research - Working Memory Is Tiny|working-memory research]] and [[Research - The Substrate Is Four Things|the substrate lesson]] ground why a shared library is needed in the first place.
