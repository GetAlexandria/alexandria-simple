## WHAT

Ahead of the library-building pilot, put the same ten directors in front of a forced choice, not a survey: a named colleague they manage and grow, or AI quietly folded into tools they already use, and ask which they would pick and which they would actually pay for.

## WHY

This is the wager underneath everything else in the product, restated at its plainest and checked first. If people would rather have invisible AI features than a colleague of their own, the whole managed-colleague shape of the product is wrong, no matter how well the deeper pilot that follows performs. Running it ahead of that pilot means the coarsest, most existential question gets answered before the cohort's time is spent on the subtler question of whether a colleague grown from their own company actually earns continued use.

## WHERE

It tests [[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer]], and it directly informs [[Bet - Named Colleagues]] because the choice it puts in front of directors is a named managed colleague rather than AI hidden inside tools. It draws on the same ten directors as [[Experiment - Ten-Director Library Pilot]], asked before that pilot begins, so a rejection here would call the pilot itself into question before it starts.

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — the choice has not been put to anyone. In the present it is planned, riding ahead of the pilot it shares a cohort with; the prediction and the stop are drafted here but not yet frozen. What is intended next is that it runs once, over the same ten directors, before the pilot they also sit inside begins.

## HOW

The instrument is a forced choice, not a rating scale: each director is shown the two shapes side by side and asked to pick one and say what they would pay for it — revealed preference applied before any build has happened, rather than after. The read is binary and per-person, tallied across ten, with no formal success threshold, because this runs ahead of the real experiment rather than being one itself. Because it runs on the same independent, real directors the pilot will run on, a reading here carries piloted-strength weight on the narrower question of which shape they want, even though it settles nothing about whether either shape, once built, actually earns continued use. The same guardrail that protects the pilot protects this probe — these are real relationships, spent once, never twice.
