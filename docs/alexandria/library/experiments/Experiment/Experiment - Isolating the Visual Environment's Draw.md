## WHAT

Whether a visual, traversible working environment earns real adoption in its own right, or merely rides along on the colleagues who happen to live inside it, is the subtler of the product's two big wagers, and this experiment is the plan to answer it rather than assume it. Restated as the question it secretly is: if the environment itself were varied while the colleagues stayed the same, would people still show a preference driven by the environment, or would the reading collapse back into whichever bet is loudest and easiest to observe?

## WHY

The colleagues bet is coarse and self-evident — if people rejected AI colleagues outright, that failure would be loud and unmistakable. The environment bet is not that kind of question; it can be over-credited by good storytelling or quietly under-credited for lack of proof, and either mistake sends investment to the wrong place. Answering this honestly buys the company a real basis for how much further building and maintaining the visual, traversible surface deserves, instead of a decision made on which bet is more exciting to talk about.

## WHERE

It tests [[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment]] and its product-level refraction, colleague growth rendered visually. [[Research - Attribution State of the Art|A desk-research survey]] of how the applied sciences isolate one cause from several that move together — controlled experiments, coarser-grained randomization, quasi-experimental designs, constructed choice instruments, algorithmic credit-splitting, and self-report at the floor — already exists in the library, and whichever rung of that portfolio fits a real, available cohort is what this experiment's actual instrument will be drawn from once it is shaped.

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — no instrument has been chosen and nothing has run. In the present it is planned and deliberately held behind the nearer-term priority of proving the colleagues bet with real, independent directors; it is named and linked here so the intent is not lost, not because it is next in line. What is intended in the future is that once it is actually shaped, an instrument is chosen from the portfolio this experiment draws from, and the prediction and stopping rule freeze at that moment, the same way any other experiment's do.

## HOW

No instrument is committed yet. The portfolio it will be chosen from ranges from a true controlled experiment, if a comparable population can be split at random, down through constructed choice tasks that vary how visual the environment is without touching the live product, to credit-splitting methods that work on data a live, unsplit product already produces. Which rung gets picked determines how strong the resulting evidence lands — a constructed comparison run on people close to the company would only reach demonstrated strength, where a comparison run on real, independent, representative users would reach piloted strength — and that choice, along with the stopping rule that goes with it, is exactly what is deferred until this experiment is actually shaped rather than only named.
