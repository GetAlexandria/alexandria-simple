## WHAT

The knowledge band that holds a company's live wagers on how it wins,
and the standing rules it keeps no matter how any wager resolves. A
wager here is a falsifiable bet — what it would mean to be right, and
what rolls back if it is wrong — never a description of the system
built to embody it. In the library's causal loop this is the band
that proposes: Strategy stakes a wager that the Product plane
embodies and the Learning plane tests, feeding the answer back.

## WHY

A wager kept on its own band, apart from the system built to embody
it, is a claim that can be graded against evidence rather than a
description that quietly rewrites itself to match whatever shipped.
Giving reasoning its own address, separate from the product cards it
justifies, is what lets [[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge|a
wager stay atomic]] rather than buried as commentary inside the thing
it argues for — and lets a director always know which altitude they
are reading: intent here, the built system one plane down, proof one
plane over, [[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

Sits within a Domain, one of the three bands [[Entity - Plane]]
describes, below it and holding a set of [[Entity - Context|Contexts]].
Alexandria's is [[Entity - Strategy|the strategy keystone]]: the
company's own corporate bets and the principles held across them.

## HOW

A Strategy plane's contexts are cut one per lever of the business,
plus one for what binds them regardless of outcome. Alexandria's
four: [[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer|colleagues]] holds
the bets staking a claim on how the product's value reaches the people
who use it, the interaction layer;
[[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design|centralization]] holds the
bets staking a claim on what makes the system hard to copy, the moat
grown from the company's own design rather than assembled from parts
anyone could buy;
[[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment|environment]] holds the
bets staking a claim on the medium the work itself is done in, the
surface a colleague and a director share. The fourth,
the [[Entity - Principle|principle]] shelf, carries no wager of its own — it
holds the rules of conduct and quality that bind regardless of which of the
other three pay off.
