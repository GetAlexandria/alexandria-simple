## WHAT

One of the fourteen families-category buckets: a named stretch of the
product's story, intent going in and canon coming out. An arc joins a
wager, the product work that embodies it, and the evidence earned en
route into one release told atomically — not a schedule of dates and
dependencies, and not a vague vision either, but a plan minus its
execution. Arc lives on the Learning plane.

## WHY

A release earns its own card so a colleague can read what a stretch of
work was for, apart from the scattered tasks and evidence that carried
it — atomization applied to the story itself,
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]]. Naming the stretch as it
opens, rather than reconstructing it after the fact from whatever
survives, is what keeps a director able to walk one release's thread
end to end, [[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is a
named stretch of the product's story. Arc lives on the Learning plane,
where [[Arc - The Stories We Tell|the stories the product tells]] is
the shelf that carries this bucket's cards.

## HOW

Arc, one of the buckets in the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]],
differs from its nearest neighbor, [[Entity - Research]], by standing
on the intent side of a release rather than its settled result: an arc
is the wager and the work it embodies, joined before the story
finishes, while Research is the evidence the story leaves behind once
earned. An arc is also not the same thing as a roadmap: the roadmap is
a view drawn across several arcs, never a card of its own.
