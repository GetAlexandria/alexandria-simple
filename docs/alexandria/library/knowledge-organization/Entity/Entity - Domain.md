## WHAT

A Domain is a division, or business unit, of an organization — the level
between the Company and the knowledge bands. SocioTechnica's intended
shape is four divisions: Operations, Product, Sales & Marketing (with
New Media nested inside it), and Software Development. Product is the
only one formally stood up so far; the rest are named but not yet
formal.

Domain plays two roles that name the same thing from two altitudes. As an
organizer, it is the container level itself — the shelf a company's
divisions sit on. As one of the ten families-category buckets, it is also
the classification a card gets when its own subject is a division —
[[Domain - Playmaker's Studio Library]] carries `type: Domain` for exactly
that reason. The two readings describe one concept, not a naming
collision to resolve.

## WHY

Domain marks where one division's library ends and another's begins, so
that as the company grows, [[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]]
scales by federation instead of collapsing every division into one
undifferentiated graph. The level is named ahead of need: when a
director one day moves between divisions, the bearings are already
there, [[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The intended level of a company's library between the Company and the
Plane. Not yet enforced anywhere the library is read today — an intended
container, not a built one.

## HOW

A Domain sits below [[Entity - Company]] and above [[Entity - Plane]],
grouping a set of planes; a company's library is organized division by
division before it is organized band by band. The
[[Domain - Playmaker's Studio Library]] is a worked example: a sibling
Domain with its own product library, and also the concrete instance card
that carries this category as its Type.
