## WHAT

One of the eleven families-category buckets: an operation the product
performs — what a play does. Alexandria's worked examples are
[[Capability - Source Scan]] and [[Capability - Atomize]], verbs the
library-building process carries out. For an agentic product this is
meant to be the densest bucket — the system's real verb-map.

## WHY

A verb-map of what the product can do is the densest kind of card
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]] asks for: operations a
colleague reads and combines rather than infers from prose. The bucket
stays separate from the rules that gate those operations so that what
actually happens has a shelf of its own for the director to scan,
[[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is an
operation, not the rule that gates it.

## HOW

Capabilities is the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]] bucket that
differs from its nearest neighbor, [[Entity - Mechanisms]], by being
what a play *does* rather than the rule by which it happens:
[[Capability - Source Scan|Source Scan]] and
[[Capability - Atomize|Atomize]] are operations; the gates that get them
there are Mechanisms.
