## WHAT

One of the fourteen families-category buckets: results and external
research — evidence gathered, not the wager it tests. Research lives on
the Learning plane, where the
[[Research - The Evidence We Hold|research shelf]] holds the evidence
the company has gathered so far.

## WHY

Evidence earns its own cards so a colleague can weigh what was learned
apart from the wager it tested — atomization applied to learning itself,
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]]. Carding each piece of
evidence on its own keeps the taxonomy honest with the director about
what the company has actually learned, rather than leaving it buried in
prose, [[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is
gathered evidence. The Learning plane's research shelf carries it now.

## HOW

Research, one of the buckets in the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]],
differs from its nearest neighbor,
[[Entity - Rationale|the retired Rationale bucket]], by coming after
the fact rather than before it: the
wager Rationale used to bucket is staked in advance and now carded as a
Bet; Research is what a company learns once that wager has been tested
against reality. [[Pattern - The Approach|The Approach]] this area
takes to organizing knowledge is itself meant to be tested this way,
and the Learning plane now carries the Research that tests it.
