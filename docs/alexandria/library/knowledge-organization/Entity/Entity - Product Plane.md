## WHAT

The knowledge band that holds a company's built system — the working
knowledge and processes that make its wagers real, described as they
actually behave rather than as they were proposed. In the library's
causal loop this is the band that embodies: it takes each wager the
Strategy plane stakes and turns it into a system a colleague can read
and act on, throwing off the evidence the Learning plane then tests.

## WHY

A product card that also carried its own rationale and its own proof
would rot every time a wager's confidence moved or a measure ticked,
so a library keeps the built system on a band of its own, stable and
addressable apart from the argument for why it exists or the evidence
that it works. Holding the system this way is what lets
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge|the system stay atomic]] — a
colleague reading a product card gets exactly what is built, nothing
more — and lets a director keep their bearings: what shipped is read
here, why it was built is one plane up, whether it worked is one
plane over, [[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

Sits within a Domain, one of the three bands [[Entity - Plane]]
describes, below it and holding a set of [[Entity - Context|Contexts]].
Alexandria's is [[Entity - Alexandria]], the product keystone: the
shipped plugin, CLI, and viewer that embody the company's wagers.

## HOW

A Product plane's contexts are cut one per working system the library
must describe, plus one that keeps the description of all of them
legible. Alexandria's seven: [[library]] holds the company's knowledge
made atomic and accessible to a colleague, and [[playbook]] holds its
work processes made atomic the same way — the two the rest of the
plane exists to serve. Three contexts describe the systems that make
those two operate: [[viewer]] is the surface that makes the whole
system usable to a human director; [[ledger]] is the coordination and
QA record, the immutable account of what has happened;
[[Mechanism - Trigger|triggers]] activates and coordinates colleagues
from that same recorded truth.
[[Canvas]] holds work still in progress, held for its own review
before it joins the record. The seventh, [[knowledge-organization]] —
the context this card is filed under — holds the concepts that keep
the whole graph legible.
