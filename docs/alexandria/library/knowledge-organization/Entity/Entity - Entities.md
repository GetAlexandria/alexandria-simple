## WHAT

One of the eleven families-category buckets: a thing with its own identity
and lifecycle or state that moves through the process. Alexandria's
worked examples are [[Entity - Source]] (pending assessment → assessed)
and [[Entity - Thread]] (open → answered or residual) — cards whose
subject changes state as the library's work happens to it.

## WHY

A thing that carries its own state earns its own card because lifecycle
is exactly what a colleague must track card by card, not re-derive from
a document each time — the core trade of
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]]. Separating the thing from
the places it merely passes through spares the director from conflating
a noun with its container, [[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is a
thing, not a place, a rule, or an operation.

## HOW

Entities is the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]] bucket that differs
from its nearest neighbor, [[Entity - Surfaces]], by being the thing
itself rather than the place it sits: a [[Entity - Source|Source]] is a
thing that gets assessed and a [[Entity - Thread|Thread]] is a thing
that moves from open to answered, while an Inbox is only the place
either one sits while waiting.
