## WHAT

The knowledge band that holds a company's evidence — what it has
already learned and what it still must find out. In the library's
causal loop this is the band that tests and feeds back: it takes what
the Product plane throws off while embodying a wager and returns
proof, or its absence, to the Strategy plane that staked it.

## WHY

A wager is only as honest as the channel that can prove it wrong, so a
library keeps a band whose only job is evidence — nothing on it may
move a wager's confidence except a result. Without a dedicated home,
evidence scatters as commentary on the cards it bears on, or is lost
once whoever found it forgets it; given its own address instead, a
finding becomes [[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge|as atomic and
linkable as the wager it tests]], and a director following the loop
always knows which step they are standing on,
[[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

Sits within a Domain, one of the three bands [[Entity - Plane]]
describes, below it and holding a set of [[Entity - Context|Contexts]].
Alexandria's is [[Entity - Learning]], the learning keystone: the
plane that tells the company whether building the product the way it
did was right.

## HOW

A Learning plane's contexts are cut by the state its evidence is in —
held, sought, or flowing — plus one that narrates the story those
three tell together. Evidence already settled belongs on
[[Research - The Evidence We Hold|research]]:
a lesson distilled from prior work, a called test's result, or an
observation noticed but not yet resolved, filed as its own card rather
than left as a footnote on the wager it informs; [[Entity - Research]]
defines what a Research card is and how it differs from the wager it
grew out of. The standing plan of bounded tests still to come sits on
[[Experiment - The Tests We Run|experiments]]: a question that ends by
design, its prediction and its stop committed before it runs;
[[Entity - Experiment]] draws the
line between a test shaped like this and a quantity that simply reads.
A quantity that never resolves to a verdict and simply reports lives
on [[Measure - What We Watch|measurement]] instead — the distinction
[[Entity - Measure]] exists to draw. The fourth context,
[[Arc - The Stories We Tell|arcs]], holds no evidence of its
own: it holds the named stretches of the product's story, each
narrating the wager, the product work, and the evidence en route that
carry one stretch from intent to canon — a plan minus its execution,
never a schedule; [[Entity - Arc]] is where that shape is defined.
