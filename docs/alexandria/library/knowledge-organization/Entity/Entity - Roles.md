## WHAT

One of the eleven families-category buckets: the humans and agents who act
within the product. Alexandria's worked examples are
[[Role - Director]], the human who rules at every gate, and
[[Role - AI Colleague]], the class named agents like Raven and Damien
instantiate.

## WHY

Who acts gets a bucket apart from what is acted on, so an actor's
identity never tangles into the material it merely touches — a
separation [[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]] depends on when a
colleague composes across cards. For the director it means one stable
answer to who is responsible for a ruling or an action,
[[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is an
actor, not a thing the actor works on.

## HOW

Roles is the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]] bucket that differs
from its nearest neighbor, [[Entity - Entities]], by being who acts
rather than what is acted upon: the [[Role - Director|Director]] rules
on a [[Entity - Atomic Card|card]] that an
[[Role - AI Colleague|AI Colleague]] proposed; the card itself carries
no actor of its own.
