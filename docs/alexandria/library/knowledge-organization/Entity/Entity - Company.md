## WHAT

The outermost level of the containment hierarchy, above Domain — the
whole organization a set of divisions, and their libraries, belong to.
SocioTechnica is Alexandria's company. A Company's own library — the
federation of its divisions' libraries into one — is a planned, not yet
built, level: no Company Library exists today.

## WHY

Naming the Company level, even unbuilt, is what keeps a future
federation of divisions' atomized libraries from becoming several
incompatible graphs rather than one coherent whole a colleague can
still compose across,
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge|the wager on atomic knowledge]].
Fixing the outer boundary in advance holds the containment hierarchy
steady for the director once more than one division's library exists —
the outermost rung of the [[Principle - Legible Graph|legible graph]].

## WHERE

The intended top level of the whole containment hierarchy, above every
Domain. Not built or enforced anywhere the library reads today.

## HOW

A Company sits above [[Entity - Domain]] and groups a set of them —
SocioTechnica above Product, Software Development, and the rest. Until a
Company Library exists to hold it, corporate-altitude content stays
parked inside a division's own library, flagged for transfer:
[[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design]],
[[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer]], and
[[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment]] are the three worked
examples — corporate bets, held in Alexandria's Strategy plane only
because the Company level they belong to has nowhere else to live yet.
