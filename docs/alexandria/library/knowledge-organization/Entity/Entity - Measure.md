## WHAT

One of the fourteen families-category buckets: a standing, named
quantity that reads without end — what it counts, where it reads from,
and what it means. A measure reports continuously rather than starting
and stopping, and it never resolves to a verdict or carries an evidence
grade, because it has no stage to rate. Measure lives on the Learning
plane.

## WHY

A standing quantity earns its own card so a colleague can point at the
same defined number everywhere it is used — atomization applied to what
the company watches, [[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]]. A
quantity named once and shared, rather than folded into whichever card
needed it first, is what keeps the numbers a director steers by meaning
the same thing everywhere they appear, [[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is a
standing quantity. Measure lives on the Learning plane, where
[[Measure - What We Watch|the measures the product watches]] is the
shelf that carries this bucket's cards.

## HOW

Measure, one of the buckets in the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]],
differs from its nearest neighbor, [[Entity - Experiment]], by never
ending: an experiment is built to stop and resolve to a verdict, while
a measure simply reads, continuously, and never resolves to one. A
reading that turns out notable does not change a measure's nature — it
becomes a fresh [[Entity - Research|Research]] card instead, where a
stage and a verdict finally attach.
