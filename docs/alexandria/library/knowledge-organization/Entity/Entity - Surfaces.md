## WHAT

One of the eleven families-category buckets: a bounded place where material
lands or work is seen — not itself lifecycle-bearing. Alexandria's
worked examples are [[Surface - AX CLI]] and [[Surface - Viewer]], the
terminal and visual places the product is met.

## WHY

Where the product is met needs a fixed home of its own; what moves
across a venue changes constantly, the venue does not. Splitting the
two is how [[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]] keeps place-cards
stable while content churns, and how a director tells at a glance
whether they are reading a venue or the thing occupying it,
[[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is a
place, not the thing that moves through it.

## HOW

Surfaces is the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]] bucket that differs
from its nearest neighbor, [[Entity - Entities]], by being the place,
not the thing: the [[Surface - AX CLI|AX CLI]] and the
[[Surface - Viewer|Viewer]] are where work is seen; the cards they show
are Entities passing through them.
