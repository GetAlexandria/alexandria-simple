## WHAT

One of the fourteen families-category buckets: a falsifiable wager —
what it would mean to be right, and what rolls back if it is wrong. A
bet stakes a claim that evidence can move, carrying its own confidence
and the risks that would sink it. Bet lives on the Strategy plane.

## WHY

A wager earns its own card so a colleague can weigh what the company is
staking apart from whatever it eventually produces — atomization
applied to the company's own reasoning,
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]]. Naming the wager plainly,
with what would prove it wrong stated up front, is what keeps a
director able to tell a live bet from a settled fact at a glance,
[[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is a
falsifiable wager. Bet lives on the Strategy plane, where
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]] is a worked example: a bet
that itself carries this bucket as its Type.

## HOW

Bet, one of the buckets in the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]],
differs from its nearest neighbor, [[Entity - Principle]], by being
provisional rather than standing: a bet is a claim that evidence can
move, while a principle is a rule the company keeps regardless of how
any bet turns out. Both grew out of
[[Entity - Rationale|the retired Rationale bucket]], the single
catch-all a choice's justification used to land in before it split into
a wager staked in advance and a rule held no matter the outcome.
