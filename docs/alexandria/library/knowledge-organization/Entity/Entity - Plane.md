## WHAT

The three knowledge bands a library is organized into: Strategy, Product,
and Learning. A Plane groups a set of Contexts under one band.

## WHY

The three bands are the library's causal loop made structural: Strategy
proposes a wager, Product embodies it, Learning tests it and feeds both
— so the why of any product card sits one plane up, and the evidence one
plane over. That standing order is how
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge|atomized knowledge]] holds its
reasoning together, and how a director always knows which band they are
reading before descending into it, [[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

Sits within a Domain; every card in the library carries the Plane its
context belongs to.

## HOW

A Plane sits below [[Entity - Domain]] and above [[Entity - Context]]:
it groups a set of contexts under one knowledge band, and a Domain groups a
set of planes. Each band carries a distinct job in the library's causal
loop: [[Entity - Strategy Plane|the strategy band]] proposes a company's
wagers, [[Entity - Product Plane|the product band]] embodies them as a
working system, and [[Entity - Learning Plane|the learning band]] tests
them and feeds the answer back to the wagers that raised them. Each is
carded as its own specialization of this concept, naming that band's own
contexts and the job each is designed to do.
