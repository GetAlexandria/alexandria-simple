## WHAT

One of the eleven families-category buckets: a named recurring arc across
the product — not a one-time build, an ongoing motion. Alexandria's two
worked examples are its own core recurring patterns,
[[Pattern - Running Plays]] and [[Pattern - Updating the Library]].

## WHY

The recurring arc is knowledge the individual steps don't carry:
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]] pays off only if the whole
motion is itself readable and reusable, not just its gates. Held at its
own altitude, the shape of the product's work stays visible to the
director instead of dissolving into scattered pieces,
[[Principle - Legible Graph]].

## WHERE

The bucket a card's [[Entity - Type]] carries when its subject is a
whole recurring arc, not a single gate inside it.

## HOW

Patterns is the [[Entity - Atomic Card Category]] bucket that differs
from its nearest neighbor, [[Entity - Mechanisms]], by spanning a whole
multi-step arc rather than one gate: [[Pattern - Updating the Library]]
is the continuous arc of new material arriving and being worked toward
filed knowledge, and [[Pattern - Running Plays]] is the continuous arc
of agents picking up and running the work in the Playbook — each a whole
recurring motion, not a single gate inside one.
