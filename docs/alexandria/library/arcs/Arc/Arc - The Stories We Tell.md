## WHAT

The arcs shelf holds the named stretches of the product's story — intent going
in, canon coming out. What an arc is, and how it differs from a schedule or a
roadmap, is defined in [[Entity - Arc]].

## WHY

The product's two arcs keep each release's story as one legible thread — the
current build's intent, the work it is spending, and the evidence it earns as
it goes — rather than letting that story dissolve into scattered tasks or get
smuggled into evidence cards that then stop being evidence. Standing an arc
down keeps its banked result addressable without pretending the story is
still moving.

## WHERE

Arcs sit beside the three evidence shelves and reach across them, and beyond
them: an arc's heart is cross-plane, joining a wager from strategy, the
product work that embodies it, and the evidence en route here in learning.
Membership is a label a card wears on itself, never a list kept here — any
card in any plane can declare the arc it belongs to and whether it headlines
that arc or rides in support, so the same card can headline one stretch of
story and merely appear in another.

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — the shelf opens with its lead and the two arcs the
product's present actually holds. In the present one arc is in the air,
carrying the current build, and one is deliberately downshifting with its
yield already banked. What is intended next is that new stretches of the
story are declared as arcs when they open, and that the roadmap view is drawn
across them once enough exist to draw one.

## HOW

An arc card tells its story in one shape: a wager from strategy, embodied by
product work, proven by evidence en route — written as links a reader can
walk, with the arc's members narrated in prose rather than kept as a list.
Successive flights of one thesis stay inside one arc and are told apart by the
strength of the evidence each can earn, so a story that starts on ourselves
and later runs on real directors is one arc climbing the evidence ladder, not
two.
[[Arc - Onboard Raven → Get Good Work]] is the arc in the air: a director powers up Raven by building their own product library and gets good work out of the colleague that produces — dogfooding on ourselves now, ten real directors next, the plays that unlock after that.
[[Arc - The Colleague in the Meeting]] is the arc standing down: it put a colleague into a live meeting seat, banked the feasibility result that settled whether one can, and now waits behind the library work until whether people want one becomes the urgent question.
