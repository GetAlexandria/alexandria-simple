## WHAT

The plan is that a director powers up Raven by building out their own product
library — documenting their product, and in time their strategy and their
go-to-market — and gets good work out of the colleague that produces, unlocking
useful plays along the way. The arc runs as successive flights of that one
thesis rather than as separate efforts: first the company builds its own
library and proves the process tractable on itself, then directors who are not
us build theirs the same way with no plays unlocked yet, then the process is
made into product, then the plays that unlock are built and tested.
We will know the arc delivered when directors who are not us build a library, keep using the Raven it powers unprompted, and would pay for it — the reading that doubles as [[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered]]'s first measurement on people who did not build the product.

## WHY

A colleague grown out of a company's own documented design carries more
fidelity than a generic pile of skills or a standalone agent built apart from
the company it serves. Documenting the company is what onboards the colleague:
each part of the business written down progressively unlocks capability, the
way employment and immersion onboard a person. What that mechanism is being
staked on is the payoff in the arc's name — that a director actually gets good
work out of the colleague the build produces, enough to keep using it and pay
for it.
The claim is read against an absolute usefulness bar — do directors build, keep coming back, and commit money — not against a side-by-side comparison; that comparison is its own separate probe, [[Experiment - Grown Colleague Versus Off-the-Shelf Agent]], and is not part of this arc.

## WHERE

The wager this arc exists to move is that
[[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design|colleagues grow from company design]].
The wager that
[[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer|colleagues are the interaction layer]]
at all rides along as necessary context — if people rejected AI colleagues
outright nothing downstream would matter — but this arc's cohort of
near-solopreneur directors cannot cleanly test that wager's sharpest claim,
so the arc does not pretend to prove it.

The arc runs inside the product's library-building flow — the onboarding that
grows Raven from what a director documents — named here in prose until that
flow carries a card of its own. What each flight leaves behind, once called,
lands on the research shelf.

## WHEN

Nothing has shipped into canon yet: N/A — no flight of this arc has been
called. In the present the first flight is in the air: the company is building
its own product library with the same process it intends to hand to directors,
earning demonstrated-strength evidence on the feasibility question underneath
everything else — whether a coherent library is tractable to build and
maintain at all — with no plays unlocked yet. What is intended next is the
ten-director flight at piloted strength, then making the process into product,
then building and testing the plays that unlock; each successive flight climbs
one rung of the evidence ladder rather than opening a new arc.

## HOW

The mechanism is the company's central loop, restated for one stretch of story:
a wager from strategy, embodied by the product, proven by evidence the plane is
still earning.
[[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design]] is embodied by the library-building flow that grows Raven from a director's documented company, and is proven by the evidence en route through [[Experiment - Ten-Director Library Pilot]] — the arc's headline test, the one the arc exists for, where ten directors who are not us build real libraries and the readings are behavioral: whether they build, whether they still use Raven unprompted a month later, what fair-market value their colleague delivered, and whether they would pay.
[[Experiment - Named Colleague Versus Invisible AI]] rides in support on the same cohort, asked once just ahead of the pilot, so the coarsest question — whether people want a named colleague of their own at all — is answered before the arc spends the cohort's time on its own subtler one.
[[Research - Build Beats Read]] is the held lesson the whole thesis stands on: you come to own a world by constructing it, so a director who builds the library owns the colleague it powers in a way no handed-over agent can match.
The arc's flights are sliced by evidence strength, not by subject: the current
dogfooding flight can only ever earn demonstrated-strength evidence because
its sample is us, and the pilot flight is where the same thesis earns its
piloted reading on people who did not build the product.
