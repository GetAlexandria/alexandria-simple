## WHAT

The plan was to put a named colleague into live meetings: a real seat in the
room, held in audio and video conversation as it happens, alongside the rest
of the team. The arc built and showed that colleague working, and is now
downshifting — the library-building work underneath every colleague turned out
to need far more than expected, and this stretch of story stands behind it
until that foundation is laid. No standing metric attaches to this arc; the
proof it went after was the capability itself, and that proof is banked.

## WHY

Presence is the strongest form a colleague can take. A colleague that holds a
seat while the conversation is happening — not a message read later, not a
summary after the fact — is the interaction layer reaching all the way into
the company's real-time work, which is where the most consequential
coordination actually happens. If a colleague can hold that seat, then the
wager that colleagues are how a business works through its AI stops being
bounded by the inbox; if it cannot, the interaction layer stops at the door of
every meeting room.

## WHERE

The wager this arc exists to move is that a
[[Bet - Colleague in the Meeting|colleague appears and speaks in live meetings]],
itself one reach of the broader wager that
[[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer|colleagues are the interaction layer]].

The evidence the arc banked is held on the research shelf, where it stays good
regardless of the arc's own pace.

## WHEN

What happened is that the colleague was built and shown speaking in a live
meeting, settling the feasibility question the arc opened on. What stands in
the present is a deliberate downshift: the library work every colleague rests
on needs more than was planned for, and winding this arc down costs nothing
that matters, because its result is already held. What is intended next is
that the arc resumes when the open question it left behind — whether people
actually want a colleague speaking live in their meetings, not whether one
can — becomes the urgent question to answer.

## HOW

The mechanism runs the same loop as every arc: a wager from strategy, embodied
by the product, proven by evidence.
[[Bet - Colleague in the Meeting]] was embodied by the talking colleague that was actually built and shown holding a live seat, and is proven as far as this arc carried it by [[Research - A Colleague Can Hold a Live Meeting Seat]] — the arc's one member and its whole yield so far, a do-level demonstration on ourselves that retired the wager's feasibility risk while leaving its desirability risk untouched.
That single banked result is why downshifting is safe: the arc's story so far
is one settled answer and one question deliberately left standing, and both
survive the pause intact.
