## WHAT

The second built-in agent — the Executive Producer of New Media persona.
Coined but not truly built out: he has a coin and a few off-playbook
skills with stories captured in the playbook. An instance of the class,
not a separate pillar.

## WHY

Damien exists to demonstrate that the colleague roster is a class, not
a single assistant wearing different hats — the wager that each
colleague is a named individual with its own persistent identity, role,
and record, [[Bet - Named Colleagues]]. A second rostered persona,
however lightly built out, is the evidence that the roster generalizes
beyond the one flagship colleague.

## WHERE

The built-in agent roster's second entry, and his shipped persona
definition.

## HOW

Damien is a rostered instance of [[Role - AI Colleague]] with his own coin,
responsibilities, and skills — evidence that agents are a class, not a
single assistant.
