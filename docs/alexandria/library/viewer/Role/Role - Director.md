## WHAT

The human running the show, that all AI colleagues report to — the leader
of a small team (4–16 people) responsible for top and bottom line,
managing agents from the visual interface. A pillar of the product: the
whole gate model hangs on this actor — every ruling in the timeline is the
director's.

## WHY

The director is who the whole colleague roster exists to serve — the
wager that business works with named colleagues as its primary way of
interacting with AI, rather than AI hidden inside its tools,
[[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer]]. Every gate, every ruling,
and every colleague's behavior is built around fitting this one
person's rhythm, judgment, and authority over the business.

## WHERE

Named as "directors (our users)" in the banked product description;
appears in the record wherever a gate suspends for a ruling.

## HOW

The director rules at every gate: answering the [[Mechanism - Human Gate]]
when a run suspends and ruling on the whole draft library at the
[[Mechanism - Confirmation Gate]]. Every [[Entity - AI Colleague]] works
to fit this person's rhythm and culture.
