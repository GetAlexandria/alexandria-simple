## WHAT

The default agent — the product-owner colleague the director works with
most, and the one somewhat built-out example of an AI colleague. An
instance of the class, not a separate pillar.

## WHY

Raven exists to prove out the wager that a colleague should be met as a
named individual with a persistent identity, not an interchangeable
capability — [[Bet - Named Colleagues]]. Her voice carries that wager in
practice: collegial and warm rather than transactional
([[Principle - Conversational Warmth]]), and professional rather than
gimmicky or over-cute ([[Principle - Professional, Not Daffy]]).

## WHERE

The built-in agent roster's default entry; met through its coin and its
own family of viewer routes.

## HOW

Raven is the flagship instance of [[Role - AI Colleague]]: it drafts and
mediates the [[Entity - Basic Product Description]] slot by slot and
relays the director's reactions to waiting runs.
