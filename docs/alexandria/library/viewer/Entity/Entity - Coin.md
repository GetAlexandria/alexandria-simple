## WHAT

The "magical" coin that fronts an AI colleague — a name and personality
rendered as a coin the director clicks to assign work. It lives inside
the Tray, one coin per AI colleague. The coin is the payoff of the
whole system — everything else exists to make it work.

## WHY

The wager the coin carries is that a colleague should be met as an
abstract token — [[Bet - The Coin as Abstract Token]] — legible as a
working part of the team without borrowing the uncanny weight of a
simulated person. It is the face of the larger wager that business works
with named colleagues rather than AI hidden inside its tools,
[[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer]]; the warmth belongs in how
the colleague speaks — [[Principle - Conversational Warmth]],
[[Principle - Professional, Not Daffy]] — not in a rendered face.

## WHERE

The agent coin artwork and each colleague's own page, rendered in the
viewer's Tray.

## HOW

It is a rendering derived from the [[Role - AI Colleague]] it fronts, shown
inside the [[Surface - Tray]], and serves as the navigation hub to that
colleague's hotplays, playbook, knowledge bank, and agent page; clicking it
is one way a play is requested.
