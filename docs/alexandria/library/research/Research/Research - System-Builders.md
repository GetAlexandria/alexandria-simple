## WHAT

This corpus is the study of a particular kind of software: the city-and-civilization
builder, where an enormous, tangled system of interacting parts becomes something an
ordinary person can hold in their head and steer, because they built it themselves, one
placed thing at a time. Two lessons out of the corpus are load-bearing for the product
today — that a person owns and understands a world by constructing it rather than
reading about it, and that the world they build becomes its own command center, a living
thing they act on directly rather than a diagram they consult from outside.

## WHY

Holding this evidence buys the product its clearest precedent for staking onboarding and
its work surface on construction rather than instruction. This genre of software has
already solved, at real scale and for a long time, the exact problem the product needs
solved: how to make an enormous, interdependent system legible to one person without
requiring that person to already understand it going in. Without this corpus in hand, the
case for a construction-first onboarding and a living, map-like work surface would rest on
intuition alone rather than on a proven line of prior software.

## WHERE

Two lessons drawn from this corpus carry evidence into the strategy: that
[[Research - Build Beats Read|build beats read]], and that
[[Research - The Map Is the Command Center|the map is the command center]].
Both trace to the same body of prior study and both bear on the same pair of company
wagers: that the work environment must be visual and traversible, and that a director
builds real fidelity into their colleague by constructing their own company's picture of
it rather than being handed a template.

## WHEN

This card's past is N/A — no earlier version of this reading exists to supersede, so it
opens at its founding layer, even though the software genre it studies is old and the
internal notes drawing lessons from it predate this shelf. What stands in the present is
the corpus lead with its two confirmed lessons in place. What is intended next is that the
further lessons this corpus surfaced — how a system stays legible as its scope grows, and
how starting from a small seed makes a large world memorable — get their own reading
before they join the shelf as cards of their own.

## HOW

A body of research opens, is drawn from, and reopens as new material is read against it,
which is why this lead sits at the aggregate grain rather than being ranked among its own
members — it organizes them by containment, not by altitude. Each lesson beneath it is
graded on the same basis: a synthesis read out of shipped software and the internal notes
written about it, not yet tested against how directors actually behave inside this
product. The evidence stands at the reported stage until the product's own
construction-first onboarding gives it a demonstrated or piloted reading of its own.
