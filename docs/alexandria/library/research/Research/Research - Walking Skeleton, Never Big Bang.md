## WHAT

No high-reliability domain built its foundations in one complete pass before it started
operating. Every one of them grew a thin, working version of the whole system first, then
let real use reveal which foundation was still missing, then added exactly that one before
moving on. Foundations-first does not mean building every foundation before anything else
runs — it means never running anything atop a foundation that has not been laid, while
laying only as much of the foundation as the next real piece of work actually demands.

## WHY

This lesson matters because it resolves a real tension in how the product's own substrate
is being built: taking foundations seriously does not require finishing them before
anything useful can run. A thin, working slice run end to end is what actually surfaces
which foundation is missing next, in a way that planning every foundation in the abstract,
ahead of any real use, cannot. It is permission to build brick by brick without treating
that as a compromise on the seriousness of the foundations-first commitment.

## WHERE

This lesson grounds the way the product's substrate is actually being assembled,
brick by brick rather than in one complete pass, in service of the same wager that
a centralized, deliberately designed substrate is what
[[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design|colleagues are grown from]].

## WHEN

This card's past is N/A — it opens here at its founding layer, drawing on how ten
independent fields each arrived at their foundations. What stands now is a
demonstrated-grade reading, because the product's own substrate has genuinely been built
this way — a thin, working version extended one missing piece at a time — not merely
described this way from other fields' example. What is intended next is watching whether
this same walking-skeleton discipline continues to hold as the substrate grows well past
what today's brick-by-brick build has covered so far.

## HOW

The evidence is a cross-domain synthesis of how ten fields built their foundations,
strengthened here to a demonstrated reading because the product's own build has followed
the same discipline on itself — a constructed instrument, built and observed by the team
making the claim, which is why it stands short of a piloted reading rather than beyond it.
What it does not yet establish is whether this discipline holds equally well once the
substrate is being extended by people other than the team that has been building it brick
by brick from the start.
