## WHAT

The research shelf holds the evidence the product already has in hand — the
settled learning, past by nature, that a colleague can pick up and use. Three
sorts of thing live here: the lessons a body of prior work taught, distilled
into something usable rather than left as a bibliography; the results a called
experiment or a crossed measurement threshold produced; and the open
observations that have been noticed but not yet resolved into either a lesson or
a new wager.

## WHY

What fills this shelf is not arbitrary: each corpus was chosen because it
converges on a choice the product's own architecture had already made, and
the company's own banked results sit beside them on the same terms. What a
research shelf is for, and why a library keeps one at all, is defined in
[[Entity - Research]].

## WHERE

The shelf's members group under the wager they serve, since learning is the
channel that runs back up to the bets a director stakes the company on. The
[[Research - Attribution State of the Art|synthesis of how the applied sciences attribute a cause to an outcome]]
is the shelf's one piece of grounding not yet tied to a corpus of its own.

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — the shelf opened with its lead laid down and its
first held evidence promoted into place. In the present it holds three
corpora, each with the lessons judged load-bearing, and the company's own
internal learnings. What is intended next is that the lessons each corpus
surfaced but did not yet confirm get their own reading, and that the shelf
keeps growing as later work throws off evidence of its own.

## HOW

Order comes from three tools, not a fourth folder level. Altitude ranks the
cards: a corpus lead sits at the aggregate of its own lessons, since research
opens, runs, and reopens as new layers land, while each lesson or case sits at
the value grain, where meaning is carried by content. Containment links tie a
corpus lead to the lessons beneath it.

The deepest corpus is [[Research - High-Reliability Systems]], the field guide
to how organizations that cannot afford to fail run high-tempo work, read
across ten domains as one convergent body rather than ten separate case
studies. Six lessons hang beneath it.
[[Research - Build the Envelope, Not the Capability]] is the frame the other
five sit inside: the scarce resource is not what a colleague can do but what a
director can absorb, so the discipline layer beneath the colleagues comes
before more capability. [[Research - The Substrate Is Four Things]] names the
floor every domain converged on — a trusted record, a shared vocabulary, roles
held apart from people, and a grammar for time — the same four-part shape the
product's own foundations already embody.
[[Research - Walking Skeleton, Never Big Bang]] is how that floor actually
gets laid: a thin, working whole first, then exactly the foundation the next
real piece of work demands, the way the product's own substrate has in fact
been built. [[Research - A Rulebook Turns Operator into Legislator]] is the
case for playbook depth as safety infrastructure, because a decision made in
advance lets whoever is in the seat execute calmly rather than improvise under
pressure. [[Research - Roles Are Separable from People]] is why a colleague is
a persistent, named role rather than interchangeable capability, the way a
watch station outlives everyone who has ever stood it. And
[[Research - Attention Is Sacred]] closes the corpus with its warning: what
deserves a director's notice must be deliberately engineered, because the
alert that matters is exactly the one lost when everything alerts.

Beside it, [[Research - System-Builders]] studies the city-and-civilization
genre of software, where an enormous, tangled system of interacting parts
becomes something one ordinary person can hold in their head and steer. Its
two lessons carry the product's construction-first case:
[[Research - Build Beats Read]], that a person comes to own and remember a
world by building it from a seed, piece by piece, in a way no handed-over,
finished map achieves; and [[Research - The Map Is the Command Center]], that
once an operation is rendered as a living map, the map stops being a picture
of the work and becomes the place the work happens.

The third corpus, [[Research - Tools for Thought]], holds the tradition of
tools built to extend what a mind can hold rather than automate what it does,
and supplies the reason the builder genre's patterns work. Four lessons carry
it: [[Research - The Iconic Stage Is Required]], that understanding passes
through an image-based stage a text-only interface forces a mind to skip;
[[Research - Working Memory Is Tiny]], that a head holds only a handful of
patterns at once, so everything past that handful needs somewhere else to
live; [[Research - Represent Knowledge with Models, Not Symbols]], that
knowledge held as structure a person can move through spares every reader
rebuilding the relationships from prose alone; and
[[Research - The Mental Model Is the Hidden Circuit]], that what makes a
library amplify thinking is the connections between its cards, never the
cards read alone.

Beside the corpora sit the learnings the company has banked about itself.
[[Research - A Colleague Can Hold a Live Meeting Seat]] is a result: a
colleague was built and shown speaking live in a real meeting, retiring the
feasibility question while leaving desirability untouched.
[[Research - How We Chose the Library's Structure]] records that the library's
organizing method was earned by trying alternatives against real content and
watching where they broke, and
[[Research - Is the Library Organization Method Working]] is the open
observation that keeps the winning method a hypothesis under watch rather than
a fact assumed. One piece of grounding stands apart from any corpus:
[[Research - Attribution State of the Art]], the applied sciences' ladder of
instruments for crediting an outcome to its cause, held for the day rising
adoption must be credited to the right wager.
