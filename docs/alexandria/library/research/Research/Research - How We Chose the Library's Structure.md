## WHAT

Before settling on the library's current organizing method, several different ways of
structuring the library were actually tried and dogfooded on the company's own knowledge,
not merely discussed in the abstract. The method that won crosses a structural grain — how
finely a piece of knowledge is cut, from a whole system down to a single component — with
a taxonomy of product-noun categories, treated as two separate, orthogonal ways of
classifying a card rather than one contested list trying to do both jobs at once.

## WHY

This result matters because it is evidence that the library's organizing method was earned
by trying alternatives against real content and watching where they broke, not chosen on
first instinct or by analogy alone. Landing on two independent axes, rather than one list
straining to carry both a structural and a categorical meaning, is what makes the resulting
library legible to a person and composable for a colleague at the same time — and that
result came from testing the alternative, not from assuming it up front.

## WHERE

This result is what settled the current structure of the wager that knowledge is
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge|atomized into typed, linked cards]] a
colleague reads and composes better than prose.

It also stands as concrete evidence for the broader wager that great
[[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design|colleagues are grown from a deliberately designed company substrate]],
since choosing the substrate's own organizing method well is part of what that
design has to get right.

## WHEN

What happened is that the company tried more than one way of organizing its own library
against real content, before settling on crossing structural grain with a product-noun
taxonomy as two separate axes. What stands now is that this method is the one in active
use, governing how the library is actually built today. What is intended next is that
whether the method keeps holding as the library grows far larger than it is today becomes
its own open question, rather than being assumed settled by this result alone.

## HOW

The evidence is behavioral, not a stated preference: alternative structures were actually
built and used against real company knowledge, and the one that held up is the one now in
use, which is do-level evidence rather than say-level. It was demonstrated on the team's
own library rather than on an independent, representative body of content unconnected to
the people who chose it, so it stands at the demonstrated stage rather than the piloted
one — a strong reading of what worked for us, not yet proof that the same method holds for
a library someone else builds.
