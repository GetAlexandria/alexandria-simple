## WHAT

Whether the library's organizing method — crossing a structural grain with a product-noun
taxonomy as two separate axes — is actually the right method remains open. It has been
chosen and is in active use, but the question of whether it holds up as the library grows
far past its current size, and whether it genuinely serves a colleague composing from it
as well as it serves a person reading it, has not yet been answered either way.

## WHY

This observation matters because the organizing method itself is a wager, not a settled
fact, and the card that names the method says as much about itself — that what is learned
about whether it actually works belongs here, on the learning plane, once there was a
place for that evidence to live. Naming the open question explicitly is what keeps the
method honest: it stays a hypothesis under test rather than something quietly treated as
proven simply because it is the one currently in use.

## WHERE

This observation nominates
[[Pattern - The Approach|the product's own organizing method]] — the pattern that
governs how every other card in the library is structured — as a standing question
the learning plane is responsible for eventually answering.

It follows directly from
[[Research - How We Chose the Library's Structure|the result that settled which method to try]]
in the first place, and stays open until enough use of the library at real scale
answers it one way or the other.

## WHEN

This card's past is N/A — no earlier version of this observation exists, so it opens here
the moment the method it questions was named as something the learning plane should watch
rather than assume. What stands now is an open question with no verdict either way — not a
lesson, not a result, just a nomination for future evidence to resolve. What is intended
next is that this observation matures into a settled lesson once enough use of the library
at real scale, by people other than the ones who chose the method, gives a clear answer.

## HOW

There is no evidence yet beyond the fact that the question has been named — this card
marks an open observation, not a tested claim, and carries no verdict because none has been
reached. What would resolve it is watching whether the library, as it grows well past its
current size and is used by directors who did not choose its method, stays legible to a
person and composable for a colleague, or instead strains and needs to be revisited.
