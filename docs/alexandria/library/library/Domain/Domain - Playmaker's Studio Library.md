## WHAT

The federation pointer — the Playmaker's Studio is a separate,
supporting product that will be spun out, and it keeps its own product
library because companies with multiple products should have separate
product libraries for each. The pointer is correct as a reference to a
library that federates by design, not by product machinery. The
federation vision: multiple libraries managed by business units that
link to each other, creating a library of libraries — planned, not
shipped.

## WHY

Marking the Playmaker's Studio as its own library, even while it lives
inside the same repository, keeps its knowledge from being mistaken for
Alexandria's own. That separation is what makes an eventual spin-out
clean: the Studio's library leaves with the Studio, with nothing to
untangle on either side.

## WHERE

The sibling bounded library kept by the Playmaker's Studio.

## HOW

It links the product's [[Entity - Alexandria Product Library]] out to
the federated PMS library — one leg of the three-way "library" carve
the director ratified. The Playmaker's Studio is a sibling
[[Entity - Domain]] — a separate business unit with its own
product library, where Alexandria's plays are made.
