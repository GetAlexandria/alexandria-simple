## WHAT

The federation pointer — Skillmaker.Studio (renamed from Playmaker's
Studio) is a separate product that keeps its own product library,
because companies with multiple products should have separate product
libraries for each. Since the pivot it is no longer merely a supporting
product to be spun out: Skillmaker.Studio is the product SocioTechnica
intends to commercialize, while Alexandria stays the company's own
working environment. The pointer is correct as a reference to a library
that federates by design, not by product machinery. The federation
vision: multiple libraries managed by business units that link to each
other, creating a library of libraries — planned, not shipped.

## WHY

Marking Skillmaker.Studio as its own library, even while it lives
inside the same repository, keeps its knowledge from being mistaken for
Alexandria's own. That separation is what makes an eventual spin-out
clean: the Studio's library leaves with the Studio, with nothing to
untangle on either side.

## WHERE

The sibling bounded library kept by Skillmaker.Studio; its division is
one of the five regions on the company's work map, planned for William.

## HOW

It links the product's [[Entity - Alexandria Product Library]] out to
the federated Studio library — one leg of the three-way "library" carve
the director ratified. Skillmaker.Studio is a sibling
[[Entity - Domain]] — a separate business unit with its own
product library, where Alexandria's plays are made.
