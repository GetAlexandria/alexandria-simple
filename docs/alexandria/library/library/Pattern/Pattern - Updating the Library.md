## WHAT

The continuous addition of new, interrelated information into the
library — the other of the product's two core recurring patterns.
New material keeps arriving, keeps getting worked toward finished
knowledge, and keeps landing as cards that connect to what is already
there. Like its sibling pattern, it is human, agentic, and software
work together: a director hands over material, an agent works it
toward filed knowledge, and the library grows a little with every
pass.

## WHY

The pattern is what makes good on the promise that the library is never a
finished artifact but a living source of truth,
[[Bet - Library as Living Source of Truth]]. It is also the concrete
shape the ledger's feedback loop takes on the ground — recorded facts
flowing back in as new material rather than sitting unused,
[[Bet - Kept Live by the Ledger Loop]] — so work compounds instead of
needing a fresh pass each time ([[Principle - Cumulative, Not Sisyphean]]).

## WHERE

Wherever new material enters the company and is worked toward the
library — an ongoing motion, not a one-time build.

## HOW

It relates to each finished [[Entity - Atomic Card]] the library gains
and to the [[Capability - Source Conversion]] process that works
handed-over material toward the state new cards are drafted from.
