## WHAT

A subject band of company knowledge worked toward the bank — real, live
gamification machinery for Raven, not parked prototyping. Today's five
shipped areas are vision, vocabulary, bets, guardrails, and user research,
each prerequisite-gated on vision. Lifecycle-bearing: available, then in
progress, then ready for atomization, then banked (plus a locked state).
This specific five-area shape is itself transitional and is expected
to be redesigned around the product's plane build-out stages.

## WHY

The area exists so a director can watch a colleague's knowledge actually
grow, subject by subject, rather than reading it off a settings screen —
[[Bet - Visualized Colleague Growth]]. Each area also feeds the library
that same knowledge once it banks, which is the concrete way a colleague's
growth becomes company knowledge that stays current rather than living
only in the colleague's own head,
[[Bet - Library as Living Source of Truth]].

## WHERE

Surfaced at the viewer's knowledge bank view.

## HOW

When an area reaches ready for atomization, card production runs against it
and it produces [[Entity - Atomic Card]] files in the library — the visible
leveling-up progression that refracts
[[Bet - Visualized Colleague Growth]] on the Strategy plane.
