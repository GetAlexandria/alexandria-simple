## WHAT

Break a confirmed library into finished card bodies — plan the work,
then draft, validate, grade, and publish each card.

## WHY

Breaking confirmed material into finished cards rather than leaving it as
narrative is the whole practice of the wager that atomized, linked
knowledge is what a colleague can actually compose with,
[[Bet - Atomic, Agent-Readable Knowledge]]. Drafting, validating,
grading, and publishing each card in turn is how that wager gets tested
one piece of knowledge at a time rather than asserted once for the whole
library.

## WHERE

Wherever confirmed material is worked, card by card, into its finished
form.

## HOW

It operates on the [[Entity - Source of Truth]] and produces each
finished [[Entity - Atomic Card]] as it is drafted, validated, graded,
and published.
