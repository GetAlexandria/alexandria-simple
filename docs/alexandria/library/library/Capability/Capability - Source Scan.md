## WHAT

Turn raw source material into a draft empty-library bundle — stub
cards, a keystone index, and open questions.

## WHY

Starting from what the company's real material already contains, instead
of an empty template or an assumed structure, is how the library begins
life as an honest reflection of the company rather than a guess at one —
the founding move behind [[Bet - Library as Living Source of Truth]].

## WHERE

Run once at the start of building a library, before any card carries a
finished body.

## HOW

It operates on the draft [[Entity - Alexandria Product Library]],
producing the stub cards, keystone, and open questions the rest of the
build works from.
