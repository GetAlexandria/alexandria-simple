## WHAT

Co-author the product's onboarding Vision with the colleague, one slot
at a time, then bank it as canonical knowledge.

## WHY

Co-authoring the Vision one slot at a time, then banking it as canonical,
is how the very first material a new director provides becomes real,
current company knowledge rather than an onboarding form nobody reads
again — [[Bet - Library as Living Source of Truth]] starting from slot
one. Reaching a banked, usable Vision quickly is also what gives a new
director a genuine payoff before they have built out the rest of the
system ([[Principle - First Servable Loop]]).

## WHERE

Wherever the director and the colleague work through the onboarding
Vision together, slot by slot.

## HOW

It operates on the [[Entity - Basic Product Description]] and is
related to [[Role - Raven]], the colleague who drafts and mediates it
with the director.
