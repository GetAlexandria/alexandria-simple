## WHAT

The measurement shelf holds the standing quantities the product watches without
end. What a measure is, and how it differs from a test that resolves to a
verdict, is defined in [[Entity - Measure]].

## WHY

Some of what the strategy stakes itself on is not a question that ends but a
level that must be held and watched — value delivered, cost incurred, quality
sustained — and the golden metric together with the measures that feed it is
how that watch is kept defined and shared rather than left to each wager's
own accounting.

## WHERE

Measures read from the product surfaces the AI colleagues do their work
through, and are cited by the experiments that borrow them and the keystones
that steer by them. The apex of the shelf is the product's golden metric,
[[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered|fair-market value delivered]], carried
as its own card. Beneath it sit the per-wager input measures that feed it — one adoption signal
per company wager — with the diagnostics that isolate which wager earned a
gain left to the experiments shelf.

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — no reading has happened yet on this shelf. In the
present the family is defined in full — the golden metric and its three input
measures — but none are instrumented, so nothing reads. What is intended next
is instrumentation against real use, starting with the team's own dogfooding
and then the ten-director pilot.

## HOW

The shelf is one family with an apex. The golden metric sits at the pillar; the
per-wager measures that compose it sit at the aggregate grain and link upward
as its inputs, so the claim that one metric unifies the wagers is structural
rather than merely asserted. A measure carries a target and a trend and nothing
that stages it. A reading that crosses statistical power and says something
notable does not change the measure's nature — it emits a fresh Research card
where a stage and a verdict finally attach. Quality, which no instrument reads
alone, is supplied by human cadence: a standing review that reads value
delivered and a light, frequent pull that surfaces trouble early.

The family's first four members are in place:
[[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered|fair-market value delivered]] is the
apex, and [[Measure - Switching and Consolidation Hours|switching and consolidation hours]],
[[Measure - Adoption and Substitution|adoption and substitution]], and
[[Measure - Needed But Undone Hours|needed but undone hours]] are its inputs,
each linking upward to say which wager to credit when the apex moves, rather
than leaving that claim to prose alone.
