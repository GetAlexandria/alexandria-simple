## WHAT

Counts how much of a team's real day-to-day work moves into living inside Alexandria's
visual, traversible environment and out of whatever it used before — a plain command line,
scattered lists and documents, or a flatter, non-spatial tool doing the same job. The
reading is simple at heart: does a team actually live here, day over day, or does it drift
back to the plainer path once trying it stops being new.

## WHY

The bet this measure watches is the most expensive of the company's three wagers to be
wrong about, and it is also the one where a team's own behavior is the cleanest witness
available: if people keep choosing the map-first, spatial surface when an equally capable
plain list or command line sits right next to it, the wager is earning its cost; if they
quietly drift back to the plainer path, no amount of storytelling about the environment
being more legible will save it. This measure keeps that judgment behavioral rather than a
matter of asking people whether they like it, since stated preference is the least
trustworthy witness in the building.

## WHERE

Reads from how much of a team's real activity happens through the traversible surface
itself — moving through it, building on it — versus falling back to a flatter, non-spatial
path for the same work. It feeds the volume term of
[[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered]] and is the adoption half of the case for
[[Bet - A Visual, Traversible Work Environment]]; the diagnostic half — isolating whether the
visual environment itself, rather than the colleagues living inside it, is what is actually
earning the adoption — is left to a planned test of attribution described but not yet built.

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — no team has been watched long enough yet for a reading either
way. In the present it is defined, not instrumented; the shelf holds the definition and
nothing else. What is intended next is instrumentation once real use begins in earnest, read
continuously rather than as a single before-and-after snapshot, since drift back to a
plainer path can happen slowly, well after an initial adoption looked like a win.

## HOW

Measured as the live share of a team's real work carried inside the traversible surface,
tracked over time rather than as a one-time yes-or-no, so a drift back to the plain path
shows up even after a period that initially looked like adoption. Its limit: a rising reading
confirms people are choosing the environment over the plain alternative, but not why —
whether a colleague living inside it, rather than the environment's own shape, is the real
draw is left to the planned attribution test.
