## WHAT

Counts the hours of real, wanted work that nobody — human or AI colleague — is actually
getting to: tasks a team names as work it wishes were being done, sitting undone for want of
someone to do them, rather than work already claimed and simply moving slowly. It is the
companion reading to the value delivered by the colleagues that already exist, standing in
for the work the roster has not yet been asked, or is not yet able, to pick up.

## WHY

[[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered]] can climb even while a company is quietly falling
short of its own ambitions, if the reason it climbs is that the roster only ever attempts
the easy, already-covered work and nothing harder ever gets a colleague assigned to it. This
measure exists to keep that blind spot visible, since it is exactly what the golden figure
cannot tell on its own: a company whose AI colleagues deliver rising value while a mounting
pile of needed work goes untouched is not the win the headline number alone would suggest.
It stands beside the wager that colleagues should become the primary way the business gets
work done, because that wager is existential rather than subtle — if colleagues are
genuinely earning their place, the backlog of needed-but-undone work should be shrinking as
the roster is asked to carry more of it, not merely holding steady while the easy work gets
automated first.

## WHERE

Reads from the team's own backlog and its own account of what it wishes were happening but
is not — work a director names as wanted, whether or not a colleague yet exists who could
take it on. It does not feed the volume term of [[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered]] the
way the other input measures do; it stands beside it as a guardrail reading, watching for a
version of success where the golden figure rises while real ambition still goes unmet, and it
is the companion measure for [[Bet - Colleagues as the Interaction Layer]].

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — the backlog this measure would read has not yet been named or
counted in one place. In the present it is defined, uninstrumented. What is intended next is
instrumentation as the team's own list of wanted-but-uncovered work becomes something kept in
one place rather than scattered across memory and conversation, so a reading here can be
trusted as a real count rather than a guess.

## HOW

Measured as hours of named, wanted work with no colleague currently carrying it, tallied from
what the team itself says it wishes were being done rather than inferred from what is
missing. Its limit: what counts as wanted is a judgment call that can shrink simply because
ambition shrinks to match capacity, so a falling reading has to be checked against whether
the team's ambitions actually grew rather than assumed to mean the backlog is genuinely
clearing.
