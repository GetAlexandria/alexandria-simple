## WHAT

Counts the hours a team stops spending inside other tools once its work is centralized
inside Alexandria's own substrate instead — a separate retrieval-augmented chat client, a
standalone command-line agent, or a scattered set of point tools a colleague used to need
stitched together by hand. Every hour a team used to spend switching between tools,
re-explaining context one of those tools had already lost, or maintaining a parallel setup
outside the library counts here once it stops being spent, because it no longer needs to
be.

## WHY

The wager this measure watches, that great colleagues are grown from a centralized company
substrate rather than assembled from bolted-together off-the-shelf agents, is easy to assert
and just as easy to shrug off — a team can always say the bolted-together version was fine.
This measure keeps that judgment behavioral rather than rhetorical: it tests whether a team
really does fold its separate tools into Alexandria and stop paying the switching tax, or
whether the old habits keep running quietly alongside the new colleague, which would say the
centralized substrate is not yet earning the cost of building and maintaining it.

## WHERE

Reads from the team's own working pattern — which tools stay open, which get retired, and
how much of what used to be assembled by hand out of separate agents and point tools now
runs through Alexandria's own [[Entity - Ledger]] and [[Entity - Playbook]] instead. It feeds
the volume term of [[Measure - Fair-Market Value Delivered]], and the wager it watches is
[[Bet - Colleagues Grown from Company Design]] — the bet a centralized substrate has to beat
for the cost of building and keeping it alive to be worth carrying.

## WHEN

There is no past yet: N/A — no team has been watched switching away from anything, so there
is no prior reading. In the present it is defined, not instrumented — the frame is set but
no hours have been counted. What is intended next is that the ten-director pilot in the
experiments shelf becomes the first place this measure can actually read something, once
directors who did not build Alexandria bring their own existing tools with them and either
fold them in or do not.

## HOW

Measured as hours per person per week that used to go to a tool or a hand-assembled agent
setup outside Alexandria and now do not, read from what a team reports it has stopped doing
rather than from an automatic count, since a tool going quiet is not proof a team has stopped
reaching for it out of habit. Its limit: a team can retire a tool for reasons that have
nothing to do with Alexandria's substrate — a competitor's tool folding, a subscription
lapsing — so a reading here is suggestive of the centralization wager rather than sufficient
on its own; it is read alongside the pilot's own build-completion and continued-use readings
rather than trusted in isolation.
