## WHAT

One of the four fixed pieces of the Basic Product Description — person,
mechanism, the-work, refusal — drafted by Raven and ruled by the director.
A component with no life outside the description.

## WHY

Splitting the description into four fixed, separately resolvable pieces
is what lets a director reach a first useful loop through just one slot at
a time rather than the whole account at once
([[Principle - First Servable Loop]]). Working through person, mechanism,
the work, and refusal one at a time, each drafted and ruled on its own, is
also how the description avoids showing a new director more than they can
handle up front ([[Principle - Progressive Disclosure]]).

## WHERE

Drafted and updated as the director works through the description.

## HOW

Each slot moves through needs-review (when Raven drafts), approved, or
skipped (when the director rules) standing, and belongs to the
[[Entity - Basic Product Description]] it fills.
