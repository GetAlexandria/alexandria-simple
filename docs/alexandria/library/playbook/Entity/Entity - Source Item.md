## WHAT

Source material the director hands Raven while working the
description — tracked the moment it is attached. Identity-bearing,
but its lifecycle rides the description: tracked at attach, consumed
at banking.

## WHY

Tracking source material the moment it is handed over keeps every claim a
card later makes traceable back to something the director actually provided,
rather than to an unrecorded conversation. Because its lifecycle rides the
description it informs, nothing needs separate cleanup once the description
is banked and the material has done its job. Without that attach-time
record, a card could only assert its claims; with it, a card can point back
to exactly what grounded them.

## WHERE

Recorded when source material is attached to the description.

## HOW

It is attached to and consumed by the [[Entity - Basic Product Description]]
it informs.
