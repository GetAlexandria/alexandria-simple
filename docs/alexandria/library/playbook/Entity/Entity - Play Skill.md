## WHAT

The Raven-facing procedure wrapping a play — how the agent launches, mediates,
and relays it in session. A component of a play: same identity as
the play, a subordinate part with its own card.

## WHY

Its whole reason for existing is the wager that process should live in one
shared playbook every colleague draws on, rather than as scripts each agent
keeps to itself — [[Bet - Shared, Agent-Executable Playbook]]. Keeping its own
card as a subordinate part of the play, rather than dissolving into it, is
what lets the shared playbook stay legible as the thing an agent actually
reads from.

## WHERE

The written procedure that goes with a given play.

## HOW

It is the spoken wrapper of its [[Entity - Play]], read by [[Role - Raven]]
when the director asks for the play in session.
