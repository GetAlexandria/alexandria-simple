## WHAT

The continuous firing and running of plays of every kind — one of the
product's two core recurring patterns. Plays keep starting, keep
running, and keep finishing, over and over, for as long as the company
operates. Each occurrence is human, agentic, and software work together:
a director or a trigger calls a play, an agent and its tools carry it
forward, and the run itself is tracked from start to finish.

## WHY

This pattern is the wager that a colleague takes ownership of work as a
play made continuous — the play is the unit by which work is assigned,
run consistently, and measured, and this is that unit firing over and over
across every colleague, [[Bet - The Play as Unit of Ownership]]. If the
play holds up as the right unit of ownership, this pattern is what proves
it at the scale the company actually operates at.

## WHERE

Wherever a play is called and carried out — across every colleague, on
its own schedule, all the time the product is in use.

## HOW

It relates to the [[Entity - Play]] definitions being called, over and
over, and to each resulting [[Entity - Play Run]] as the work
advances, suspends, resumes, and completes.
