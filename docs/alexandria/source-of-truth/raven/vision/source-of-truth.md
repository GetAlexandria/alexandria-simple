# Raven Product Context

Generated from approved Raven Vision slots.

## Vision

### The Person

The leader of the team — generally a small startup team or a small division (4–16 people), initially in the tech field. They are responsible for the top and bottom line of what they're doing, and they are looking to scale and leverage their use of AI to improve both.

### The Mechanism

You have AI colleagues. They have responsibilities that they handle faithfully, independently. You can assign them things and they do it. They have areas of responsibility that they can handle — it's not just a prompt window that responds to you, it's members of your team that you rely on.

### The Work

You have a federated set of context libraries that communicate the company's plan, the product's plan, the product's state, and your learning to date — a living business plan for humans, and specialty context that often never leaves the boardroom or watercooler, for agents. You have a playbook which organizes "plays" — combinations of agentic, software, and human activities — that you can reliably run. There is a visual interface that directors (our users) have to manage their agents, and agents are functionally a name and personality represented by a "magical" coin — that is the front for this playbook. Plays are usually managed by agents — they're not the agents themselves; plays are kind of like backup singers in that way. Independence is achieved partially by triggers and the ledger — the ledger is immutable and demonstrates what has happened in the company, making history easier to track — and it is the best source for triggers because it is the source of truth for what has happened. Combining the company context, reliable plays, triggers, and the ledger, it all comes together into the capability of building a team of AI employees. We've referred to it as an operating plane, or mission control, for agentic companies / AI-native companies.

### What It's Not

It's not an OS for chaotic or midsized organizations — it requires unity and synchronization, which are often hard to come by in family-run businesses or yolo fundraising schemes. It's not a big pile of skills, and it's not "better prompt engineering." It's a full system, not a boost or upgrade to what you're already doing.
