## WHAT
The human who owns intent and judgment: picks the play, clarifies purpose and
constraints, rules decomposition granularity, and confirms each stage advance.
Every design artifact is agent-drafted from that intent and approved at the gates,
not Director-authored.

## WHERE
README "Division of labor" and "The loop" (the Who column; the two gates are the
Director's).

## HOW
The Director is the decider at the [[Mechanism - Director Gate]], judging the
[[Entity - Brief]] at Gate 1 and the read-out at Gate 2. The [[Role - Hardener]]
and the other agent doers serve this judgment; they never rule.
