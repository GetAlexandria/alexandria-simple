# Research brief — Size the Opportunity (step 0)

Drafted 2026-06-11 by the orchestrator from the Director's step-0 frame
(agreed same day). This is the second play in the ground-before-design
run sequence. Size the Opportunity is a compound input to Write the One-
Pager / PRD — it supplies the why-now and value-vs-alternatives section
of that artifact.

Two mandates, same as the write-the-one-pager pattern:

**A. Ground the output.** What the sizing artifact *is* — its forms,
structures, and what a finished run produces.

**B. Pre-answer the elicitation.** The brief conversation runs on
`../../TEMPLATE-brief.md`'s sections. For each, find the expert answer
before asking the Director, so design time is spent ruling on researched
options, not generating from scratch:
- §1 Goal — what a successful run produces; the done-condition; what
  a *failed* one looks like.
- §2 Trigger — when in expert practice this step fires in a product
  process; what must exist first.
- §3 Required knowledge — the inputs experts say you need; what
  practitioners do when one is missing.
- §4 Golden path — the expert step-by-step method for sizing an
  opportunity.
- §5 What could go wrong — documented failure modes and root causes,
  with severity.
- §7 Proof spec — how experts judge one; rubric; what separates a
  strong sizing from a weak one.

## Segments (one Sonnet researcher each)

Two parallel research reports were commissioned:

1. **Method report** — what opportunity sizing is, the three-layer
   framework (TAM/SAM/SOM), top-down vs bottom-up methods, Cagan's
   Opportunity Assessment, RICE scoring (Intercom), Ulwick's ODI
   Opportunity Algorithm, Torres' Opportunity Solution Tree, Shopify's
   tiered sizing practice, best-practice conventions (declare-don't-block,
   North Star consistency, post-launch calibration).

2. **Quality/failure report** — what makes a sizing fail; the 1%-of-TAM
   trap; top-down-only reliance; revenue vs. transaction-volume conflation;
   the do-nothing alternative; demographic vs. behavioral segmentation;
   single-method anchoring; the JTBD lens on sizing; competitive-saturation
   as a constraint; cost of delay; alternative-sizing consistency.

## Claim discipline

Every claim carries its source (URL + who said it); quotes verbatim where
fetchable; primary sources preferred and fetched where possible; anything
unverifiable is flagged, not dropped or laundered. Raw claims land in
`extracted-claims.md`; a verification pass checks the load-bearing search-
snippet-only claims; confirmed material graduates to `grounding.md`, caveats
inline.
