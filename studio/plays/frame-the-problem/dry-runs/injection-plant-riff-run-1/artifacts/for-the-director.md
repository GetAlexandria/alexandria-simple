### 1. A specific instance of someone missing the deadline — what did it look like?

The thin spot: we know eleven people missed the thirty-day window last month, but we don't have a single concrete story of how it happened. Did someone not know the policy existed? Did someone know but lose track of time? Did someone try to find the deadline and fail?

Evidence bar: one real instance with enough detail to see the mechanism — how the employee encountered (or never encountered) the deadline. He doesn't need the exact date; he needs one real story.
