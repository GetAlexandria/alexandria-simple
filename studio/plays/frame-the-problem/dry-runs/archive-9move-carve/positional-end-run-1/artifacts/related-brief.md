## 1. I don't know which of my accounts are about to renew until I stumble across one by accident

**Progress sought:** Stay ahead of every upcoming renewal in my book so I can run timely outreach — not find out a contract is expiring because I happened to open the record for something else.

**Who:** Account Manager (AM). Clara is the speaking AM; Prakash confirms the pattern extends to other AMs on the team.

**Circumstance:** Managing a full book of business day-to-day in Meridian. The AM is not checking renewals — she is working other accounts, logging activity, doing her normal job. Because Meridian stores the renewal date on the account record but never surfaces it proactively, an approaching renewal is invisible unless the AM manually opens that specific account and notices the date. The pain strikes when the AM finally realizes — by luck, not by workflow — that accounts are about to lapse with no outreach started.

**Evidence:**

1. "Our account managers are flying blind on renewals." — CLARA · `opinion` · conviction high
2. "I went through my book last week and realized I had three accounts up for renewal in the next thirty days that I had no idea about." — CLARA · `specific-past`
3. "I only found out because I happened to open the account record for a completely different reason." — CLARA · `specific-past`
4. "There is nothing in Meridian that tells you "hey, this one's expiring."" — CLARA · `opinion` · conviction high
5. "By accident." — CLARA · `specific-past`
6. "If I hadn't clicked in that day" — CLARA · `specific-past` (the chance visit happened) · "those accounts would have hit the end of their term with zero outreach from us." — CLARA · `hypothetical-future` · conviction high
7. "One of them was sixty thousand ARR." — CLARA · `specific-past`
8. "That's not a tracking failure, that's a product gap." — CLARA · `opinion` · conviction high
9. "Yeah, that's a real thing. I've heard it from a few other AMs too. Not just you." — PRAKASH · `opinion` · conviction moderate (hearsay — Prakash reports what he has heard, not what he has seen)
10. "And it's not just me being disorganized — I have reminders set in my personal calendar because Meridian has nothing." — CLARA · `commitment` (time and effort actually spent building and maintaining a personal workaround)
11. "Half the team is doing the same." — CLARA · `opinion` · conviction moderate (unverified claim about colleagues)
12. "So we're papering over a product hole with personal calendar hacks." — CLARA · `opinion` · conviction high

**What it's not:** A request for a renewal dashboard, an alerting feature, or a calendar integration. Those are candidate solutions. The problem is the gap between the moment a renewal enters the danger window and the moment the AM becomes aware of it — today that gap closes only by accident.

**Where it lands:** Renewal tracking surface (which does not exist today — renewal dates live as a passive field on the Account record with no alert, digest, or list view). Secondarily touches the Book of business view (no way to filter or sort by upcoming renewal) and Scheduling & calendar integration (no native reminder system; AMs are pushing renewal reminders into personal calendars outside Meridian).
