## P-RENEWAL — "I have accounts about to expire and nothing told me"

checks: pass

**Progress sought:** Know which accounts are approaching renewal in time to plan and execute outreach — before the term ends, not after stumbling on the date by chance.

**Who:** Account Manager (AM). Clara speaks from direct, personal experience; Prakash corroborates that other AMs report the same problem.

**Circumstance:** An AM manages a book of many accounts simultaneously. Renewal dates exist as a field on the account record in Meridian, but nothing in the product surfaces approaching renewals proactively — no alert, no filtered view, no digest. The AM discovers an imminent renewal only if she happens to open the account record for an unrelated reason and notices the date. Between that gap — data in the system, no signal to the person — renewals can reach the end of their term with zero outreach.

**What it's not:** A request for a renewal-alert dashboard, a notification system, or a calendar push — those are candidate solutions. The problem is that Meridian stores the renewal date but gives the AM no signal when action is needed. Any design that closes the gap between "renewal date exists in the system" and "the AM knows about it in time to act" addresses the problem; the shape of that signal is open.

**Where it lands:** Renewal tracking (no dedicated surface today — the surface map confirms the renewal date is stored as a field on the account record but there is no dashboard, alert, or list view that surfaces accounts by upcoming renewal date). Adjacent surfaces: Book of business view (where an AM would expect to see approaching renewals across her accounts); Scheduling & calendar integration (one-directional sync cannot push reminders back to the AM's calendar, which is why AMs resort to manual calendar entries).

**Insight:** Clara's workaround — maintaining personal calendar reminders — is itself evidence that the need is persistent and recurring, not a one-time miss. The cost she pays is ongoing: every time a new account enters her book or a renewal date shifts, the external calendar must be manually updated. The product owns the authoritative data but forces the AM to replicate it elsewhere to get any time-awareness from it.
