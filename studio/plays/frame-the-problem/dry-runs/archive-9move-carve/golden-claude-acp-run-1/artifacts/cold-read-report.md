## Restatement

### What's going on?
A team uses a library system that requires desktop "conversion sessions" to ingest source material. Someone named Maya pitched a browser extension to fix two pain points at once. A conversation happened where those two pain points were pulled apart, and a teammate named Dev argued they're actually separate problems. This document records that analysis: two distinct problems, framed independently, with the solution-bundling question left open.

### Who hurts, and when?
1. **Crew members (Maya specifically)** hurt during research browsing. They find useful material across dozens of browser tabs over days, but there's no way to capture it into the library in the moment — the only path is a later conversion session. By the time they sit down to convert, context is lost and material never makes it in.
2. **Directors** hurt at conversion time. They can't tell whether material is already in the library without leaving the conversion flow to manually browse, so they re-add things that are already banked. This is reported secondhand by Maya, not directly by a director.
