# Problem Framing — Account managers have no renewal visibility in Meridian
status: draft (v1)

## The problem(s)

### Renewals sneak up on us because Meridian doesn't surface them

- **Who has it:** Account managers (Clara, and reportedly other AMs on the team), in the circumstance where accounts approach their renewal date — there is no proactive signal from the product, so the AM only discovers an imminent renewal by accident or through personal workarounds.
- **Evidence:**
  - "I went through my book last week and realized I had three accounts up for renewal in the next thirty days that I had no idea about." — `first-hand: a specific past instance` (Clara, last week, three accounts discovered)
  - "I only found out because I happened to open the account record for a completely different reason." — `first-hand: a specific past instance` (Clara, same incident — accidental discovery)
  - "There is nothing in Meridian that tells you 'hey, this one's expiring.'" — `first-hand: a specific past instance` (Clara describing the product's current state, grounded in the same incident)
  - "One of them was sixty thousand ARR." — `first-hand: a specific past instance` (Clara, a specific account with material revenue at risk in the same batch)
  - "If I hadn't clicked in that day those accounts would have hit the end of their term with zero outreach from us." — `hypothetical` (what would have happened had she not stumbled on it — plausible but did not actually occur)
  - "I've heard it from a few other AMs too. Not just you." — `assumed / hand-wavey` (Prakash corroborating the pattern but with no specific instance named)
- **Thin spot:** Prakash says he's heard it from other AMs, but no second specific instance is on the table. We have Clara's one concrete story; the claim that this is team-wide rests on Prakash's general impression so far.

### AMs are papering over the gap with personal calendar hacks

- **Who has it:** The same account managers, in the ongoing circumstance where they know Meridian won't remind them — they maintain a parallel personal reminder system to compensate.
- **Evidence:**
  - "I have reminders set in my personal calendar because Meridian has nothing." — `first-hand: a specific past instance` (Clara describing her own current workaround)
  - "Half the team is doing the same." — `assumed / hand-wavey` (Clara's estimate of how widespread the workaround is — no specific second person named)
- **Thin spot:** Clara says half the team does this, but that's her impression, not a verified count. A second AM confirming "yes, I do the same thing" with a specific example would harden the claim.

## How they relate (a guess)

These are the same underlying problem (Meridian has no renewal surfacing) seen from two angles: the acute failure mode (renewals get missed) and the chronic coping mechanism (personal calendars). The second problem exists *because of* the first — if Meridian surfaced renewals, the calendar hacks would be unnecessary. Held loosely: it's possible some AMs would keep personal reminders even with product support, but the dependency seems straightforward.

## What this means for the solution (so far)

No specific solution has been proposed in the material yet — Clara frames it as a product gap, and the director asked for the problem to be framed, not a solution to be scoped. The evidence so far **supports** the claim that there is a real product gap: Clara's specific instance (three renewals, one at $60K ARR, discovered by accident last week) is concrete and recent. The team-wide claim is plausible but still hand-wavey — it rests on Prakash's general corroboration and Clara's estimate that "half the team" uses the workaround, neither backed by a second specific instance.
