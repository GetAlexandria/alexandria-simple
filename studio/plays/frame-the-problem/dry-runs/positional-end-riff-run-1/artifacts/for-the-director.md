# For the Director — Raven's marching orders

## What to put in front of him

Show him `runtime/problem-framing.md` as-is. Clara's story is concrete and strong — three renewals in thirty days found by accident, one at $60K ARR. That's the kind of instance that earns the problem. Tell him the framing stands on that story and ask him to react: does it capture what he heard, or is there something else underneath it?

## What to draw out

There are two thin spots. For each, the evidence bar and the move:

### 1. The team-wide pattern

**What we have:** Prakash said "I've heard it from a few other AMs too," and Clara said "half the team is doing the same." Neither gives a second specific instance.

**Evidence bar:** One more AM with a specific recent story — not "it happens all the time," but something like "last month I almost missed the Acme renewal because nothing flagged it."

**The move:** "Prakash mentioned he's heard this from other AMs. Can you think of a specific time another AM ran into this recently?" If he gives the hand-wavey version ("oh yeah, it happens a lot"), the follow-up is simply: "Can you tell me about a specific time that happened recently?" He doesn't need the exact date — he needs one real instance.

### 2. The workaround is widespread

**What we have:** Clara says she uses personal calendar reminders and half the team does too. We have her word but no second confirmation.

**Evidence bar:** One other AM confirming they maintain their own reminder system, ideally with a concrete detail (what tool, how they set it up, what falls through).

**The move:** This will likely come out naturally if the first thin spot gets filled — if another AM has a near-miss story, asking "how do you track renewals today?" will surface the workaround. Don't force it as a separate question unless the conversation stalls.
