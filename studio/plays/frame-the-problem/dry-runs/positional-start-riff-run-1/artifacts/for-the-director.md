# For the Director — what Raven puts in front of him and what to draw out

## What to put in front of him
Hand him the problem framing as-is. Clara's instance is sharp — three renewals nearly missed, one at $60K, discovered by accident last week. That's real. The framing separates two problems: the missing visibility and the workaround culture it created. Let him react to the shape.

## What to draw out

### Thin spot 1: Prakash's corroboration is hand-wavey
Prakash said "I've heard it from a few other AMs too. Not just you." That's secondhand and unspecified — no names, no incidents.

**Evidence bar:** One other AM describing a specific time they nearly missed or did miss a renewal because Meridian didn't surface it.

**The move:** "Prakash, you mentioned other AMs have hit this too — can you think of a specific time one of them told you about a renewal they almost missed?"

If he gives a hand-wavey answer ("yeah it comes up a lot"), the follow-up is: "Can you tell me about a specific time that happened recently?"

### Thin spot 2: "Half the team" running personal calendar hacks
Clara says half the team keeps personal calendar reminders. That's plausible but unverified.

**Evidence bar:** One other AM confirming they maintain personal reminders for renewals because Meridian doesn't track them.

**The move:** "Clara mentioned half the team runs personal calendar reminders for renewals — does anyone else here do that, or know someone who does?" If someone confirms, ask: "Can you think of a specific time you had to rely on that reminder instead of finding it in Meridian?"
