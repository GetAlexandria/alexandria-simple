# Problem Framing — Account managers have no renewal visibility in Meridian
status: draft (v1)

## The problem(s)

### AMs discover upcoming renewals by accident, not by design
- Account managers using Meridian have no system-driven signal that a renewal is approaching. They find out only if they happen to open the account record for another reason.
- evidence:
  - "I went through my book last week and realized I had three accounts up for renewal in the next thirty days that I had no idea about." — Clara. first-hand: a specific past instance (last week, her own book review)
  - "I only found out because I happened to open the account record for a completely different reason." — Clara. first-hand: a specific past instance (the same incident — discovery was accidental)
  - "One of them was sixty thousand ARR." — Clara. first-hand: a specific past instance (one of the three accounts discovered last week)
  - "If I hadn't clicked in that day those accounts would have hit the end of their term with zero outreach from us." — Clara. hypothetical (what would have happened had she not stumbled on it)
- thin spot: Clara gives one specific instance (last week, three accounts). Prakash says "I've heard it from a few other AMs too" but names no one and gives no instance — that corroboration is hand-wavey until a second AM gives their own specific past time it happened.

### AMs are papering over the product gap with personal calendar hacks
- Because Meridian surfaces no renewal signal, AMs maintain their own reminders outside the system. This is a workaround, not a workflow — it depends on each person's diligence and is invisible to the organization.
- evidence:
  - "I have reminders set in my personal calendar because Meridian has nothing." — Clara. first-hand: a specific past instance (her current, ongoing practice)
  - "Half the team is doing the same." — Clara. assumed / hand-wavey (she asserts half the team does this but gives no names or specifics)
- thin spot: "Half the team is doing the same" is Clara's assertion. It needs at least one other AM confirming they also run personal calendar reminders — ideally with a specific time they had to rely on that workaround.

## How they relate (a guess)
One drives the other — the missing renewal visibility (problem 1) forces the personal-calendar workaround (problem 2). If Meridian surfaced renewals, the workaround would disappear. Held loosely: it's possible some AMs would still keep personal reminders even with a product solution, but the forcing function is clearly the product gap.

## What this means for the solution (so far)
Supports — the material points toward a genuine product gap in Meridian (no renewal surfacing), and Clara's first-hand instance is concrete enough to take seriously: three accounts, one at $60K ARR, discovered by accident last week. The solution space (some form of renewal alerting in Meridian) is not yet specified, and the framing does not prescribe one. The main risk to the framing is that the problem might be narrower than it appears — Clara is one AM with one incident, and Prakash's corroboration ("a few other AMs") is hand-wavey. If it turns out to be Clara-specific, the urgency changes.
