# For the Director — Raven's marching orders

## What to put in front of him

Lead with the draft framing. Clara's instance is strong — three renewals nearly
missed, one at $60K ARR, discovered by accident last week. That's the anchor.
The framing has two problems separated out: the missing renewal visibility and
the personal-calendar workaround it spawned. Show him both.

## What to draw out

There are two thin spots. Each needs one specific past instance to cross the
evidence bar.

### 1. Other AMs experiencing the same blind spot

Prakash said "I've heard it from a few other AMs too" — that's hand-wavey. We
need one real instance from a second person.

**The move:** "Prakash mentioned other AMs have hit this too. Can you tell me
about a specific time one of them missed or nearly missed a renewal because
Meridian didn't surface it?"

Good evidence looks like: "Two weeks ago, Jordan realized the Acme contract was
lapsing in ten days and had to scramble." Hand-wavey sounds like: "It happens to
everyone" or "The whole team complains about it." If he gives the hand-wavey
version, follow up: "Can you tell me about a specific time that happened
recently — even roughly when and who?"

### 2. The team-wide calendar-hack workaround

Clara said "half the team" is using personal calendars. That's plausible but
unearned — we only have Clara's own practice confirmed.

**The move:** "Clara mentioned she uses personal calendar reminders as a
workaround, and that others do too. Do you know of a specific AM besides Clara
who's doing the same thing?"

Good evidence: a name and a description of their workaround. Hand-wavey: "Yeah,
everyone does it." Same follow-up if needed: "Who specifically — and what does
their workaround look like?"

## Priority

The first thin spot matters more. If the problem is team-wide, it's a product
gap worth investing in. If it's concentrated in one workflow, the right fix might
be smaller. The calendar-hack question is secondary — it strengthens the case but
doesn't change the framing on its own.
