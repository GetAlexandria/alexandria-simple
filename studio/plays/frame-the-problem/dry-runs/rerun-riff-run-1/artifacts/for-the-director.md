# For the Director

## What to put in front of him

The draft framing (runtime/problem-framing.md). Lead with: "Maya's extension
pitch has two real problems underneath it — losing sources in tabs before
conversion, and directors re-adding material they can't tell is already banked.
Here's a first pass at separating them."

Let him react to the structure before going to thin spots.

## What to draw out

Two thin spots need one specific instance each:

### 1. Tab-loss: what did it actually cost?

The evidence bar: Dev has watched Maya lose sources in tabs, but we don't have a
specific time where that loss had a downstream consequence — a conversion session
that came out thin, a library gap someone later hit, a decision made without
material that was found-and-lost.

The move: "You and Dev both say sources die in tabs. Can you tell me about a
specific time that happened and it actually cost you something — a session that
came out worse, a gap you hit later?"

She doesn't need the exact date. She needs one real instance where the loss
mattered, not just the loss itself.

### 2. Duplicate banking: is it Priya only, or wider?

The evidence bar: Jules's Priya/Tuesday instance is solid and carries real
downstream harm (lying coverage bar, nearly skipped session). Maya's claim that
"every director has this problem" is hand-wavey — she's generalizing from a
secondhand read.

The move: "Jules gave us Priya's instance — that one's concrete. Maya, when you
say every director has this problem, can you point to a specific director besides
Priya who told you they'd re-added something?"

If she can, the problem is wider than one director and the priority call
changes. If she can't, we have a real but possibly narrow problem — still worth
solving for Priya's case alone (a lying coverage bar is serious), but the scope
is different.
