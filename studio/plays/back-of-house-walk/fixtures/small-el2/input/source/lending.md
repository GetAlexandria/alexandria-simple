# The Lending Desk

A **borrower** checks out a packet at the lending desk, which opens a **seed
loan** recording who took what and when. The loan stays open until the borrower
returns a share of the harvested seeds.

Returning closes the loan and adds the returned seeds back to the catalog as a
new packet.

The return window is written down twice and the two notes disagree: the desk
sign says seeds are due back "by the end of the growing season," while the
borrower slip says "within one year." Nobody has said which one is binding.
