## WHAT

The Seed Library lets neighbours borrow seeds, grow them, and return a share of
the new seeds at harvest.

## WHERE

It runs in two parts, each its own bounded area: the [[catalog]] of what seeds
are on hand, and the [[lending]] desk where borrowing and returning happen.

## HOW

The whole system tracks one thing — a seed packet — from the moment it is
catalogued to the moment its seeds come back. The [[catalog]] holds the packets
and what is known about each variety; the [[lending]] desk opens and closes the
loans that move a packet out and bring new seed back.
