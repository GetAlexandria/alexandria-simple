# Read Coherence — Seed Library

The scan's honest self-assessment: what a stranger would and wouldn't understand
from this bundle.

## What a stranger would understand

The Seed Library tracks one thing — a seed packet — from cataloguing through
lending and back. Two parts do the work: the catalog holds packets and what is
known about each variety; the lending desk opens and closes the loans that move a
packet out and bring new seed back. The keystone story and the two contexts read
in order.

## What a stranger would not understand

- **Why a share, and how much?** The source says a borrower returns "a share" of
  the harvest but never says what share or how it is decided.
- **What happens when seeds are not returned.** The packet can be "recorded as
  not returned," but no consequence, follow-up, or write-off is described.
- **Who runs the library day to day.** Only the librarian and borrower roles
  appear; whether anyone else is involved is unstated.

## Three reservations

1. The type vocabulary used is the default profile — the library ships no card
   taxonomy of its own, so the director should ratify or replace it.
2. There is no strategy or market material in the source, so the "why" of the
   library is thin.
3. The germination-rate tracking is specified but never realized; the cards
   describe the intent, not a working feature.

## Hot Spots that are likely real product flaws

- The two conflicting return windows are a genuine product contradiction, not a
  scan artifact — the source really does say both.
