## WHAT

The borrower takes seeds out to grow and promises a share of the harvest back.

## WHERE

At the lending desk, at checkout and again at return.

## HOW

The borrower opens an [[Entity - Seed Loan]] at checkout and closes it by
returning a share of the new seeds at harvest.
