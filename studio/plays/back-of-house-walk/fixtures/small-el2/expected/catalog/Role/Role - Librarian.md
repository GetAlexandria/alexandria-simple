## WHAT

The librarian keeps the catalog: they take in donated seeds and make each packet
ready to lend.

## WHERE

At the catalog, whenever a new donation arrives.

## HOW

The librarian catalogues each [[Entity - Seed Packet]] — giving it a shelf code,
recording its variety, and marking it available so a borrower can find it.
