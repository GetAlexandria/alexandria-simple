## WHAT

A seed packet is the one thing the library tracks from end to end: it arrives as
a donation, is catalogued, checked out, and either returned at harvest or
recorded as not returned.

## WHERE

It lives on the shelves of the catalog and, while checked out, on the borrower's
side of the lending desk.

## HOW

When a donation arrives, the [[Role - Librarian]] gives the packet a shelf code
and marks it available. Each packet follows the [[Reference - Germination
Standard]] recorded for its variety. When its last seeds are checked out, it is
marked empty and drops off the available shelf.
