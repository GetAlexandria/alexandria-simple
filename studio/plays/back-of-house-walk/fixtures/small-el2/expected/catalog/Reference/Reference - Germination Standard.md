## WHAT

The germination standard is the share of seeds a variety is expected to sprout,
copied from the grower's note.

## WHERE

Recorded against each variety in the catalog.

## HOW

Every [[Entity - Seed Packet]] carries the standard for its variety. The catalog
is meant to track the rate each variety actually achieves once borrowers report
back, but no report has ever been recorded, so that number stays blank.
