# Saddle slice: product surface map — Lantern (fixture, synthetic)

Conceptual map of what exists today. Lantern is a knowledge-library product:
directors convert scattered source material into an approved, banked card
library their team and agents draw on. (Fictional stand-in product for
dry-runs; matches the world of meeting-snippet-01.)

## Surfaces

- **Library** — the banked card graph. Browsable by area; coverage bars show
  how filled each area is. The product's center of gravity.
- **Source Conversion** — the flow that turns raw source material into banked
  cards: intake (gather sources) → draft → director approval → atomize → bank.
  Intake accepts uploads and pasted links/text in a desktop session.
- **Roadmap board** — a filled area of the library rendered as a working
  board; updated as decisions land.
- **Search & duplicate awareness** — minimal today: browse-only navigation;
  no global search, no "already banked" signal anywhere outside the Library
  view itself.
- **Director dashboard** — conversion progress, pending approvals, coverage
  at a glance.

## Known seams

- Capture happens only inside a desktop intake session; nothing exists for
  capture at the moment of encounter (reading, browsing, meetings).
- Duplicate detection runs only at atomize time, not at intake — and it is
  area-local: it compares within the area being atomized, not across areas, so
  the same source can be banked separately in two different areas without notice.
- No freshness signal: a banked card gives no indication of how current it is.
  A card from two years ago looks identical to one banked yesterday; nothing
  flags a card as stale.
- Coverage bars count the cards present in an area; they do not weigh freshness
  or de-duplicate, so stale or duplicated cards still register as full coverage.
- Conversion know-how is undocumented: how to run a conversion — especially the
  atomize step — lives in veteran crew members' heads, with no guide or
  onboarding surface.
