# Saddle slice: who the users are — Lantern (fixture, synthetic)

## Director
The customer. Runs source conversions, approves drafts, banks cards; owns the
library's truth. Time-poor; touches Lantern in concentrated sessions a few
times a week, not continuously. Pain tolerance for process is low — they do
conversions because they want the *outcome* (a trustworthy library), not the
activity.

## Crew member
Internal team-side user (researchers, curators — Maya is one). Gathers and
stages source material for conversion sessions, often across many browser
tabs and documents over days. Works continuously in Lantern during the week.
Frequently fields director questions about what's already in the library.
