## The picture

Crew members lose source material in browser tabs between the moment of discovery and a conversion session, so good sources never make it into the library. Directors also re-add content that's already banked because there's no signal at intake time that it exists. These are distinct problems — one is about capturing at the right moment, the other about visibility across what's already there — and whether they share a root is still open.
