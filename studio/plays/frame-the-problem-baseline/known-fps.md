# Known false positives — Frame the Problem

Patterns a fresh-eyes checker or grader reliably flags that are dispositioned
by design. Consume this BEFORE reporting (README, field-review rules: each
play keeps this ledger). Entries name exact patterns with provenance — the
ledger never excuses a novel instance. When in doubt, flag it and cite the
nearest entry so the disposition can be checked rather than assumed.

| # | Pattern a fresh checker/grader flags | Why it is not a defect | Provenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The cold-reader comprehension check (brief §9) has no in-prompt counterpart | External gate by design — a separate agent receives the emitted brief; an in-prompt simulation would be doer-dishonest (the F7 class) | Lint 3, A1 disposition |
| 2 | Frontmatter slug `surface-map` vs prose "surface map" | Accepted convention: frontmatter keys are slugs; prose uses the same name with spaces | Lint 3, D4 disposition |
| 3 | The 75-word ceiling stated in step 6 and echoed at step 7 and the output-format label | Step 6 is the rule's home; the echoes are points of use, not duplicate homes | Lint 4, D borderline (accepted) |
| 4 | Sizing words ("quick," "small," "tiny") inside evidence quotes despite the hard limit | The ban binds Raven's own words only; the room's sizing words are legal inside verbatim quotes — and only there | Brief §10.5 |
| 5 | A vivid near-miss graded `specific-past` instead of `commitment` | Correct — nothing was paid; a near-miss stays `specific-past` however vivid. (The inverse — a near-miss graded `commitment` — IS a defect; see carve-outs) | Brief §10 round-2 counter-cue; prompt gallery |
| 6 | "Hunch: none earned," an empty brief, or an Unclear section read as missing content | All designed, valid outcomes — "none earned" is frequent and correct; the empty brief is a complete run | Brief §1; brief §10 |

| 8 | The step 5 sizing-lexicon scan list (9 words) flagged as non-exhaustive | Known and accepted — validated by the confirmation runs, backstopped by the grader's checklist | Lint 4, C note |

**Standing carve-outs — never excluded by this ledger:** silent drops of any
kind; invented content; a sizing or sequencing word in Raven's own text; and
the two open residuals carried on the grader's checklist (the hunch claiming
a disputed cause; commitment-inflation on vivid-pain quotes) — those are
always reported, every run.
