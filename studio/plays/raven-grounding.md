# Raven — Grounding

*The footprint of what Raven is built from. This is the role the studio
writes plays for: a Technical Product person, modelled as a
build-an-employee sheet so every play has a seat — a tier and a job — before
it is ever authored.*

## The three tiers

Raven is one role read at three altitudes. The same eight product jobs run
through all three; what changes is where the weight sits.

- **Technical Coordinator** — *inputs; runs the machine.* Heaviest on
  delivery, communication logistics, and operations. Keeps the product
  machine turning and barely touches the build decisions. The technical
  layer here is literacy and upkeep, not architecture calls.
- **Technical PM** — *owns a feature end-to-end.* From a validated problem,
  through a buildable spec, to a shipped, measured result. This is the
  load-bearing tier — most of the golden path lives here.
- **Technical Sr. Manager** — *owns strategy across features.* The bets, the
  roadmap, the narrative to leadership, the architecture standards that hold
  across features.

Reading the tiers as a ladder is deliberate: a tier node is a preview of
future-you, not a label. Flip between them to see what the same job looks
like one rung up.

## Base product core + technical overlay

The model is **one shared product core plus a technical overlay**. The eight
jobs — Insight, Strategy, Definition, Delivery, Launch, Analytics,
Communication, Operations — are the product core every PM has, drawn as
dashed-gold *slot* cards. Technical Product is that base book **plus** the
teal overlay cards the "Technical" lens adds.

Nothing is removed by the overlay; it *concentrates*. It is densest in
Insight, Definition, and Delivery — where feasibility, the existing system,
and the build plan live — and nearly absent in Launch and Communication.
(A human trades breadth for that depth; an agent can stack every overlay at
once.)

Most cards are empty slots — named and placed, not yet authored. The
golden-path chain is being built first; the cards that carry a live status
badge are the ones already on the registry, and they open straight to their
workshop.

## Reconciliation — two plays added from the golden path

The original build-an-employee sheet and the golden-path registry were
drawn separately, and the audit found two plays the registry carries that
the sheet never had a card for. Rather than leave the model out of step
with the chain the factory actually runs, both were placed here:

- **Elicit Business Context** → *Customer & Market Insight*, **PM** tier.
  The PM's active constraint-surfacing work — drawing out the business goal,
  the appetite, and the stakeholders behind an ask — belongs in Insight at
  the tier that owns it.
- **Riskiest-Assumption Test** → *Strategy & Prioritization*, **Sr.
  Manager** tier. Isolating the assumption that would sink a bet, and
  testing it cheaply, sits with the tier that owns the bets.

Both are base-product plays (no technical overlay). They reconcile the
sheet to the golden path so the home tab and the registry describe the same
role. Filed for later: a second lens by company stage
(live-prototype → building → scaling).
