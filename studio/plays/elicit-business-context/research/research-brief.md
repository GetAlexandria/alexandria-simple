## Two mandates

**A. Ground the output.** What a business-context elicitation artifact *is* —
its forms, its required contents, what separates a strong capture from a
weak one, and how practitioners judge quality.

**B. Pre-answer the elicitation.** The brief conversation runs on
`../../TEMPLATE-brief.md`'s sections. For each, find the expert answer
before asking the Director, so design time is spent ruling on researched
options, not generating from scratch:
- §1 Goal — what a successful run of this play produces; done-condition;
  what a failed run looks like.
- §2 Trigger — when this play fires in a product process and what must
  exist beforehand.
- §3 Required knowledge — what inputs are needed and what happens when one
  is missing.
- §4 Golden path — the expert step-by-step method for running stakeholder
  elicitation for business context.
- §5 What could go wrong — documented failure modes and root causes, with
  severity.
- §7 Proof spec — how practitioners judge a captured business context
  artifact: checklists, rubrics, eyeball tests.

## Segments (two Sonnet researchers)

1. **Method** — the expert process for eliciting business context from
   stakeholders: BABOK elicitation techniques, Cagan's stakeholder
   constraint interviews, Shape Up appetite-setting, Amazon Working
   Backwards internal FAQ, Teresa Torres continuous discovery engagement,
   reversed Opportunity Solution Tree technique, laddering/5-Whys.
   Golden-path synthesis. Prerequisites. Block vs. proceed rule.

2. **Quality and failure** — what goes wrong when business context is
   gathered badly or not at all: HiPPO effect, authority-substitutes-for-
   evidence, strategy-as-slogan (Rumelt), appetite deferred until after
   scope, stakeholder input ingested as requirements, one-and-done capture.
   Rubric: 10 eyeball checks a non-developer Director can run. Root causes
   with named counter-practices.

## Claim discipline

Every claim carries its source (URL + who said it); quotes verbatim;
primary sources preferred and fetched where possible; anything
unverifiable is flagged, not laundered. Raw claims land in
`extracted-claims.md`; a verification pass checks load-bearing
search-snippet-only claims; confirmed material graduates to
`grounding.md`, caveats inline.
