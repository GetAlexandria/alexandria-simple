# Research brief — Market & Competitor Scan (step 0)

Drafted 2026-06-11 by the orchestrator. Research executed same day. This play
supplies the **competitive-alternatives input** of Write the One-Pager / PRD
(that play's `grounding.md §8 §3` lists "competitive alternatives" as a
required knowledge input). The two research segments were:

- **Method report** — the best-practice methods for running a competitive /
  market scan: how to scope it, gather data, analyze gaps, and route findings
  to decisions.
- **Quality/failure report** — failure modes, root causes of bad CI, the
  feature-parity trap, confirmation bias, and what makes a scan artifact
  judged "good" versus "theater."

Both mandates mirror the Write the One-Pager brief:

**A. Ground the output.** What this artifact *is* — its forms, the analytical
moves that produce it, and what distinguishes a trustworthy scan from a
collection exercise.

**B. Pre-answer the elicitation.** The brief template's sections map to
expert answers found in research, so the Director rules on options rather
than generating from scratch:

- §1 Goal — what a successful Market & Competitor Scan produces; done-
  condition; what a failed run looks like.
- §2 Trigger — when this scan fires (continuous cadence vs. event-triggered);
  what must exist before it starts.
- §3 Required knowledge — inputs practitioners say you need; what to do when
  one is missing.
- §4 Golden path — the expert step-by-step method (11 moves synthesized
  across sources).
- §5 What could go wrong — the five root causes of CI failure, with severity
  and counter-practice.
- §7 Proof spec — the 8-check eyeball rubric for judging a scan artifact.
