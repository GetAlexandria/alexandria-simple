Background

The playbook was built by researching the actual responsibilities of technical product professionals across three career tiers. The goal was to answer: what do people at each level actually do, and where does "technical" change the job?

The eight job categories are drawn from the product management literature and practice — they represent the durable, universal jobs that every PM owns regardless of company, stage, or domain. The weight assignments (Heavy / Owns / Shares / Supports / Light) capture how much of a tier's time and decision authority goes to each job.
Key finding: "Technical" is not a separate job family — it is an additive overlay on top of a shared product core. The overlay concentrates in three jobs (Insight, Definition, Delivery) and is nearly absent in two (Launch, Communication). This means every technical PM is first a PM, and the technical layer is the differential — the part that changes when you hire specifically for technical depth.
